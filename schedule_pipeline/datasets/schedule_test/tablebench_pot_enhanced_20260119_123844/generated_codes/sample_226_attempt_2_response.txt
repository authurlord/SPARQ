Step 1: The question asks whether live births or deaths are the primary driver of natural change per year. Natural change is calculated as live births minus deaths. To determine which factor is more influential, we can compare the magnitude of live births and deaths across all periods.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Convert 'live births per year' and 'deaths per year' from strings with spaces to integers for numerical analysis.

Step 4: Compute the absolute difference between live births and deaths for each period to assess the contribution of each to natural change.

Step 5: Compare the total contributions of live births and deaths over time by summing their values across all periods.

Step 6: The larger sum indicates the primary driver.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean and convert columns to numeric
df['live births per year'] = df['live births per year'].str.replace(' ', '').astype(int)
df['deaths per year'] = df['deaths per year'].str.replace(' ', '').astype(int)

# Calculate total live births and total deaths over all periods
total_births = df['live births per year'].sum()
total_deaths = df['deaths per year'].sum()

# Determine which is the primary driver
if total_births > total_deaths:
    primary_driver = "live births per year"
else:
    primary_driver = "deaths per year"

print(f"Final Answer: {primary_driver}")
```
