Step 1: The user wants to predict the likely average finish position and total winnings for the next racing season based on historical data from 1990 to 2002.  
Step 2: Since the driver's last active year is 2002 (with 5 starts), we assume the next season will also have around 5 races.  
Step 3: To estimate the likely average finish, we calculate the mean of the 'avg finish' column across all years.  
Step 4: To estimate total winnings, we calculate the mean of the 'winnings' column across all years.  
Step 5: Use these averages as predictions for the next season.  
Step 6: Print the results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate average finish and total winnings across all years
avg_finish = df['avg finish'].mean()
total_winnings = df['winnings'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_finish:.1f}, {total_winnings:.0f}")
```
