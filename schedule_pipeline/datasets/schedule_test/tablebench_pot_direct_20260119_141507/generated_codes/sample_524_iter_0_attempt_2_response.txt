Step 1: Understand the question — we are to calculate the change in total seating capacity of the airline's fleet from 2004 to 2008.  
Step 2: Identify relevant data — the "seating" column gives the seating capacity of each aircraft type. The "introduced" column tells us when an aircraft type was introduced.  
Step 3: Focus on aircraft types introduced before or during 2004 and those introduced after or during 2008.  
- Before 2004: Only "boeing 737 - 204" (introduced 1995) and "boeing 737 - 300" (introduced 1996) are before 2004.  
- After 2008: "airbus a321 - 200" (introduced 2008), "airbus a320 - 200" (introduced 2008), and "boeing 737 - 700" (introduced 2000, but retired in 2011 — not relevant for 2008).  
But the question asks for the change *from 2004 to 2008*. So we need to consider only aircraft that were in service during this period.  
- In 2004: Aircraft in service: "airbus a319 - 100" (introduced 2004), "airbus a320 - 200" (2008), "boeing 737 - 300" (1996–2007), "boeing 737 - 204" (1995–1996).  
- In 2008: New aircraft introduced: "airbus a320 - 200", "airbus a321 - 200", "boeing 737 - 700".  
So, from 2004 to 2008, the fleet changes by adding new aircraft and retiring older ones.  
We need to compute the total seating capacity at the start (2004) and end (2008), then find the difference.  

However, note:  
- "boeing 737 - 204" retired in 1996 → not in service in 2004.  
- "boeing 737 - 300" retired in 2007 → still in service in 2004, but not in 2008.  
- "airbus a319 - 100" introduced in 2004 → in service from 2004 onward.  
- "airbus a320 - 200" introduced in 2008 → starts in 2008.  
- "airbus a321 - 200" introduced in 2008 → starts in 2008.  
- "boeing 737 - 700" introduced in 2000 → still in service in 2008.  

So, in 2004:  
- airbus a319 - 100: 156  
- boeing 737 - 300: 148  
Total = 156 + 148 = 304  

In 2008:  
- airbus a320 - 200: 180  
- airbus a321 - 200: 220  
- boeing 737 - 700: 149  
Total = 180 + 220 + 149 = 549  

Change = 549 - 304 = 245  

Thus, the total seating capacity increased by 245 seats.

import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter aircraft introduced before or in 2004 (in service in 2004)
# Exclude retired ones before 2004

# airbus a319 - 100: introduced 2004 → in 2004

# 2004 capacity:

for _, row in df.iterrows():
    if row['introduced'] <= '2004' and row['retired'] == '-' or row['retired'] > '2004':
        capacity_2004 += int(row['seating'])

# 2008 capacity:

# Apply filtering
def is_active_in_year(year):
    return (pd.to_numeric(df['introduced']) <= year) and (df['retired'] == '-' or pd.to_numeric(df['retired']) > year)

# But since years are strings, we do manual string comparison
capacity_2004 = 0

    # 2004: introduced <= 2004 and retired > 2004 or retired not specified

    # 2008: introduced <= 2008 and retired > 2008 or retired not specified
    if introduced <= '2008' and (retired == '-' or retired > '2008'):
