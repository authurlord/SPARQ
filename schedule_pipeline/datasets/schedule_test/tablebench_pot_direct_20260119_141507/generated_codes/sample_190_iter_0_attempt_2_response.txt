Step 1: The question asks for the top 2 factors (from 'innings', 'runs scored', 'balls faced') that significantly influence 'average' and 'sr' (strike rate).  
Step 2: Since 'average' and 'sr' are derived from 'runs scored' and 'balls faced', we can analyze the correlation between these variables.  
Step 3: We will compute the correlation between each of the three factors and both 'average' and 'sr'.  
Step 4: Identify the two factors with the highest absolute correlation to either 'average' or 'sr' — these are the most influential.  

Note: Since 'average' = runs / balls, and 'sr' = runs / balls * 100, both are directly related to 'runs scored' and 'balls faced'.  
We will calculate pairwise correlations and pick the top 2 factors.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Select numerical columns for correlation
numeric_columns = ['innings', 'runs scored', 'balls faced', 'average', 'sr']
df_numeric = df[numeric_columns].dropna()

# Compute correlation matrix
correlation_matrix = df_numeric.corr()

# Extract correlations between factors and 'average', 'sr'
correlations_with_average = correlation_matrix['average'].abs().sort_values(ascending=False)
correlations_with_sr = correlation_matrix['sr'].abs().sort_values(ascending=False)

# Combine and find top 2 factors (excluding 'average' and 'sr' themselves)
all_correlations = correlations_with_average.drop('average').combine_first(correlations_with_sr.drop('sr'))
top_2_factors = all_correlations.sort_values(ascending=False).head(2).index.tolist()

print(f"Final Answer: {top_2_factors[0]}, {top_2_factors[1]}")
```
