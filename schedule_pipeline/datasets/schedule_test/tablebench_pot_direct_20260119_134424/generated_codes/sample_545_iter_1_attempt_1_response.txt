Step 1: The user wants the average total value per year for years between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Convert the 'year' column to integer type to allow numerical comparison.  
Step 4: Filter rows where the year is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).  
Step 5: Convert the 'total' column to float for accurate arithmetic.  
Step 6: Calculate the mean of the filtered 'total' values.  
Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'year' column to integer
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'], errors='coerce')
# Convert 'total' column to float
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'], errors='coerce')
# Filter data for years between 2005 and 2009 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2005) & (df['year'] <= 2009)]
# Calculate average total
average_total = filtered_df['total'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_total:.2f}")
```
