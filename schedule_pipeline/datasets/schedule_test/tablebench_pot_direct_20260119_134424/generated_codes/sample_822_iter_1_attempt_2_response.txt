Step 1: The user wants to calculate the average and standard deviation of 'us viewers (millions)' across all episodes.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'us viewers (millions)' column to numeric, handling any potential formatting issues.
Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation.
Step 5: Print both values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'us viewers (millions)' to numeric, ensuring proper parsing
df['us viewers (millions)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['us viewers (millions)'], errors='coerce')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation
mean_viewers = df['us viewers (millions)'].mean()
std_dev_viewers = df['us viewers (millions)'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_viewers:.2f}, {std_dev_viewers:.2f}")
```
