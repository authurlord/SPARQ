Step 1: The user wants the revenue growth rate from 2001 to 2002.  
Step 2: Revenue growth rate is calculated as:  
    ((Revenue in 2002 - Revenue in 2001) / Revenue in 2001) * 100  
Step 3: Extract total revenue for 2001 and 2002 from the table.  
Step 4: Apply the formula and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract total revenue for 2001 and 2002
revenue_2001 = df.loc[df['year'] == '2001', 'total revenue'].values[0]
revenue_2002 = df.loc[df['year'] == '2002', 'total revenue'].values[0]

# Calculate revenue growth rate
growth_rate = ((revenue_2002 - revenue_2001) / revenue_2001) * 100
print(f"Final Answer: {growth_rate:.2f}")
```
