Step 1: Identify the regions with area greater than 20,000 square kilometers.  
Step 2: Filter the dataframe to include only rows where 'area (km square)' > 20000.  
Step 3: Sum the 'population' column for these filtered rows.  
Step 4: Print the total population.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter regions with area > 20000 km² and sum their population
filtered_population = df[df['area (km square)'] > 20000]['population'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {filtered_population}")
```
