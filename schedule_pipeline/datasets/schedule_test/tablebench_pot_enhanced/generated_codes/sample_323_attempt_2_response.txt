Step 1: Identify the row where the athlete achieved a personal best (PB) time. The "Notes" column contains the entry "2:12:19 PB", which indicates a personal best.
Step 2: Extract the corresponding "Year" and "Venue" for that row.
Step 3: Print the year and venue as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row where Notes contain 'PB'
pb_row = df[df['Notes'] == '2:12:19 PB']
# Extract the Year and Venue
year = pb_row['Year'].values[0]
venue = pb_row['Venue'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}, {venue}")
```
