Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between "Length [km]" and "Drainage basin area [km2]".  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the two columns of interest: 'Length [km]' and 'Drainage basin area [km2]'.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.  
Step 5: Print the result.

Note: The correlation coefficient ranges from -1 to 1, where values close to 1 indicate a strong positive relationship.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'Length [km]' and 'Drainage basin area [km2]'
correlation = df['Length [km]'].corr(df['Drainage basin area [km2]'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
