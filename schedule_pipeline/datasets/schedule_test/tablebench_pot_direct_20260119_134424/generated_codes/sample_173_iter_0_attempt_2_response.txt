Step 1: The question asks about the impact of an increase in eccentricity on periselene and aposelene distances. From orbital mechanics, we know that:
- Periselene (closest approach to the Moon) is given by: $ r_p = a(1 - e) $
- Aposelene (farthest distance from the Moon) is given by: $ r_a = a(1 + e) $
- Where $ a $ is the semi-major axis and $ e $ is the eccentricity.
- As eccentricity ($ e $) increases, periselene decreases and aposelene increases.

Step 2: We will analyze the provided data to observe trends:
- Extract the 'eccentricity', 'periselene (km)', and 'aposelene (km)' columns.
- Check if higher eccentricity correlates with lower periselene and higher aposelene.

Step 3: Use Python to verify this trend by sorting the data by eccentricity and observing the changes in periselene and aposelene.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert relevant columns to float for numerical analysis
df['eccentricity'] = pd.to_numeric(df['eccentricity'])
df['periselene (km)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['periselene (km)'])
df['aposelene (km)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['aposelene (km)'])

# Sort by eccentricity to observe the trend
df_sorted = df.sort_values(by='eccentricity', ascending=False)

# Display the sorted data to analyze the trend
print("Sorted by eccentricity (descending):")
print(df_sorted[['epoch (utc)', 'eccentricity', 'periselene (km)', 'aposelene (km)']])

# Final answer based on the trend observed
print("Final Answer: periselene decreases, aposelene increases")
```
