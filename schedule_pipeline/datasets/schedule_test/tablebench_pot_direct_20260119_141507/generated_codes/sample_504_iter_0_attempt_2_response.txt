Step 1: Understand that Barangay Density = (no of barangays) / (area in km²).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe and convert the "no of barangays" and "area (km square)" columns to numeric.  
Step 3: Calculate the barangay density for each city/municipality.  
Step 4: Find the city with the highest density and return its name.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert relevant columns to numeric
df['no of barangays'] = pd.to_numeric(df['no of barangays'], errors='coerce')
df['area (km square)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square)'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate barangay density
df['barangay_density'] = df['no of barangays'] / df['area (km square)']

# Find the city with the highest density
highest_density_city = df.loc[df['barangay_density'].idxmax(), 'city / municipality']
print(f"Final Answer: {highest_density_city}")
```
