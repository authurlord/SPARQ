Step 1: Identify the relevant data for Lincoln City in the Third Division North.  
Step 2: Look at the rows where Club is "Lincoln City" and Division is "Third Division North".  
Step 3: Sum the "Goals" column for those rows.  
Step 4: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Club is "Lincoln City" and Division is "Third Division North"
filtered_df = df[(df['Club'] == 'Lincoln City') & (df['Division'] == 'Third Division North')]
# Sum the 'Goals' column for these rows
total_goals = filtered_df['Goals'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_goals}")
```
