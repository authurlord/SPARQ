Step 1: The user wants to find the year with the largest increase in wins compared to the previous year.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'wins' column to integer type for numerical comparison.
Step 4: Calculate the difference in wins between consecutive years.
Step 5: Identify the year with the maximum increase.
Step 6: Output the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'wins' to integer
df['wins'] = pd.to_numeric(df['wins'])
# Calculate the difference in wins from the previous year
df['win_increase'] = df['wins'].diff()
# Find the year with the maximum increase (excluding the first year)
max_increase_year = df.loc[df['win_increase'].idxmax(), 'year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_increase_year}")
```
