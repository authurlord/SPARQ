Step 1: The user wants the average total value per year from 2005 to 2009.  
Step 2: Filter the data for years between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).  
Step 3: Calculate the mean of the 'total' column for the filtered rows.  
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where year is between 2005 and 2009 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].astype(int) >= 2005) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2009)]
# Calculate the mean of the 'total' column
average_total = filtered_df['total'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_total:.2f}")
```
