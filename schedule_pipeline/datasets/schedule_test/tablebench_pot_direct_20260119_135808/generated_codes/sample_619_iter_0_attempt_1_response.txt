Step 1: The user wants the total sales of the top 3 companies in the 'banking' industry.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'industry' is 'banking'.
Step 4: Sort by 'sales (billion )' in descending order and take the top 3.
Step 5: Sum the 'sales (billion )' values and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for banking industry
banking_companies = df[df['industry'] == 'banking']
# Sort by sales in descending order and get top 3
top_3_bank_sales = banking_companies.nlargest(3, 'sales (billion )')['sales (billion )'].astype(float).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {top_3_bank_sales:.2f}")
```
