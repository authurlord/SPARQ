Step 1: The question asks about the relationship between international tourism receipts and international tourist arrivals. This implies we need to analyze the correlation or trend between these two variables.

Step 2: Extract the relevant columns: 'internl tourist arrivals 2011 (x1000)' and 'internl tourism receipts 2011 (million usd)'.

Step 3: Compute the correlation coefficient between these two variables to determine the strength and direction of the relationship.

Step 4: Print the correlation value as the answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Select the relevant columns
arrivals = df['internl tourist arrivals 2011 (x1000)']
receipts = df['internl tourism receipts 2011 (million usd)']

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = arrivals.corr(receipts)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
