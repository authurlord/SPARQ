Step 1: Identify the companies in the 'banking' industry from the table.
Step 2: Filter the rows where the 'industry' column is 'banking'.
Step 3: Select only the top 3 companies by sales (billion).
Step 4: Sum their sales to get the total.

Note: The data shows that the 'banking' industry includes:
- Citigroup (rank 1)
- General Electric (rank 2) — but industry is "conglomerates", not banking
- American International Group (rank 3) — insurance
- Bank of America (rank 4) — banking
- HSBC (rank 5) — banking

So, the banking companies are:
- Citigroup
- Bank of America
- HSBC

These are the only ones in the 'banking' industry. Since there are only 3, they are the top 3.

Step 5: Extract their sales and sum them.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for banking industry
banking_df = df[df['industry'] == 'banking']
# Extract sales values and sum them
total_sales = banking_df['sales (billion )'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_sales}")
```
