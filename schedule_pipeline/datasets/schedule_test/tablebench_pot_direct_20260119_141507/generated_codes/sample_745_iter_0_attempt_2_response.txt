Step 3: We need to find the average finish position and total winnings for the driver in the year when they participated in the most races (which is 2002, with 5 races). Since there is no explicit driver name or individual tracking, we assume the question refers to the performance of the driver in the year with the highest number of starts (i.e., 2002).

Step 6: Output the average finish position and total winnings from 2002.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row for the year 2002 (last active year)
```
