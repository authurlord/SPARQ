Step 1: The user wants to find the nation that won exactly 4 gold medals and 3 silver medals.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Gold' is 4 and 'Silver' is 3.  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding 'Nation' value.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter nation with 4 gold and 3 silver medals
result = df[(df['Gold'] == 4) & (df['Silver'] == 3)]['Nation'].values
print(f"Final Answer: {result[0]}")
```
