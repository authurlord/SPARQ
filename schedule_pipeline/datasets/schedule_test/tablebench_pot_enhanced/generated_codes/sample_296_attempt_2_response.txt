Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete won a gold medal (1st position) in the 200m event at the European Junior Championships.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Event' is '200 m', 'Position' is '1st', and 'Competition' is 'European Junior Championships'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for gold medal (1st position) in 200m at European Junior Championships
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == '200 m') & (df['Position'] == '1st') & (df['Competition'] == 'European Junior Championships')]
year = filtered_df['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
