Step 1: The user is asking for a prediction of the number of major hurricanes in the next decade based on historical data from 1860 to 1868. However, the data provided only spans 9 years, and there is no clear trend or statistical model available to make a reliable prediction. Therefore, we can only calculate the average number of major hurricanes per year over the given period as a baseline estimate.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Extract the 'number of major hurricanes' column.

Step 4: Compute the mean (average) number of major hurricanes per year.

Step 5: Since the question asks for a prediction for the next decade, multiply the average by 10 to estimate total major hurricanes over 10 years.

Step 6: Output the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate average number of major hurricanes per year
avg_major_hurricanes_per_year = df['number of major hurricanes'].mean()
# Predict total for the next decade
predicted_total = avg_major_hurricanes_per_year * 10
print(f"Final Answer: {predicted_total:.1f}")
```
