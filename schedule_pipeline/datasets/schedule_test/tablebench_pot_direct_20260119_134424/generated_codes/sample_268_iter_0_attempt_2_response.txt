Step 1: The user wants the average rating of episodes that meet two conditions:
- Viewership (in millions) is at least 10 million.
- Timeslot rank is within the top 3 (i.e., timeslot rank <= 3).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where 'viewers (m)' >= 10.0 and 'timeslot rank' <= 3.

Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'rating' column for the filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'viewers (m)' to float and 'timeslot rank' to int for comparison
df['viewers (m)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['viewers (m)'])
df['timeslot rank'] = pd.to_numeric(df['timeslot rank'])

# Filter episodes with viewership >= 10 million and timeslot rank <= 3
filtered_episodes = df[(df['viewers (m)'] >= 10.0) & (df['timeslot rank'] <= 3)]

# Calculate average rating
avg_rating = filtered_episodes['rating'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_rating:.2f}")
```
