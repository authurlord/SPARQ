Step 1: The user is asking about the trend of qualifying speed ('qual') from 1960 to 1971 and how this trend might influence finishing positions in the next year (1972).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'qual' column and convert it to numeric for analysis.  
Step 4: Plot the 'qual' values over the years to visualize the trend.  
Step 5: Analyze the trend (increasing/decreasing) and infer its potential impact on finishing positions.  
Step 6: Since we cannot predict the future with certainty, we can only infer based on observed trends—higher qualifying speeds generally correlate with better finishing positions.  
Step 7: Use matplotlib to plot the trend.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'qual' to numeric
df['qual'] = pd.to_numeric(df['qual'])

# Plot the trend of qualifying speed over the years
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 5))
plt.plot(df['year'], df['qual'], marker='o', linestyle='-', color='b')
plt.title('Trend of Qualifying Speed (1960–1971)')
plt.xlabel('Year')
plt.ylabel('Qualifying Speed (mph)')
plt.grid(True)
plt.show()

# Analyze trend: if qualifying speed is increasing, it may lead to better finishing positions
trend = "increasing" if df['qual'].iloc[-1] > df['qual'].iloc[0] else "decreasing"
print(f"Final Answer: {trend}")
```
