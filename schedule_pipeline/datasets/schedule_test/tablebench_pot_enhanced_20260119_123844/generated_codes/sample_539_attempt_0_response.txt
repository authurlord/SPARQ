Step 1: The user wants to identify the year(s) where the natural growth rate significantly differs from the average natural growth rate between 1990 and 2000.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter data for years 1990 to 2000 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Calculate the average natural growth rate for this period.  
Step 5: Identify the year(s) where the natural growth rate deviates significantly (e.g., more than 0.5 units away from the average).  
Step 6: Return the year(s) that meet the condition.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'natural growth' to float, handling potential string values
df['natural growth'] = pd.to_numeric(df['natural growth'], errors='coerce')

# Filter data for years 1990 to 2000
df_1990_2000 = df[(df['year'].astype(int) >= 1990) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2000)]

# Calculate average natural growth rate
avg_natural_growth = df_1990_2000['natural growth'].mean()

# Define a threshold for "significantly different" (e.g., > 0.5 from average)
threshold = 0.5
significant_years = df_1990_2000[
    (df_1990_2000['natural growth'] - avg_natural_growth).abs() > threshold
]['year'].tolist()

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(significant_years)}")
```
