Step 1: The user wants the average total value per year for the years 2005 to 2009.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'total' column for the filtered data.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years 2005 to 2009 inclusive
filtered_df = df[df['year'].between(2005, 2009)]
# Calculate the average of the 'total' column
average_total = filtered_df['total'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_total:.2f}")
```
