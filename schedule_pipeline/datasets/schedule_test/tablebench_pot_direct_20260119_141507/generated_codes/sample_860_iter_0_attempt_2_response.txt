Step 1: Identify the relevant data for Córdoba and Valencia.  
The "Executions in persona" and "Executions in effigie" columns are needed.  
Step 2: Find the total executions (in persona + in effigie) for both Córdoba and Valencia.  
Step 3: Compute the difference between the two totals.  

From the table:  
- Córdoba: Executions in persona = 17, Executions in effigie = 19 → Total = 17 + 19 = 36  
- Valencia: Executions in persona = 2, Executions in effigie = 0 → Total = 2 + 0 = 2  
Difference = 36 - 2 = 34  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the relevant rows for Córdoba and Valencia
cordoba_executions = df[df['Tribunal'] == 'Córdoba']['Executions in persona'].values[0] + df[df['Tribunal'] == 'Córdoba']['Executions in effigie'].values[0]
valencia_executions = df[df['Tribunal'] == 'Valencia']['Executions in persona'].values[0] + df[df['Tribunal'] == 'Valencia']['Executions in effigie'].values[0]

difference = cordoba_executions - valencia_executions
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
