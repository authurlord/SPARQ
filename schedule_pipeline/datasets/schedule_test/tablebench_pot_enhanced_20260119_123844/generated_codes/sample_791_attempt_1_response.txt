Step 1: The user wants the median number of aircraft movements for the top 10 busiest airports.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the top 10 airports (first 10 rows based on rank).
Step 4: Extract the 'aircraft movements' column and calculate the median.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Select top 10 airports based on rank
top_10 = df.head(10)
# Calculate median of aircraft movements
median_movements = top_10['aircraft movements'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_movements}")
```
