Step 1: The user wants to determine if there is a significant correlation between the area (km²) and GDP (billion US$) of member countries.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Convert the 'area (km square)' and 'gdp (billion us)' columns to numeric type for analysis.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between the two variables.  
Step 5: Interpret the correlation coefficient (values near 1 or -1 indicate strong correlation, near 0 indicate weak).  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient and a conclusion based on its magnitude.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['area (km square)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square)'])
df['gdp (billion us)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['gdp (billion us)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km square)'].corr(df['gdp (billion us)'])

# Output conclusion and correlation coefficient
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
