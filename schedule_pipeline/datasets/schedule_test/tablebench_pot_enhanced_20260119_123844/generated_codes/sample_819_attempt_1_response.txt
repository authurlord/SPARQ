Step 1: Identify the couples who have performed more than 7 dances by filtering the 'number of dances' column.
Step 2: Extract the 'average' points for these couples.
Step 3: Calculate the variance of these average points.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter couples who performed more than 7 dances
filtered_data = df[df['number of dances'] > 7]
# Calculate variance of the 'average' column
variance = filtered_data['average'].var()
print(f"Final Answer: {variance:.2f}")
```
