Step 1: Identify the total passengers at Glasgow International Airport from the table.  
Step 2: Calculate a 15% increase on that value.  
Step 3: Output the resulting number of passengers in 2008.  

Note: The original value for Glasgow International is in the row with rank 8, under "total passengers".  

Corrected code to avoid f-string issues and ensure clarity:

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the total passengers at Glasgow International Airport
glasgow_passengers = df[df['airport'] == 'glasgow international']['total passengers'].values[0]
# Calculate 15% increase
increased_passengers = glasgow_passengers * 1.15
print(f"Final Answer: {int(increased_passengers)}")
```
