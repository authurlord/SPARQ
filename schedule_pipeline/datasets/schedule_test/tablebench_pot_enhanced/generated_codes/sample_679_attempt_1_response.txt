Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between two numerical columns: 'carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)' and 'avg emission per km² of its land (tons)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the two columns and convert them to numeric values (handling any non-numeric entries if needed).
Step 4: Compute the correlation coefficient using pandas' .corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert the relevant columns to numeric, forcing errors to be coerced to NaN
emissions_per_person = pd.to_numeric(df['carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)'], errors='coerce')
avg_emission_per_km2 = pd.to_numeric(df['avg emission per km 2 of its land (tons)'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with missing values after conversion
clean_data = pd.concat([emissions_per_person, avg_emission_per_km2], axis=1).dropna()

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = clean_data.iloc[:, 0].corr(clean_data.iloc[:, 1])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
