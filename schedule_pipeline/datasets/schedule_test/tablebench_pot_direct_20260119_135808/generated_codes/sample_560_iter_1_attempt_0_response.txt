Step 1: The user wants the average number of appearances (apps) per year for Castleford Tigers from 2005 to 2009.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the year is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).
Step 4: Select the 'apps' column and convert it to numeric.
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the 'apps' column for those years.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years 2005 to 2009
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= '2005') & (df['year'] <= '2009')]
# Convert 'apps' column to numeric
filtered_df['apps'] = pd.to_numeric(filtered_df['apps'])
# Calculate average apps
avg_apps = filtered_df['apps'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_apps:.1f}")
```
