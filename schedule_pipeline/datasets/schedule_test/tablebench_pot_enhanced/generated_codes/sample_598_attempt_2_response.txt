Step 1: The user wants to calculate the new viewership after a 10% increase from 9.2 million.
Step 2: Use the formula: new viewership = original viewership * (1 + 0.10).
Step 3: Perform the calculation and print the result.
```python
# Original viewership in millions
original_viewership = 9.2

# Calculate 10% increase
new_viewership = original_viewership * 1.10

print(f"Final Answer: {new_viewership:.1f}")
```
