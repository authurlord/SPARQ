Step 1: Identify the artist with the highest and second-highest points.  
Step 2: Extract the points for these two artists.  
Step 3: Compute the difference between the highest and second-highest points.  
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by points in descending order and get the top two
top_two = df.sort_values(by='points', ascending=False).head(2)
highest_points = top_two.iloc[0]['points']
second_highest_points = top_two.iloc[1]['points']
difference = highest_points - second_highest_points
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
