Step 1: The user wants to find the competition where the athlete achieved a position of 37th in the 20 km walk event.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'Position' is '37th' and 'Event' is '20 km walk'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Competition' column value from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for position 37th in 20 km walk
filtered_row = df[(df['Position'] == '37th') & (df['Event'] == '20 km walk')]
competition = filtered_row['Competition'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {competition}")
```
