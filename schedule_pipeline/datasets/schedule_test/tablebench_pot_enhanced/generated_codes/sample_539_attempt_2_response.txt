Step 1: The user wants to identify the year(s) where the natural growth rate was significantly different from the average natural growth rate between 1990 and 2000.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter data for years 1990 to 2000 (inclusive).

Step 4: Calculate the average natural growth rate for this period.

Step 5: Compare each year's natural growth rate to the average, and flag any that are significantly different (e.g., more than one standard deviation away or visually distinct; here we use a simple threshold of >1.5 times the average as "significantly different" for clarity).

Step 6: Return the year(s) that meet the condition.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'natural growth' to float
df['natural growth'] = pd.to_numeric(df['natural growth'], errors='coerce')

# Filter data from 1990 to 2000
df_1990_2000 = df[(df['year'] >= '1990') & (df['year'] <= '2000')]

# Calculate average natural growth rate
avg_growth = df_1990_2000['natural growth'].mean()

# Identify years with significantly different natural growth (e.g., > 1.5x average)
# We'll define "significantly different" as values outside the range [avg - 0.5, avg + 0.5] for simplicity
threshold = 0.5
diff_years = df_1990_2000[abs(df_1990_2000['natural growth'] - avg_growth) > threshold]['year']

# Output the result
print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(diff_years)}")
```
