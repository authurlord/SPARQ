Step 1: The user wants to find the correlation between the 'poles' and 'wins' columns across different series and seasons.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'poles' and 'wins' columns.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.  
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'poles' and 'wins'
correlation = df['poles'].corr(df['wins'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
