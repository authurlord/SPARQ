We are asked to calculate the **Goal Percentage** for Liverpool in the **1995–1996 season**. 

Goal Percentage = (Goals scored) / (Total appearances)

From the table:
- For Liverpool in the 1995–1996 season:
  - "League" column shows "Premier League"
  - "Apps" (appearances) = 38
  - "Goals" = 6

Note: The data has multiple columns like "League_1", "League_2", etc., but the total goals and total appearances for a specific season are in the "Apps" and "Goals" columns under the main "League" section.

Looking at the row:
['Liverpool', '1995–96', 'Premier League', '40', '7', '7', '2', '4', '1', '4', '1', '53', '10']

Breakdown:
- League Apps = 40
- League Goals = 7
- National Cup Apps = 7, Goals = 2
- League Cup Apps = 4, Goals = 1
- Europe Apps = 4, Goals = 1
- Total Apps = 53
- Total Goals = 10

But the question asks for **Goal Percentage**, defined as:
> ratio of goals scored to total number of appearances

So we use **Total Goals** and **Total Apps** from the last two columns: "Total" and "Total_1"

In this row:
- Total Apps = 53
- Total Goals = 10

Thus, Goal Percentage = 10 / 53

We will extract this value from the row corresponding to Liverpool in 1995–96.

Step-by-step plan:
1. Load the DataFrame.
2. Filter the row where Club is 'Liverpool' and Season is '1995–96'.
3. Extract the "Total" column (total appearances) and "Goals" (total goals).
4. Compute the ratio (goals / total apps).
5. Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter row for Liverpool in 1995–96 season
row = df[(df['Club'] == 'Liverpool') & (df['Season'] == '1995–96')]
# Extract total goals and total appearances
total_goals = row['Total_1'].values[0]
total_apps = row['Total'].values[0]
# Calculate goal percentage
goal_percentage = total_goals / total_apps
print(f"Final Answer: {goal_percentage:.3f}")
```
