Step 1: The user wants the average revenue of the top 5 companies in the 'banking' industry.  
Step 2: Filter the dataframe to include only rows where the 'industry' is 'banking'.  
Step 3: Among these, select the top 5 companies by revenue (we assume ranking by revenue is implied by the rank in the table).  
Step 4: Extract the 'revenue (millions)' values for these top 5 companies and compute their mean.  
Step 5: Print the result.

Note: In the table, the 'rank' column does not directly indicate revenue order, but since the data is sorted by rank, we can assume that higher rank numbers correspond to lower positions. So, the top 5 companies in banking are the first 5 entries with 'industry' = 'banking'.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where industry is 'banking'
banking_df = df[df['industry'] == 'banking']

# Take the top 5 companies (first 5 rows) in banking industry
top_5_banking = banking_df.head(5)

# Calculate the average revenue (in millions)
average_revenue = top_5_banking['revenue (millions)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_revenue:.1f}")
```
