The error occurred because the "agricultural panel" column contains string values (like '4') that are being compared with integers. However, in the data, the values are actually numeric strings. We need to convert them to integers before performing the comparison.

Step 1: Load the dataframe.
Step 2: Convert the "agricultural panel" column to integers.
Step 3: Filter parties that have at least 2 seats in the agricultural panel.
Step 4: Sum the total seats for those parties.
Step 5: Calculate the percentage of total seats they represent.
Step 6: Print both the total number of seats and the percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert agricultural panel column to integer
df['agricultural panel'] = pd.to_numeric(df['agricultural panel'], errors='coerce')

# Filter parties with at least 2 seats in agricultural panel
filtered_parties = df[df['agricultural panel'] >= 2]

# Calculate total seats for these parties
total_seats_filtered = filtered_parties['total'].sum()

# Total seats across all parties
total_seats_all = df['total'].sum()

# Calculate percentage
percentage = (total_seats_filtered / total_seats_all) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {total_seats_filtered}, {percentage:.1f}")
```
