Step 1: The user wants the total number of males in the 20-29 and 30-39 age groups.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Identify the row corresponding to males in the 20-29 age group (index 5, column 'POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_3') and the 30-39 age group (index 5, column 'POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_4').
Step 4: Sum these two values and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the number of males in the 20-29 age group and 30-39 age group
males_20_29 = df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_3']
males_30_39 = df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_4']
total_males = males_20_29 + males_30_39
print(f"Final Answer: {total_males}")
```
