The error occurred because the 'share of votes' column contains string values (e.g., '26.2%'), not numeric values. To compute the difference between the highest and lowest shares, we must first convert the percentage strings to numeric values by removing the '%' sign and converting to float.

Step 1: Load the dataframe.
Step 2: Convert the 'share of votes' column from string with '%' to numeric (float).
Step 3: Find the maximum and minimum values of the share of votes.
Step 4: Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest.
Step 5: Identify the election corresponding to the highest and lowest share of votes.
Step 6: Print the election with the highest share, the election with the lowest share, and the difference.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'share of votes' to numeric by removing '%' and converting to float
df['share of votes'] = df['share of votes'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)

# Find the election with the highest and lowest share of votes
max_votes = df.loc[df['share of votes'].idxmax(), 'election']
min_votes = df.loc[df['share of votes'].idxmin(), 'election']
difference = df['share of votes'].max() - df['share of votes'].min()

print(f"Final Answer: {max_votes}, {min_votes}, {difference:.1f}")
```
