The error occurred because the year column contains strings (e.g., "2002"), and we are trying to filter based on year comparison. We need to ensure that the year values are properly converted to integers when filtering.

Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the dataframe.
2. Extract the 'mintage' values for years 2002 to 2005.
3. Convert the 'year' column to integer type to enable proper comparison.
4. Filter rows where year is between 2002 and 2005 (inclusive).
5. Sum the mintages of those rows.
6. Divide the total by 5 to get the number of coins per person.
7. Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'year' column to integer for proper filtering
df['year'] = df['year'].astype(int)
# Filter rows where year is between 2002 and 2005 inclusive
filtered_mintage = df[(df['year'] >= 2002) & (df['year'] <= 2005)]['mintage'].sum()
# Divide by 5 to get coins per person
coins_per_person = filtered_mintage / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {coins_per_person}")
```
