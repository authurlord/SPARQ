Step 1: The user wants the total area of all countries/regions listed in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'area km square' column and convert it to numeric.
Step 4: Sum the values and print the total.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'area km square' to numeric and sum
total_area = df['area km square'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_area}")
```
