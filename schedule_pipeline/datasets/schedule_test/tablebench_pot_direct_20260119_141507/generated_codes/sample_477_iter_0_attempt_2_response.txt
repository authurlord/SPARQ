Step 1: The question asks to identify songs whose values significantly deviate from the pattern observed in other songs.  
Step 2: "Values" here likely refer to the "points" column, as it is numerical and can be analyzed for outliers.  
Step 3: We will calculate the mean and standard deviation of the "points" column to establish a baseline.  
Step 4: Identify any song with points that are more than 2 standard deviations away from the mean (common outlier detection method).  
Step 5: Return the song names corresponding to such outliers.

Note: The "place" column has a value of "-5" which is unusual (negative), but we focus on "points" as the primary numerical variable for deviation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation of points
mean_points = df['points'].mean()
std_points = df['points'].std()

# Identify outliers using 2 standard deviations rule
outliers = df[(df['points'] > mean_points + 2 * std_points) | (df['points'] < mean_points - 2 * std_points)]

# Extract the song names of outliers
outlier_songs = outliers['song'].tolist()

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(outlier_songs)}")
```
