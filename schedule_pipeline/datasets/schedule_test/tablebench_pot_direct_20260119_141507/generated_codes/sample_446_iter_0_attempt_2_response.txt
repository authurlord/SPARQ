Step 1: The question asks for member countries whose population or GDP per capita deviates significantly from the norm. "Significantly deviate" implies outliers — values that are far from the mean or median.

Step 2: We will calculate the mean and standard deviation of population and GDP per capita. Then, identify countries where population or GDP per capita is more than 2 standard deviations away from the mean (common outlier rule).

Step 3: Since there are duplicate entries (e.g., Luxembourg and Netherlands), we first deduplicate by member country and ensure we're analyzing unique entries.

Step 4: Use the z-score method to detect outliers in both population and GDP per capita.

Step 5: Print the list of such countries.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Remove duplicates based on 'member countries'
df = df.drop_duplicates(subset='member countries')

# Convert population and GDP per capita to numeric
df['population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population'], errors='coerce')
df['gdp per capita (us)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['gdp per capita (us)'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with NaN after conversion
df = df.dropna(subset=['population', 'gdp per capita (us)'])

# Calculate mean and std for population and GDP per capita
mean_pop = df['population'].mean()
std_pop = df['population'].std()
mean_gdp_per_capita = df['gdp per capita (us)'].mean()
std_gdp_per_capita = df['gdp per capita (us)'].std()

# Identify outliers using z-score: |z| > 2
outliers_pop = df[(np.abs((df['population'] - mean_pop) / std_pop) > 2)]
outliers_gdp = df[(np.abs((df['gdp per capita (us)'] - mean_gdp_per_capita) / std_gdp_per_capita) > 2)]

# Combine unique countries that are outliers in either metric
outlier_countries = set()
for country in outliers_pop['member countries']:
    outlier_countries.add(country)
for country in outliers_gdp['member countries']:
    outlier_countries.add(country)

# Convert to list and print
print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(outlier_countries)}")
```
