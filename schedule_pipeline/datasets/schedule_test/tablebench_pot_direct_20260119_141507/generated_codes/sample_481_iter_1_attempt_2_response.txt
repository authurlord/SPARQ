Step 1: The user wants to identify breeds with unusually high or low registrations in specific years.  
"Unusually high or low" suggests outliers — values that are significantly above or below the mean or median for each year.  

Step 2: We will analyze the data by year (2002, 2005, 2011) and calculate the mean and standard deviation for each breed's registration.  
Then, we'll flag breeds whose registrations are more than 2 standard deviations away from the mean in any year.

Step 3: We'll identify such breeds and years, and report them clearly.

Note: Since the data is categorical (breed), and we want "unusually high or low", we will use statistical outlier detection per year.

We will:
- Group by year.
- For each year, compute mean and std of registrations.
- Identify any breed with registration > mean + 2*std or < mean - 2*std.

Let’s implement this step-by-step:

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert registration columns to numeric (in case of string numbers)
for col in ['2002 registrations', '2005 registrations', '2011 registrations']:
    df[col] = pd.to_numeric(df[col], errors='coerce')

# Identify outliers per year using 2 standard deviations from mean
outliers = []

for year_col in ['2002 registrations', '2005 registrations', '2011 registrations']:
    year_data = df[[year_col, 'breed']].dropna()
    if year_data.empty:
        continue
    
    mean_val = year_data[year_col].mean()
    std_val = year_data[year_col].std()
    if std_val == 0:
        continue
    
    # Find outliers: more than 2 std away from mean
    lower_bound = mean_val - 2 * std_val
    upper_bound = mean_val + 2 * std_val
    
    outliers_in_year = year_data[
        (year_data[year_col] < lower_bound) | (year_data[year_col] > upper_bound)
    ]
    
    if not outliers_in_year.empty:
        for _, row in outliers_in_year.iterrows():
            outliers.append({
                'year': year_col.split()[0],
                'breed': row['breed'],
                'registration': row[year_col]
            })

# Extract unique breed-year combinations
outlier_breeds = set()
for o in outliers:
    outlier_breeds.add(o['breed'])

# Final answer: list of breeds with outliers (as requested)
final_breeds = list(outlier_breeds)

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(final_breeds)}")
```
