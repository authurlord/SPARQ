Step 1: The user wants to find the team that has exactly 13 points and a goal difference of 17.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'points' is 13 and 'difference' is 17.  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding 'team' name.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter team with 13 points and goal difference of 17
team = df[(df['points'] == 13) & (df['difference'] == 17)]['team'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {team}")
```
