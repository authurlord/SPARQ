Step 1: The user wants to calculate the new combined student population after increasing the current populations of Karolinska Institutet and Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences by 18%.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter the rows for 'karolinska institutet' and 'swedish university of agricultural sciences'.

Step 4: Extract their 'student population ( fte , 2009)' values, convert to integers.

Step 5: Sum the two populations.

Step 6: Increase the sum by 18% (multiply by 1.18).

Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter the two universities
karolinska = df[df['university'] == 'karolinska institutet']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]
swedish_ag = df[df['university'] == 'swedish university of agricultural sciences']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]

# Convert to integers and calculate combined population
combined_population = int(karolinska) + int(swedish_ag)

# Increase by 18%
new_combined_population = combined_population * 1.18

print(f"Final Answer: {int(new_combined_population)}")
```
