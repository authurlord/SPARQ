Step 1: Identify the earliest two years in the table. From the data, the years are 1893, 1894, 1896, 1897, and 1898. The earliest two years are 1893 and 1894.

Step 2: Filter the rows corresponding to these two years.

Step 3: Sum the "Quantity" column for these filtered rows.

Step 4: Output the total quantity.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for the earliest two years: 1893 and 1894
filtered_df = df[(df['Year'] == '1893') | (df['Year'] == '1894')]
# Sum the quantity of orders
total_quantity = filtered_df['Quantity'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_quantity}")
```
