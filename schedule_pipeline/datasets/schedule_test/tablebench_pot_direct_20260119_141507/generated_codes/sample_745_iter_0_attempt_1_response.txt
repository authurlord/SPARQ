Step 1: The question asks for an estimate of the average finish position and total winnings for a driver in the next racing season, assuming they participate in a similar number of races as in their last active year.

Step 2: Identify the "last active year" from the data. Looking at the years listed (1990 to 2002), the most recent year is 2002, with only 5 starts. However, the driver had 1 start in 2001 and 5 in 2002. Since 2002 is the last active year, we use that as the reference.

Step 3: Extract the average finish position and total winnings from the row corresponding to 2002.

Step 4: Use those values as the likely estimates for the next season.

Step 5: Print the average finish position and total winnings from the 2002 row.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row for the last active year (2002)
last_active_row = df[df['year'] == '2002'].iloc[0]
average_finish_position = last_active_row['avg finish']
total_winnings = last_active_row['winnings']
print(f"Final Answer: {average_finish_position}, {total_winnings}")
```
