Step 1: The user is asking about the relationship between GDP (PPP) per capita and metropolitan population. Specifically, they want to know how GDP per capita changes as population increases.

Step 2: We need to examine the trend in GDP (PPP) per capita as metropolitan population increases. Since both variables are numerical, we can analyze their correlation or plot them to observe the relationship.

Step 3: Load the data into a DataFrame.
Step 4: Extract the 'metropolitan population (2006) millions' and 'gdp (ppp) us per capita' columns.
Step 5: Compute the correlation between these two variables to determine the nature of the relationship (positive, negative, or no trend).
Step 6: Optionally, visualize the relationship using a scatter plot for clarity.

Step 7: Since the question asks "how does it change", the most relevant answer is the correlation coefficient, which indicates the strength and direction of the relationship.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the relevant columns
population = df['metropolitan population (2006) millions']
gdp_per_capita = df['gdp (ppp) us per capita']

# Calculate the correlation between population and GDP per capita
correlation = population.corr(gdp_per_capita)

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
