Step 1: The user wants to identify the year(s) where the natural growth rate was significantly different from the average natural growth rate between 1990 and 2000.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter data for years 1990 to 2000 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Calculate the average natural growth rate for this period.  
Step 5: Compare each year's natural growth rate to the average.  
Step 6: Identify years where the difference is large (e.g., more than 0.5 units away from the average).  
Step 7: Return the year(s) that stand out.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter data for years 1990 to 2000
df_1990_2000 = df[(df['year'] >= '1990') & (df['year'] <= '2000')]

# Convert 'natural growth' to float for calculation
df_1990_2000['natural growth'] = pd.to_numeric(df_1990_2000['natural growth'])

# Calculate average natural growth rate from 1990 to 2000
avg_natural_growth = df_1990_2000['natural growth'].mean()

# Find years where natural growth is significantly different (more than 0.5 away from average)
threshold = 0.5
diff = abs(df_1990_2000['natural growth'] - avg_natural_growth)
significant_years = df_1990_2000[diff > threshold]['year']

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(significant_years)}")
```
