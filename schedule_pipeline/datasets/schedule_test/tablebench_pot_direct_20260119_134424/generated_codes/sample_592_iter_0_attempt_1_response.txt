Step 1: The user wants the difference in population between the 6th ranked city (valenza) and the city 'acqui terme'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the row where 'city' is 'acqui terme' and extract its population.
Step 4: Filter the row where 'rank' is '6th' and extract its population.
Step 5: Calculate the difference between the two populations and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract population of 'acqui terme'
acqui_population = df[df['city'] == 'acqui terme']['population'].iloc[0]
# Extract population of 'valenza' (6th ranked city)
valenza_population = df[df['rank'] == '6th']['population'].iloc[0]
# Calculate the difference
difference = int(valenza_population) - int(acqui_population)
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
