Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in 'property taxes' from 2000 to 2005.  
Step 2: Extract the 'property taxes' values for years 2000 to 2005.  
Step 3: Calculate the total increase over 5 years and divide by 5 to get the average annual increase.  
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract property taxes for years 2000 to 2005
property_taxes = df[df['year'].between(2000, 2005)]['property taxes'].astype(int)
# Calculate the average annual increase
total_increase = property_taxes.iloc[-1] - property_taxes.iloc[0]
average_increase = total_increase / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {average_increase:.0f}")
```
