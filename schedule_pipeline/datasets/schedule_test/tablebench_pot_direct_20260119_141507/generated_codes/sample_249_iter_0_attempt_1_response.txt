Step 1: Identify companies in the "oil and gas" industry with sales of at least 300 billion.  
Step 2: Filter the dataframe for these companies.  
Step 3: Extract the "market value (billion)" column for the filtered companies.  
Step 4: Compute the average of the filtered market values.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter companies in 'oil and gas' industry with sales >= 300 billion
filtered_df = df[(df['industry'] == 'oil and gas') & (df['sales (billion )'] >= 300)]
# Calculate average market value
avg_market_value = filtered_df['market value (billion )'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_market_value:.1f}")
```
