Step 1: The user wants the total number of wins by teams with a win percentage greater than 0.7.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'win pct' > 0.7.
Step 4: Sum the 'wins' column for these filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter teams with win percentage greater than 0.7
filtered_teams = df[df['win pct'].astype(float) > 0.7]
# Sum the wins for these teams
total_wins = filtered_teams['wins'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_wins}")
```
