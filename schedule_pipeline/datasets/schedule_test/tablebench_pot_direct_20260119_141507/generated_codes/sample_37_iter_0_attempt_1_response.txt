Step 1: The user wants the total average rating of all TV dramas.  
Step 2: The "average" column contains the average ratings for each drama.  
Step 3: We need to sum all values in the "average" column and divide by the number of rows to get the mean (average rating).  
Step 4: Since the question asks for the "total average rating", it is interpreted as the mean average rating across all dramas.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the mean of the 'average' column
mean_average_rating = df['average'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_average_rating}")
```
