Note: For the current mayor (Michel Angers), 'Leaving' is 'Current', so we assume they are still serving. Since no end year is given, we cannot include a full duration. But since the question asks for "all mayors listed", and Michel Angers has been in office since 2009, we can assume we calculate up to the current year (2024) for consistency unless specified otherwise.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Taking Office' and 'Leaving' to integers, handling 'Current' as 2024
df['Taking Office'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Taking Office'], errors='coerce')
df['Leaving'] = df['Leaving'].replace('Current', 2024)
df['Leaving'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Leaving'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate years served
df['Years Served'] = df['Leaving'] - df['Taking Office']

# Sum total years served
total_years = df['Years Served'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {int(total_years)}")
```
