Step 1: The user wants to determine if there is a relationship between typhus and typhoid fever cases across years.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'typhus' and 'typhoid fever' columns to numeric, handling any non-numeric values (e.g., '2940 (avg)' in 191822).
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between typhus and typhoid fever.
Step 5: Interpret the result: if the correlation is positive and significant, it suggests that an increase in typhus may be associated with an increase in typhoid fever.
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'typhus' and 'typhoid fever' columns to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['typhus'] = pd.to_numeric(df['typhus'], errors='coerce')
df['typhoid fever'] = pd.to_numeric(df['typhoid fever'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with missing values after conversion
df.dropna(subset=['typhus', 'typhoid fever'], inplace=True)

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['typhus'].corr(df['typhoid fever'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
