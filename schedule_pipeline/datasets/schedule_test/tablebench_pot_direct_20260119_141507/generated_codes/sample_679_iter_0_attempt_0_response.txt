Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between two numerical columns: 'carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person)' and 'avg emission per km² of its land'.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the two relevant columns.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient using pandas' `corr()` method.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between the two specified columns
correlation = df['carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)'].corr(df['avg emission per km² of its land (tons)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
