Step 1: The user wants to calculate the average score in the 'tumbling' category across all teams.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'tumbling' column and convert it to numeric values.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'tumbling' column and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'tumbling' column to numeric
df['tumbling'] = pd.to_numeric(df['tumbling'])
# Calculate the average score in the 'tumbling' category
average_tumbling = df['tumbling'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_tumbling:.1f}")
```
