Step 1: Identify the top 5 companies in the oil and gas industry from the table.
Step 2: Extract their sales values from the 'sales (billion )' column.
Step 3: Create a bar chart to visualize these sales values with company names on the x-axis and sales on the y-axis.

Note: The companies in the oil and gas industry are:
- ExxonMobil
- Royal Dutch Shell
- PetroChina
- Petrobras
- Chevron
- Total

We will take the top 5 by sales in this industry.

Step 4: Use matplotlib to plot the bar chart.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter companies in the oil and gas industry
oil_gas_companies = df[df['industry'] == 'oil and gas']

# Sort by sales in descending order and take top 5
top_5_oil_gas = oil_gas_companies.sort_values(by='sales (billion )', ascending=False).head(5)

# Extract company names and sales
companies = top_5_oil_gas['company']
sales = top_5_oil_gas['sales (billion )'].astype(float)

# Create bar chart
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
plt.bar(companies, sales, color='skyblue')
plt.title('Sales of Top 5 Oil and Gas Companies')
plt.xlabel('Company')
plt.ylabel('Sales (billion USD)')
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

# Final Answer: The bar chart has been created and displayed.
```
