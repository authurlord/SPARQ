Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of metabolic rates for female animals and to identify which animal has a metabolic rate more than one standard deviation away from the mean.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Sex' is 'Female'.
Step 4: Convert 'Metabolic rate' to float.
Step 5: Calculate the standard deviation.
Step 6: Identify animals with metabolic rates more than one standard deviation above or below the mean.
Step 7: Print the standard deviation and the animal(s) that meet the condition.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for female animals
female_data = df[df['Sex'] == 'Female']
# Convert 'Metabolic rate' to float
female_data['Metabolic rate'] = pd.to_numeric(female_data['Metabolic rate'])
# Calculate mean and standard deviation
mean_rate = female_data['Metabolic rate'].mean()
std_dev = female_data['Metabolic rate'].std()
# Find animals more than one standard deviation away from the mean
outliers = female_data[abs(female_data['Metabolic rate'] - mean_rate) > std_dev]
# Extract animal IDs
outlier_animals = outliers['Animal'].tolist()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_dev:.2f}, {', '.join(outlier_animals)}")
```
