Step 1: The question asks about the relationship between the human development index (2000) and population density across municipalities with varying areas. This implies we need to analyze the correlation between these two variables.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the relevant columns: 'human development index (2000)' and 'population density ( / km 2 )'. We will also consider 'area (km 2 )' as a factor that varies across municipalities.

Step 3: Calculate the correlation coefficient between 'human development index (2000)' and 'population density ( / km 2 )' to determine the strength and direction of their relationship.

Step 4: Print the correlation value.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between human development index and population density
correlation = df['human development index (2000)'].corr(df['population density ( / km 2 )'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
