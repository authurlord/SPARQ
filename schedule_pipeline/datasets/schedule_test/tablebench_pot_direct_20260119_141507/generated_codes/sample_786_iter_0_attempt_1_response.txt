Step 1: The user wants the median winnings for drivers who drove a Chevrolet car.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'make' is 'chevrolet'.  
Step 4: Extract the 'winnings' column from the filtered data.  
Step 5: Calculate the median of the 'winnings' values and print it.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter drivers who drove a Chevrolet car
chevrolet_drivers = df[df['make'] == 'chevrolet']
# Calculate the median of winnings for these drivers
median_winnings = chevrolet_drivers['winnings'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_winnings}")
```
