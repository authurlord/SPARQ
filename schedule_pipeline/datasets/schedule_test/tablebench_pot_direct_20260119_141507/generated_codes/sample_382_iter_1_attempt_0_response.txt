The error occurred because the 'win pct' column contains strings (e.g., '0.667') rather than floats, so we cannot directly compare them with a numeric condition like `>= 0.7`.

Step 1: Identify that the 'win pct' column is stored as strings.
Step 2: Convert the 'win pct' column to float for numerical comparison.
Step 3: Filter teams where the win percentage is 0.7 or higher.
Step 4: Count how many such teams exist.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'win pct' to float and filter teams with win percentage >= 0.7
df['win pct'] = df['win pct'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
teams_high_win_pct = df[df['win pct'] >= 0.7]
count_high_win_pct = len(teams_high_win_pct)

print(f"Final Answer: {count_high_win_pct}")
```
