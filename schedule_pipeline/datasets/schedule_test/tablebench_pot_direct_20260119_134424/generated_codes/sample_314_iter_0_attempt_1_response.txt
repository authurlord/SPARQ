Step 1: Identify the relevant information from the table. We are looking for a year where:
- The work was nominated for "Song of the Year" at the Grammy Awards.
- The work also won an award (i.e., "Result" is "Won").
- The category should be "Song of the Year" and the association should be "Grammy Awards".

Step 2: Filter rows where:
- 'Association' is 'Grammy Awards'
- 'Category' is 'Song of the Year'
- 'Result' is 'Won'

Step 3: Extract the corresponding 'Year' value.

Step 4: Print the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Association is 'Grammy Awards', Category is 'Song of the Year', and Result is 'Won'
filtered_df = df[(df['Association'] == 'Grammy Awards') & 
                 (df['Category'] == 'Song of the Year') & 
                 (df['Result'] == 'Won')]

# Extract the year
year = filtered_df['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
