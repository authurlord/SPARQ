Step 1: The user wants to find the nation that has a total of 13 medals.  
Step 2: Look at the 'total' column in the table. The row with 'total' in the 'nation' column has a total of 64 medals, so we are looking for a nation where the 'total' column value is 13.  
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'total' equals 13 and extract the corresponding nation.  
Step 4: Print the nation name.

Note: From the data, the first row (rank 1) has 'total' = 13 and the nation is 'south korea'.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the nation where total medals is 13
nation_with_13_medals = df[df['total'] == 13]['nation'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation_with_13_medals}")
```
