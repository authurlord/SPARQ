Step 1: The user wants the mean and median of the 'rating' column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'rating' column, convert to numeric (in case of any formatting issues), and compute mean and median.
Step 4: Print both values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'rating' to numeric and calculate mean and median
ratings = pd.to_numeric(df['rating'], errors='coerce')
mean_rating = ratings.mean()
median_rating = ratings.median()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_rating:.2f}, {median_rating:.2f}")
```
