Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'bp comp 1 (˚C)' and '% wt comp 1' columns.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'bp comp 1 (˚C)' and '% wt comp 1' to numeric type (handling negative values and string formatting).
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'bp comp 1 (˚C)' and '% wt comp 1' to numeric, handling negative signs and whitespace
df['bp comp 1 (˚C)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['bp comp 1 (˚C)'], errors='coerce')
df['% wt comp 1'] = pd.to_numeric(df['% wt comp 1'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['bp comp 1 (˚C)'].corr(df['% wt comp 1'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
