Step 1: The user wants the total number of students who took SAT subject tests in mathematics, including both Level 1 and Level 2.  
Step 2: Identify the rows where the 'subject' is "mathematics" (either "mathematics" or "mathematics level 1" or "mathematics level 2").  
Step 3: Extract the 'number of students' for those rows and sum them.  
Step 4: Print the total.

Note: From the table, "mathematics level 1" and "mathematics level 2" are both under the "mathematics" category. We need to sum their student counts.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where subject is 'mathematics', 'mathematics level 1', or 'mathematics level 2'
math_rows = df[(df['subject'] == 'mathematics') | 
               (df['subject'] == 'mathematics level 1') | 
               (df['subject'] == 'mathematics level 2')]

# Sum the number of students for these rows
total_students_math = math_rows['number of students'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_students_math}")
```
