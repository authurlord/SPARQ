Step 1: The user wants the total number of medals (sum of M36, M36B1, and M36B2) for the months from May 1944 to August 1944.  
Step 2: Filter the rows corresponding to the months in the range (May 1944 to August 1944).  
Step 3: For each filtered row, sum the values of M36, M36B1, and M36B2.  
Step 4: Add up these sums to get the total.  

Note: The data includes "Post-war" and "Total" rows which should be excluded. Only include months from May 1944 to August 1944.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for months from May 1944 to August 1944
filtered_df = df[(df['Month'] >= 'May 1944') & (df['Month'] <= 'August 1944')]

# Calculate the total medals (sum of M36 + M36B1 + M36B2) for the filtered rows
total_medals = filtered_df['M36'].sum() + filtered_df['M36B1'].sum() + filtered_df['M36B2'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_medals}")
```
