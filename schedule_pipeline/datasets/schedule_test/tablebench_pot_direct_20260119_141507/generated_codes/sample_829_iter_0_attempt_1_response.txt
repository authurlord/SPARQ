Step 1: The user wants the mean and standard deviation of the 'bello' column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'bello' column.
Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation using pandas functions.
Step 5: Print both values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation of 'bello' column
mean_bello = df['bello'].mean()
std_bello = df['bello'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_bello:.1f}, {std_bello:.1f}")
```
