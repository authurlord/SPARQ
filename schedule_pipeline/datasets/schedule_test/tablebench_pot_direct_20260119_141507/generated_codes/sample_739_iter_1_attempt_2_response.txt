Step 1: The user is asking to predict viewership and BBC Three weekly ranking for a hypothetical episode 9, based on historical data from episodes 1 to 8.  
Step 2: Since there is no explicit model or pattern provided (e.g., linear trend, regression), we must assess whether a simple trend (like average or median) can be used as a reasonable approximation.  
Step 3: We will calculate the average viewership and average BBC Three weekly ranking from the given episodes (1 to 8).  
Step 4: These averages can serve as a reasonable prediction for episode 9, assuming consistent trends.  

Note: This is a prediction based on simple averaging, not a statistical model.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert viewers to numeric (they are strings with commas)
df['viewers'] = df['viewers'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)

# Calculate average viewership and average BBC Three weekly ranking
avg_viewers = df['viewers'].mean()
avg_ranking = df['bbc three weekly ranking'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_viewers:.0f}, {avg_ranking:.0f}")
```
