Step 1: The user wants to find the year with the maximum increase in the number of tropical storms compared to the previous year.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'number of tropical storms' column to integers.
Step 4: Calculate the difference between consecutive years using the .diff() method.
Step 5: Find the index of the maximum difference.
Step 6: Get the corresponding year and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'number of tropical storms' to integer
df['number of tropical storms'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of tropical storms'])
# Calculate the difference between consecutive years
df['diff'] = df['number of tropical storms'].diff()
# Find the year with the maximum increase
max_increase_year = df.loc[df['diff'].idxmax(), 'year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_increase_year}")
```
