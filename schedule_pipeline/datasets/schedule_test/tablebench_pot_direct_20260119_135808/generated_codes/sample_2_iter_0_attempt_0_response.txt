Step 1: The user wants the total GDP (nominal) for countries with a UN budget greater than 2%.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'un budget' is greater than 2% (convert to float after removing the % sign).
Step 4: Sum the 'gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011' column for these countries.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'un budget' to float by removing '%' and dividing by 100
df['un budget'] = df['un budget'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Filter countries with UN budget > 2%
filtered_df = df[df['un budget'] > 2]
# Sum the GDP (nominal) for these countries
total_gdp_nominal = filtered_df['gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_gdp_nominal}")
```
