Step 1: The question asks about the relationship between elevation and prominence of mountains in Papua New Guinea. We need to analyze whether there is a trend (e.g., positive correlation) as elevation increases.

Step 2: Load the data into a DataFrame and extract the 'elevation (m)' and 'prominence (m)' columns.

Step 3: Calculate the correlation between elevation and prominence to determine if they change together.

Step 4: Interpret the correlation coefficient: a positive value indicates that as elevation increases, prominence tends to increase as well.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract elevation and prominence columns
elevation = df['elevation (m)'].astype(float)
prominence = df['prominence (m)'].astype(float)

# Calculate the correlation between elevation and prominence
correlation = elevation.corr(prominence)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
