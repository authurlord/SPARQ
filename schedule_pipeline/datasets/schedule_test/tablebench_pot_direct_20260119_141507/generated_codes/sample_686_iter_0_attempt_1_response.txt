Step 1: Identify the metro area with the highest 'number of jews (wjc)' and the one with the lowest.  
Step 2: Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest values.  
Step 3: Return both the metro area with the highest value and the difference.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'number of jews (wjc)' to integer for accurate comparison
df['number of jews (wjc)'] = df['number of jews (wjc)'].astype(int)

# Find the metro area with the highest and lowest 'number of jews (wjc)'
max_jews = df.loc[df['number of jews (wjc)'].idxmax(), 'metro area']
min_jews = df.loc[df['number of jews (wjc)'].idxmin(), 'metro area']

# Calculate the difference
difference = df['number of jews (wjc)'].max() - df['number of jews (wjc)'].min()

print(f"Final Answer: {max_jews}, {difference}")
```
