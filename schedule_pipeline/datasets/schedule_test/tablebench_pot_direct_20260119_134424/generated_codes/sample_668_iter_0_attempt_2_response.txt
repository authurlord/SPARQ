Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the 'area (km square) territorial waters' and 'percentage of total area (foreez)' for each ecozone.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the two columns: 'area (km square) territorial waters' and 'percentage of total area (foreez)'.
Step 4: Convert these columns to numeric type to ensure proper calculation.
Step 5: Compute the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() function.
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric, handling any potential non-numeric entries
df['area (km square) territorial waters'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square) territorial waters'], errors='coerce')
df['percentage of total area (foreez)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with NaN values in either column
df.dropna(subset=['area (km square) territorial waters', 'percentage of total area (foreez)'], inplace=True)

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km square) territorial waters'].corr(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
