Step 1: The user is asking about the relationship between international tourist arrivals and international tourism receipts. Specifically, they want to understand how receipts change as arrivals increase.

Step 2: We need to examine the correlation between "internl tourist arrivals 2011 (x1000)" and "internl tourism receipts 2011 (million usd)".

Step 3: Load the dataframe and compute the correlation coefficient between these two columns.

Step 4: Interpret the result — a positive correlation indicates that as tourist arrivals increase, receipts also tend to increase.

Step 5: Print the correlation value.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between tourist arrivals and tourism receipts
correlation = df['internl tourist arrivals 2011 (x1000)'].corr(df['internl tourism receipts 2011 (million usd)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
