Step 1: The user wants to identify the year and venue where the athlete achieved a personal best (PB) time in the Irish National Marathon Championship.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'Notes' column contains 'PB' and the 'Competition' is 'Irish National Marathon Championship'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' and 'Venue' for that row.
Step 5: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for rows where Notes contain 'PB' and Competition is 'Irish National Marathon Championship'
pb_row = df[(df['Notes'] == '2:12:19 PB') & (df['Competition'] == 'Irish National Marathon Championship')]
# Extract Year and Venue
year = pb_row['Year'].values[0]
venue = pb_row['Venue'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}, {venue}")
```
