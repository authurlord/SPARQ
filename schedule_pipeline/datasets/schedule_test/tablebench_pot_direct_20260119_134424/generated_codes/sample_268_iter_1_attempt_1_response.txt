Step 1: The user wants the average rating of episodes that meet two conditions:
- Viewership of at least 10 million (viewers (m) >= 10)
- Ranked within the top 3 in their timeslot (timeslot rank <= 3)

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'viewers (m)' and 'timeslot rank' columns to numeric, handling any non-numeric values.
Step 4: Filter episodes based on the two conditions.
Step 5: Calculate the average of the 'rating' column for the filtered episodes.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert necessary columns to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['viewers (m)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['viewers (m)'], errors='coerce')
df['timeslot rank'] = pd.to_numeric(df['timeslot rank'], errors='coerce')
df['rating'] = pd.to_numeric(df['rating'], errors='coerce')

# Filter episodes with viewers >= 10 million and timeslot rank <= 3
filtered_episodes = df[(df['viewers (m)'] >= 10) & (df['timeslot rank'] <= 3)]

# Calculate average rating
average_rating = filtered_episodes['rating'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_rating:.2f}")
```
