Step 1: The user wants the total number of locomotives introduced between 1867 and 1873, but only for the '2 - 4 - 0' and '0 - 6 - 0' types.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where:
- 'introduced' is between 1867 and 1873 (inclusive).
- 'type' is either '2 - 4 - 0' or '0 - 6 - 0'.

Step 4: Sum the 'total' column for the filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter for years between 1867 and 1873 (inclusive) and specific types
filtered_df = df[
    (df['introduced'].astype(int) >= 1867) &
    (df['introduced'].astype(int) <= 1873) &
    (df['type'].isin(['2 - 4 - 0', '0 - 6 - 0']))
]

# Sum the 'total' column for the filtered rows
total_locomotives = filtered_df['total'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_locomotives}")
```
