Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete won their first gold medal in the 100 m hurdles event.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'Event' is '100 m hurdles' and the 'Position' is '1st'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the first such row (which will be the first gold medal).
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 100 m hurdles and first place
gold_medal_rows = df[(df['Event'] == '100 m hurdles') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Get the first year (earliest) when they won gold
first_gold_year = gold_medal_rows['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {first_gold_year}")
```
