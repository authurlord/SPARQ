Step 1: The user wants the difference in total days of inclement weather (snow, storms, and fog) between the year with the most sunshine and the year with the least sunshine.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Parse the 'sunshine (hrs / year)' column to numeric values (removing spaces and converting to integers).

Step 4: Calculate the total inclement weather days (snow + storms + fog) for each row.

Step 5: Identify the year with the most sunshine and the year with the least sunshine.

Step 6: Find the corresponding inclement weather days for both years.

Step 7: Compute the difference and print.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Clean and convert 'sunshine (hrs / year)' to numeric, removing spaces
df['sunshine (hrs / year)'] = df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].str.replace(' ', '').astype(int)

# Calculate total inclement weather days (snow + storms + fog)
df['inclement_days'] = df['snow (days / year)'].astype(int) + df['storms (days / year)'].astype(int) + df['fog (days / year)'].astype(int)

# Find the year with the most and least sunshine
max_sunshine_idx = df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].idxmax()
min_sunshine_idx = df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].idxmin()

# Get inclement weather days for those years
max_sunshine_inclement = df.loc[max_sunshine_idx, 'inclement_days']
min_sunshine_inclement = df.loc[min_sunshine_idx, 'inclement_days']

# Calculate difference
difference = max_sunshine_inclement - min_sunshine_inclement
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
