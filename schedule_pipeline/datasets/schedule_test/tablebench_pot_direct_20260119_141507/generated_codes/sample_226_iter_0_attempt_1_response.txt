Step 1: The user is asking which factor — live births per year or deaths per year — has a greater influence on the natural change per year.  
Natural change per year is calculated as:  
**Natural change = Live births per year - Deaths per year**  
So, the natural change is directly driven by both live births and deaths, but we need to determine which one contributes more significantly.  

Step 2: We will compute the difference between live births and deaths for each period.  
If live births are much larger than deaths, then live births are the primary driver.  
If deaths are much larger than live births, then deaths are the primary driver.  
However, since natural change is simply the difference, we can analyze whether the live births or deaths have a larger magnitude in contributing to the change.  

But note: since natural change = live births - deaths, the "driver" depends on which term dominates.  
We can check the absolute values of live births and deaths and compare them.  
If live births > deaths, then live births dominate.  
If deaths > live births, then deaths dominate.  

Step 3: Calculate the ratio of live births to deaths per year across all periods.  
If live births consistently exceed deaths and are significantly larger, then live births are the primary driver.  
If deaths are consistently higher or close to live births, then deaths may be the primary driver.  

Step 4: Analyze the data.  
We can compute the average live births and deaths, and compare them.  
If average live births > average deaths, then live births are the primary driver.  
If average deaths > average live births, then deaths are the primary driver.  

Let’s calculate:

- Average live births per year
- Average deaths per year
- Compare the two

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'live births per year' and 'deaths per year' to numeric
df['live births per year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['live births per year'].str.replace(',', ''), errors='coerce')
df['deaths per year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['deaths per year'].str.replace(',', ''), errors='coerce')

avg_live_births = df['live births per year'].mean()
avg_deaths = df['deaths per year'].mean()

if avg_live_births > avg_deaths:
    primary_driver = 'live births per year'
else:
    primary_driver = 'deaths per year'

print(f"Final Answer: {primary_driver}")
```
