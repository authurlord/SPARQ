Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between two numerical columns: 'carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)' and 'avg emission per km² of its land (tons)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the two columns and compute the correlation coefficient using pandas.
Step 4: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate correlation between the two columns
correlation = df['carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)'].corr(df['avg emission per km 2 of its land (tons)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
