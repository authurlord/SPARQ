Step 1: The user wants two values:  
- The median total number of medals won by nations in the top 5 rankings (ranks 1 to 5).  
- The median total number of medals won by all countries (all rows).  

Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'total' column and convert it to integers.  
Step 4: Calculate the median for the top 5 nations (first 5 rows).  
Step 5: Calculate the median for all nations.  
Step 6: Print both medians in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total' column to integer
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'])

# Median for top 5 rankings
top_5_medals = df.head(5)['total'].median()

# Median for all countries
all_medals_median = df['total'].median()

print(f"Final Answer: {top_5_medals}, {all_medals_median}")
```
