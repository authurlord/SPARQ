Step 1: The user wants the total increase in net assets over a 3-year period from 2005/2006 to 2007/2008. This means we need to sum the 'increase in net assets' values for the years 2005/2006, 2006/2007, and 2007/2008.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter the rows corresponding to the years 2005/2006, 2006/2007, and 2007/2008.

Step 4: Sum the 'increase in net assets' column for these rows.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for the specified years
filtered_df = df[df['year'].isin(['2005 / 2006', '2006 / 2007', '2007 / 2008'])]
# Calculate total increase in net assets
total_increase = filtered_df['increase in net assets'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_increase}")
```
