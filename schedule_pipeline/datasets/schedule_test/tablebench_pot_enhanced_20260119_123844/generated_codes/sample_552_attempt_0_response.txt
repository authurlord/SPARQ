Step 1: The user wants the average number of Indians admitted per year from 2000 to 2010.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'indians admitted' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Exclude the 'total' row, calculate the mean for rows 2000 to 2010 (first 12 rows excluding the last total row).
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'indians admitted' to integer
df['indians admitted'] = pd.to_numeric(df['indians admitted'])
# Exclude the 'total' row and calculate mean for years 2000 to 2010
mean_indians = df.loc[0:10, 'indians admitted'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_indians:.1f}")
```
