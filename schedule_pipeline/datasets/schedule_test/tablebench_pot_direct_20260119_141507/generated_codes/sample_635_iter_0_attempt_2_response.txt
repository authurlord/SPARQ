Step 1: Identify intervals with a "just ratio" that involves the number 11.  
Looking at the "just ratio" column, we need to find entries where the ratio includes "11".  
From the data:  
- "11:8 wide fourth" → has 11  
- "undecimal major third" → has 11 (14:11)  
- "undecimal neutral third" → has 11 (11:9)  

Step 2: Extract the "size (cents)" for these intervals.  
Step 3: Compute the average of these values.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where 'just ratio' contains '11'
filtered_df = df[df['just ratio'].str.contains('11', na=False)]
# Extract 'size (cents)' and compute mean
average_size_cents = filtered_df['size (cents)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_size_cents:.2f}")
```
