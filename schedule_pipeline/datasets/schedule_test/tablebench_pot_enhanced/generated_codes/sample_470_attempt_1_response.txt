Step 1: The user wants to identify countries with values that deviate significantly from the norm. To do this, we will analyze the data in the columns '2011 (imf)' and '2008 (cia factbook)' by calculating the mean and standard deviation for each column. Then, we will flag countries where the values are more than 2 standard deviations away from the mean (commonly considered outliers).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Extract the numerical columns '2011 (imf)' and '2008 (cia factbook)' and convert them to numeric type.

Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation for each column.

Step 5: Identify countries where the values in either column deviate significantly (more than 2 standard deviations from the mean).

Step 6: Return the list of such countries.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert the relevant columns to numeric
df['2011 (imf)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['2011 (imf)'], errors='coerce')
df['2008 (cia factbook)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['2008 (cia factbook)'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate mean and std for each column
mean_2011 = df['2011 (imf)'].mean()
std_2011 = df['2011 (imf)'].std()

mean_2008 = df['2008 (cia factbook)'].mean()
std_2008 = df['2008 (cia factbook)'].std()

# Identify outliers (more than 2 standard deviations from the mean)
outliers_2011 = df[abs(df['2011 (imf)'] - mean_2011) > 2 * std_2011]['nation']
outliers_2008 = df[abs(df['2008 (cia factbook)'] - mean_2008) > 2 * std_2008]['nation']

# Combine both sets of outliers
outliers = set(outliers_2011).union(set(outliers_2008))

# Print the final answer
print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(outliers)}")
```
