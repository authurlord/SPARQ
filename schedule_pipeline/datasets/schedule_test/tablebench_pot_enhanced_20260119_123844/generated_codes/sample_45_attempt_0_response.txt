Step 1: The user wants the total number of years that all ambassadors served.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'Start' and 'End' columns to integers.
Step 4: Calculate the difference between 'End' and 'Start' for each row.
Step 5: Sum all the differences to get the total years served.
Step 6: Print the total.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Start' and 'End' to integers
df['Start'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Start'])
df['End'] = pd.to_numeric(df['End'])
# Calculate the years served for each ambassador
df['Years Served'] = df['End'] - df['Start']
# Sum the total years served
total_years = df['Years Served'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_years}")
```
