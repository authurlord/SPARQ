Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand' across the autonomous communities.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the two columns: 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand'.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient using pandas.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand'
correlation = df['total renewable generation'].corr(df['total electricity demand'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
