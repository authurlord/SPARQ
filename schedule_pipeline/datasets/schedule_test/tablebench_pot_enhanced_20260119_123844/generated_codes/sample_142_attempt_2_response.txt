Step 1: The user wants a bar chart comparing the goal differences of the top 5 teams in the league.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the top 5 teams based on their position (first 5 rows).
Step 4: Extract the 'club' and 'goal difference' columns.
Step 5: Use matplotlib to create a bar chart with clubs on the x-axis and goal differences on the y-axis.
Step 6: Display the chart.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Select the top 5 teams
top_5_teams = df.head(5)

# Extract club names and goal differences
clubs = top_5_teams['club']
goal_diff = top_5_teams['goal difference']

# Create a bar chart
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
plt.bar(clubs, goal_diff, color='skyblue')
plt.title('Goal Differences of Top 5 Teams')
plt.xlabel('Club')
plt.ylabel('Goal Difference')
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()
```
