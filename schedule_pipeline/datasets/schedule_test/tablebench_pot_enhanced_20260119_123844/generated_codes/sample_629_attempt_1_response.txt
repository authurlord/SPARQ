Step 1: The user wants the total sales of the top 5 albums, which are ranked 1 to 5 in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'sales' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Sum the sales of the first 5 rows (top 5 albums).
Step 5: Print the total sales.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'sales' column to integer
df['sales'] = df['sales'].astype(int)
# Sum the sales of the top 5 albums
total_sales = df['sales'].head(5).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_sales}")
```
