Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in 'property taxes' from 2000 to 2005.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'property taxes' column and convert it to numeric.  
Step 4: Calculate the difference between the values in 2005 and 2000, then divide by the number of years (5) to get the average annual increase.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'property taxes' to numeric
df['property taxes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['property taxes'])
# Get the property taxes for 2000 and 2005
property_taxes_2000 = df[df['year'] == '2000']['property taxes'].values[0]
property_taxes_2005 = df[df['year'] == '2005']['property taxes'].values[0]
# Calculate average annual increase
annual_increase = (property_taxes_2005 - property_taxes_2000) / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {annual_increase:.0f}")
```
