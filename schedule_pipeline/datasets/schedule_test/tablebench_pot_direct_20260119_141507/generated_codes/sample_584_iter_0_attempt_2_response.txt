Step 1: The user wants the average issue price of coins released between 2002 and 2005.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2002 and 2005 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Extract the 'issue price' column from the filtered data.  
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the 'issue price' values and print it.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years between 2002 and 2005 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2002) & (df['year'] <= 2005)]
# Calculate the mean of 'issue price' column
average_issue_price = filtered_df['issue price'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_issue_price:.2f}")
```
