Step 1: The user wants to understand the relationship between maximum pressure (p max) and projectile diameter (p1 diameter).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'p1 diameter (mm)' and 'p max ( bar )' columns.  
Step 4: Sort the data by projectile diameter in ascending order to observe the trend.  
Step 5: Print the sorted data to analyze the change in p max with increasing p1 diameter.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by projectile diameter to observe the trend
sorted_df = df.sort_values(by='p1 diameter (mm)')
print(sorted_df[['p1 diameter (mm)', 'p max ( bar )']])
```
