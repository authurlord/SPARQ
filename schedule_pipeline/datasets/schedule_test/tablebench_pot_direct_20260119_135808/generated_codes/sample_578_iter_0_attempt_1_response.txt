Step 1: The user wants the total increase in net assets over the 3-year period from 2005/2006 to 2007/2008.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows for the years 2005/2006, 2006/2007, and 2007/2008.  
Step 4: Sum the 'increase in net assets' column for these years.  
Step 5: Print the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the data for the specified years
filtered_df = df[df['year'].isin(['2005 / 2006', '2006 / 2007', '2007 / 2008'])]
# Sum the increase in net assets
total_increase = filtered_df['increase in net assets'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_increase}")
```
