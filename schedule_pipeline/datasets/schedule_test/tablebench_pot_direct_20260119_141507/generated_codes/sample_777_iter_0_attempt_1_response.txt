Step 1: The user wants to understand the trend of total revenue over the years. This involves analyzing how total revenue changes from year to year.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'year' and 'total revenue' columns.  
Step 4: Sort by year to ensure chronological order.  
Step 5: Calculate the difference in total revenue between consecutive years to identify trends (increasing, decreasing, or stable).  
Step 6: Print a summary of the trend (e.g., increasing, decreasing, fluctuating).  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by year to ensure chronological order
df = df.sort_values(by='year')
# Calculate the change in total revenue from one year to the next
df['revenue_change'] = df['total revenue'].diff()

# Determine the trend based on the sign of the changes
trend = "increasing" if df['revenue_change'].gt(0).all() else "decreasing" if df['revenue_change'].lt(0).all() else "fluctuating"
print(f"Final Answer: {trend}")
```
