Step 1: The user wants to identify parties with at least 2 seats in the agricultural panel, sum their total seats, and calculate the percentage of the total seats (60).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where 'agricultural panel' >= 2.

Step 4: Sum the 'total' column for these parties.

Step 5: Calculate the percentage of total seats (60).

Step 6: Print both the sum and percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter parties with at least 2 seats in the agricultural panel
parties_with_agricultural_seats = df[df['agricultural panel'] >= 2]
# Sum their total seats
total_seats = parties_with_agricultural_seats['total'].sum()
# Calculate percentage
percentage = (total_seats / 60) * 100
print(f"Final Answer: {total_seats}, {percentage:.1f}")
```
