Step 1: The user wants the average points scored by songs that:
- Have a non-English language title
- Have points greater than the median points value of all songs

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Calculate the median of the 'points' column.

Step 4: Filter songs where:
- 'language' is not 'english'
- 'points' > median points

Step 5: Compute the mean of the filtered 'points' values.

Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate median points
median_points = df['points'].median()

# Filter songs with non-English language and points > median
filtered_songs = df[(df['language'] != 'english') & (df['points'] > median_points)]

# Calculate average points for filtered songs
avg_points = filtered_songs['points'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_points:.1f}")
```
