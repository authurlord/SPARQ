Step 1: The user is asking about the impact of urbanization on population growth rate between 1939 and 2008. To analyze this, we need to:
- Calculate the total population growth rate from 1939 to 2008.
- Observe the trend in urban percentage over time.
- Correlate population growth with urbanization (increase in urban %).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the population (in thousands) for 1939 and 2008.
Step 4: Compute the growth rate: ((Population_2008 - Population_1939) / Population_1939) * 100.
Step 5: Observe the urban percentage change from 1939 (28%) to 2008 (53%).
Step 6: Conclude that urbanization increased significantly during this period, and population grew despite a slight decline after 1999.

Step 7: Since the question asks for the impact, we interpret it as the overall population growth rate and the urbanization trend. The final answer will be the population growth rate as a percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract population in 1939 and 2008
pop_1939 = df[df['year (january)'] == '1939']['population (000)'].values[0]
pop_2008 = df[df['year (january)'] == '2008']['population (000)'].values[0]

# Calculate growth rate
growth_rate = ((int(pop_2008) - int(pop_1939)) / int(pop_1939)) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {growth_rate:.1f}")
```
