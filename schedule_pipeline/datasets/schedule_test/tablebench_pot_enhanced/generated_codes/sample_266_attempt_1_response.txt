Step 1: The user wants the total number of medals (sum of M36, M36B1, and M36B2) from May 1944 to August 1944.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows for the months May 1944 to August 1944.
Step 4: Convert the medal columns to integers and sum them across the filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the total.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for May 1944 to August 1944
filtered_df = df[(df['Month'] >= 'May 1944') & (df['Month'] <= 'August 1944')]
# Convert medal columns to integers and calculate total
total_medals = filtered_df[['M36', 'M36B1', 'M36B2']].astype(int).sum().sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_medals}")
```
