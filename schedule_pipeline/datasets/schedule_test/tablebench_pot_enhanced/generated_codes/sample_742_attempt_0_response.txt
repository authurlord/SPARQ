Step 1: The user wants a forecast of the percentage of popular vote in the next election year based on historical data from 1963 to 2011. Since no specific forecasting model is mentioned, we will use a simple linear regression to model the trend in the "% of popular vote" over time (Year of election) and predict the next year's value.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the 'Year of election' and '% of popular vote' columns.

Step 3: Clean the '% of popular vote' column by removing the '%' sign and converting it to a float.

Step 4: Perform linear regression using the year as the independent variable and the percentage as the dependent variable.

Step 5: Predict the percentage for the next election year (2015, assuming the pattern continues every 4 years).

Step 6: Output the forecasted percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np
from sklearn.linear_model import LinearRegression

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'Year of election' to integer and '% of popular vote' to float
df['Year of election'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Year of election'])
df['% of popular vote'] = df['% of popular vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)

# Prepare data for regression
X = df['Year of election'].values.reshape(-1, 1)
y = df['% of popular vote'].values

# Fit linear regression model
model = LinearRegression()
model.fit(X, y)

# Predict for the next election year (assuming next election is 2015)
next_year = 2015
predicted_vote = model.predict([[next_year]])[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {predicted_vote:.2f}")
```
