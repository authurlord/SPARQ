Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete won their first gold medal in the 100 m hurdles event.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the event is '100 m hurdles' and the position is '1st'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' of the first occurrence (earliest year) and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 100 m hurdles and 1st position
gold_medals = df[(df['Event'] == '100 m hurdles') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Get the earliest year
first_gold_year = gold_medals['Year'].min()
print(f"Final Answer: {first_gold_year}")
```
