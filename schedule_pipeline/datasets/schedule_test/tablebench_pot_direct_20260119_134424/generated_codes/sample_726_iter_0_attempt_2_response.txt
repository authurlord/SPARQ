Step 1: The user wants to find the beam type with the highest moment of inertia in torsion (j).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Identify the column 'moment of inertia in torsion (j) (cm 4 )' and find the maximum value.
Step 4: Retrieve the corresponding 'type' for that maximum value.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the row with the maximum value in 'moment of inertia in torsion (j) (cm 4 )'
max_j_row = df.loc[df['moment of inertia in torsion (j) (cm 4 )'].idxmax()]
beam_type = max_j_row['type']
print(f"Final Answer: {beam_type}")
```
