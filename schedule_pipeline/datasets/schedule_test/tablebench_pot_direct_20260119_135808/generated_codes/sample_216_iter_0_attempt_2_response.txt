Step 1: The user wants to determine if the wife's age at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has. To analyze this, we can compute the correlation coefficient between 'her age' and 'of children' to assess the strength and direction of the relationship.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Convert 'her age' and 'of children' columns to numeric type (in case they are not already).

Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between 'her age' and 'of children'.

Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['her age'] = pd.to_numeric(df['her age'])
df['of children'] = pd.to_numeric(df['of children'])

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['her age'].corr(df['of children'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
