Step 1: The user wants to compare the total energy production of the European Union and the United States across different years to find when the EU's total exceeded that of the United States.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows for 'european union' and 'united states'.
Step 4: Compare the 'total' values for each year and identify the year when EU's total exceeded US's total.
Step 5: Return the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for European Union and United States
eu_data = df[df['country'] == 'european union']
us_data = df[df['country'] == 'united states']

# Extract the total energy production and year for both
eu_total = eu_data['total'].values[0]
us_total = us_data['total'].values[0]

# Compare totals
if eu_total > us_total:
    year = eu_data['year'].values[0]
else:
    year = None

print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
