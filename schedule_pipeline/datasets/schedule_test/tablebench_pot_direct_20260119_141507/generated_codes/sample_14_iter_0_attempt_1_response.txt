Step 1: The user wants the total elevation of all mountains, which corresponds to the sum of the 'elevation (m)' column.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'elevation (m)' column and compute the sum.  
Step 4: Print the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sum the 'elevation (m)' column to get total elevation
total_elevation = df['elevation (m)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_elevation}")
```
