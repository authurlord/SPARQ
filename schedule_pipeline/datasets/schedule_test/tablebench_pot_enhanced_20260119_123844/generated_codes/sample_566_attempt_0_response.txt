Step 1: The user wants the total quantity of orders placed in the earliest two years (1893 and 1894).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'Year' is either 1893 or 1894.
Step 4: Sum the 'Quantity' column for these filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for the earliest two years: 1893 and 1894
earliest_years = df[df['Year'].isin(['1893', '1894'])]
# Calculate total quantity
total_quantity = earliest_years['Quantity'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_quantity}")
```
