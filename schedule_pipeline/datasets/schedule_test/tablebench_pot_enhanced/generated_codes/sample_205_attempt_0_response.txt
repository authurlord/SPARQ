Step 1: The question asks to determine whether temperature (T) or density of air (ρ) has a greater causal influence on the speed of sound (c). Since this is a physical relationship, we can analyze the correlation between c and T, and c and ρ.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Calculate the correlation coefficient between 'Speed of sound c (m/s)' and 'Temperature T (°C)', and between 'Speed of sound c (m/s)' and 'Density of air ρ (kg/m³)'.
Step 4: Compare the absolute values of the correlations. The higher absolute correlation indicates stronger influence.
Step 5: Print the result based on the comparison.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['Temperature T (°C)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficients
corr_T = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].corr(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
corr_rho = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].corr(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Determine which has greater influence
if abs(corr_T) > abs(corr_rho):
    influence = 'Temperature T (°C)'
else:
    influence = 'Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'

print(f"Final Answer: {influence}")
```
