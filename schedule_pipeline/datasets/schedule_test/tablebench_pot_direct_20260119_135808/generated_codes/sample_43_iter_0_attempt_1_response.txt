Step 1: The user wants the total commissioned capacity of power plants in Himachal Pradesh.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'state' is 'himachal pradesh'.
Step 4: Convert 'commissioned capacity (mw)' to numeric and sum it.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Himachal Pradesh
himachal_plants = df[df['state'] == 'himachal pradesh']
# Convert capacity to numeric and sum
total_capacity = himachal_plants['commissioned capacity (mw)'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_capacity}")
```
