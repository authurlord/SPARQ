Step 1: The question asks for an estimation of viewership and 18-49 average rating for season 9 based on the trend from season 1 to season 8.  
Step 2: We observe that both "viewers (in millions)" and "18 - 49 average" are decreasing over time, suggesting a clear downward trend.  
Step 3: To estimate season 9, we can calculate the average change per season in viewership and 18-49 ratings and apply it to season 8.  
Step 4: Since the data is small (only 8 seasons), we will compute the linear trend using simple arithmetic mean of differences.  

We will:
- Extract the viewership and 18-49 average values from season 1 to season 8.
- Compute the average decrease per season.
- Project season 9 by subtracting the average decrease from season 8's values.

Note: This is a simple linear projection and assumes a constant rate of decline.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract viewership and 18-49 average ratings from season 1 to 8
viewership = df['viewers (in millions)'].head(8).values
avg_18_49 = df['18 - 49 average'].head(8).values

# Calculate average decrease per season
avg_decrease_viewership = (viewership[0] - viewership[-1]) / 7
avg_decrease_18_49 = (avg_18_49[0] - avg_18_49[-1]) / 7

# Project season 9 values
projected_viewership = viewership[-1] - avg_decrease_viewership
projected_avg_18_49 = avg_18_49[-1] - avg_decrease_18_49

print(f"Final Answer: {projected_viewership:.2f}, {projected_avg_18_49:.2f}")
```
