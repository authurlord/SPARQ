Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between the area (km²) and population of municipalities.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'area (km 2 )' and 'population' columns to numeric type.
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'area (km 2 )' and 'population' to numeric
df['area (km 2 )'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km 2 )'], errors='coerce')
df['population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km 2 )'].corr(df['population'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
