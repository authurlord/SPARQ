Step 1: Understand the question. We are to find the minimum percentage increase in the Manchu population of the "north" region such that it surpasses the Manchu population of the "liaoning" region.  
- The Manchu population in "liaoning" is 5,336,895 (from the table).  
- The Manchu population in "north" is 3,002,873.  
- We need to compute the percentage increase required for "north" to exceed 5,336,895.

Step 2: Formula for percentage increase:  
$$
\text{Percentage Increase} = \left( \frac{\text{Target} - \text{Current}}{\text{Current}} \right) \times 100
$$  
Where:
- Target = Manchu population in Liaoning = 5,336,895  
- Current = Manchu population in North = 3,002,873  

Step 3: Plug in the values and compute.

```python
# Manchu population in "liaoning"
manchu_liaoning = 5336895

# Manchu population in "north"
manchu_north = 3002873

# Calculate percentage increase needed to surpass liaoning's manchu population
percentage_increase = ((manchu_liaoning - manchu_north) / manchu_north) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {percentage_increase:.2f}")
```
