Step 1: The user wants to forecast the issue price for 2008 based on the pattern from 2002 to 2007.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Observe the trend in issue prices over the years.  
Step 4: Since the issue price increased from $24.95 (2002) to $38.95 (2007), and the increase is consistent (with a jump of $10.00 over 5 years), we can assume a linear trend.  
Step 5: Calculate the annual increase and project it to 2008.  
Step 6: Print the forecasted issue price.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'issue price' to float for calculation
df['issue price'] = df['issue price'].astype(float)

# Calculate the annual increase in issue price
years = df['year'].astype(int)
prices = df['issue price']
annual_increase = (prices.iloc[-1] - prices.iloc[0]) / (years.iloc[-1] - years.iloc[0])

# Forecast for 2008
forecasted_price = prices.iloc[-1] + annual_increase
print(f"Final Answer: {forecasted_price:.2f}")
```
