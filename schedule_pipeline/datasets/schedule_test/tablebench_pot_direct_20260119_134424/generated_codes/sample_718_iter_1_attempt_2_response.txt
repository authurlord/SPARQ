Step 1: The user wants to identify the university with the highest research funding and calculate the difference between the highest and lowest research funding (in thousands).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'research funding (000)' column to numeric to allow arithmetic operations.
Step 4: Find the maximum and minimum values in the 'research funding (000)' column.
Step 5: Identify the corresponding university for the maximum funding.
Step 6: Calculate the difference and print both results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'research funding (000)' to numeric for calculations
df['research funding (000)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['research funding (000)'])
# Find the university with the highest research funding
max_funding_uni = df.loc[df['research funding (000)'].idxmax(), 'institution']
# Get the highest and lowest funding values
max_funding = df['research funding (000)'].max()
min_funding = df['research funding (000)'].min()
# Calculate the difference
difference = max_funding - min_funding
print(f"Final Answer: {max_funding_uni}, {difference}")
```
