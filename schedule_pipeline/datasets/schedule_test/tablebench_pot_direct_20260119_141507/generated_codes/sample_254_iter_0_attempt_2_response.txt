Step 1: The user wants the average elevation of districts in the Cusco region where elevation is above 4700 meters.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'region' is 'cusco' and 'elevation (m)' > 4700.  
Step 4: Compute the mean of 'elevation (m)' for the filtered rows.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter districts in Cusco with elevation above 4700 meters
filtered_df = df[(df['region'] == 'cusco') & (df['elevation (m)'] > 4700)]
# Calculate average elevation
average_elevation = filtered_df['elevation (m)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_elevation:.1f}")
```
