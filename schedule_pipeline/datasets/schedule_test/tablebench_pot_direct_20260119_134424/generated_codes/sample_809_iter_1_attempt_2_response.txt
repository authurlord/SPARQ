Step 1: The user wants the mean height of buildings in Winnipeg that have more than 10 floors.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'floors' column to integer type to allow numerical comparison.
Step 4: Filter buildings with more than 10 floors.
Step 5: Calculate the mean height of these buildings.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'floors' column to integer for numerical comparison
df['floors'] = pd.to_numeric(df['floors'], errors='coerce')
# Filter buildings with more than 10 floors
filtered_buildings = df[df['floors'] > 10]
# Calculate mean height of filtered buildings
mean_height = filtered_buildings['height'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_height:.1f}")
```
