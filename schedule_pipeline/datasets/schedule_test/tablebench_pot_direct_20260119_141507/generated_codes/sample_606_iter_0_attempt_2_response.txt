Step 1: Identify the student populations of Karolinska Institutet and Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences from the table.  
Step 2: Calculate 18% increase for each institution's student population.  
Step 3: Sum the increased populations to get the new combined student population.  
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract student populations for Karolinska Institutet and Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences
karolinska_population = df[df['university'] == 'karolinska institutet']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]
swedish_agri_population = df[df['university'] == 'swedish university of agricultural sciences']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]

# Increase each by 18%
increased_karolinska = karolinska_population * 1.18
increased_swedish_agri = swedish_agri_population * 1.18

# Calculate new combined student population
new_combined_population = increased_karolinska + increased_swedish_agri

print(f"Final Answer: {new_combined_population:.0f}")
```
