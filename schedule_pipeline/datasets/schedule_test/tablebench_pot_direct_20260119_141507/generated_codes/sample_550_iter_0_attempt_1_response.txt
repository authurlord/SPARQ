Step 1: The user wants to find the first year when the 'Political Rights' score decreased by at least 2 points compared to the previous year.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the 'Political Rights' column.  
Step 3: Iterate through the years in order, comparing each year's 'Political Rights' score with the previous year's.  
Step 4: Find the first instance where the difference is at least -2 (i.e., a decrease of 2 or more points).  

Note: We need to ensure we compare only consecutive years and check for a drop of ≥2 points.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Year' to integer and sort by Year
df['Year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Year'], errors='coerce')
df = df.sort_values('Year').reset_index(drop=True)

# Extract Political Rights scores
political_rights = df['Political Rights'].astype(int)

# Compare each year with the previous one
for i in range(1, len(political_rights)):
    if political_rights[i] - political_rights[i-1] <= -2:
        answer_year = df.iloc[i]['Year']
        break

print(f"Final Answer: {answer_year}")
```
