Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between 'Speed of sound c (m/s)' and 'Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the two columns of interest.
Step 4: Compute the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate correlation between 'Speed of sound c (m/s)' and 'Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'
correlation = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].astype(float).corr(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'].astype(float))
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
