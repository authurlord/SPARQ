Step 1: Identify the total passengers at Glasgow International Airport from the table.  
Step 2: Calculate a 15% increase on that value.  
Step 3: Output the result.

From the table, Glasgow International has "total passengers" = 8,795,727.

15% of 8,795,727 = 0.15 × 8,795,727 = 1,319,359.05  
Total passengers in 2008 = 8,795,727 + 1,319,359.05 = 10,115,086.05 ≈ 10,115,086

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract total passengers for Glasgow International
glasgow_passengers = df[df['airport'] == 'glasgow international']['total passengers'].values[0]
# Calculate 15% increase
new_passengers = glasgow_passengers * 1.15
print(f"Final Answer: {int(new_passengers)}
```
