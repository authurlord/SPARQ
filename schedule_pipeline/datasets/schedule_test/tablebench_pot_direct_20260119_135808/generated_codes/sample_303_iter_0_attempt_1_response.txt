Step 1: The user is asking for the competition where the athlete won 1st place in the 3000 m steeplechase event, and the venue must be a city that is also the capital of the Bahamas.

Step 2: From the table, identify the row where:
- The event is "3000 m steeplechase"
- The position is "1st"
- The venue is "Nassau, Bahamas"

Step 3: Confirm that Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas (known fact, and matches the data).

Step 4: Extract the competition name from that row.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter the row where event is '3000 m steeplechase', position is '1st', and venue is 'Nassau, Bahamas'
row = df[(df['Event'] == '3000 m steeplechase') & (df['Position'] == '1st') & (df['Venue'] == 'Nassau, Bahamas')]

# Extract the competition name
competition = row['Competition'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {competition}")
```
