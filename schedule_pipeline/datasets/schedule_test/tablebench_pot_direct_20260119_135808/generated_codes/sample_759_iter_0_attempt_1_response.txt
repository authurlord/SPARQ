Step 1: The user wants a projection of Tabriz's population in 2026 based on historical growth from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 2: Extract the population data for Tabriz (row 4) across the years 1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, and 2006.  
Step 3: Calculate the average annual growth rate between 1956 and 2006.  
Step 4: Use this growth rate to project the population for 2026 (20 years after 2006).  
Step 5: Use the formula: projected population = current population × (1 + growth rate)^number of years.  
Step 6: Print the projected population as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract Tabriz's population data
tabriz_data = df[df['city'] == 'tabriz']
populations = [int(tabriz_data.iloc[0][f'{year}']) for year in [1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, 2006]]

# Calculate average annual growth rate
initial_pop = populations[0]
final_pop = populations[-1]
years = len(populations) - 1  # 5 decades = 50 years
growth_rate = (final_pop / initial_pop) ** (1 / years) - 1

# Project population for 2026 (20 years after 2006)
projected_pop_2026 = final_pop * (1 + growth_rate) ** 20

print(f"Final Answer: {projected_pop_2026:.0f}")
```
