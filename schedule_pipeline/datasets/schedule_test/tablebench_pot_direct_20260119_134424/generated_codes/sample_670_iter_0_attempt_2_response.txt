Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between 'total freshwater withdrawal' and 'per capita withdrawal'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the relevant columns to numeric type to ensure proper calculation.
Step 4: Use pandas .corr() method to compute the correlation coefficient.
Step 5: Print the result as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric for calculation
df['total freshwater withdrawal'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total freshwater withdrawal'], errors='coerce')
df['per capita withdrawal'] = pd.to_numeric(df['per capita withdrawal'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['total freshwater withdrawal'].corr(df['per capita withdrawal'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
