Step 1: Understand the question. The urbanization rate is given in the "urban , %" column. We need to find the first year when this value surpassed 50%.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the "urban , %" column.  
Step 3: Iterate through the years in order and check when the urbanization rate exceeds 50%.  
Step 4: Return the first such year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert urban , % to numeric and find the first year where it exceeds 50%
df['urban , %'] = pd.to_numeric(df['urban , %'], errors='coerce')
for index, row in df.iterrows():
    if row['urban , %'] > 50:
        print(f"Final Answer: {row['year (january)']}")
        break
```
