Step 1: The question asks about the impact of urbanization on population growth rate. However, the data provided does not include a "population growth rate" column or any direct measure of growth rate. Instead, we have population (in thousands), rural/urban percentages, and years.

Step 2: We can infer population growth by computing the change in population from year to year. Urbanization is indicated by the "urban, %" column.

Step 3: To assess the impact of urbanization, we can compute the population growth rate between years and correlate it with the urbanization percentage (urban, %). However, since there's no explicit growth rate column, we calculate the year-over-year population change and then examine if there's a trend in urbanization alongside population growth.

Step 4: Since the question is about "impact", a simple correlation between urbanization percentage and population growth would be meaningful. We will:
- Compute population growth rate (percentage change) from one year to the next.
- Calculate the correlation between this growth rate and the urbanization percentage.

Note: The data starts in 1939 and ends in 2008. We'll compute growth rates for each consecutive year pair.

Step 5: Perform the analysis using pandas.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'population (000)' to numeric and ensure it's in the correct format
df['population (000)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population (000)'], errors='coerce')

# Sort by year (January)
df = df.sort_values('year (january)').reset_index(drop=True)

# Calculate year-over-year population growth rate (as percentage)
df['growth_rate'] = df['population (000)'].pct_change() * 100

# Drop the first row (no prior year)
df = df.dropna(subset=['growth_rate'])

# Extract urbanization percentage and growth rate
urbanization = df['urban , %']
growth_rate = df['growth_rate']

# Compute correlation between urbanization and population growth rate
correlation = urbanization.corr(growth_rate)

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
