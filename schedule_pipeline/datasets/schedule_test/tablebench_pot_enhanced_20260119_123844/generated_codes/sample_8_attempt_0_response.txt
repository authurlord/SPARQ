Step 1: The user wants the average percentage of national votes won by all leaders.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'of national votes' column and convert it to a numeric format after removing the '%' sign.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of this column and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Remove the '%' sign and convert to float
df['% of national vote'] = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate the average
average_national_vote = df['% of national vote'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_national_vote:.2f}")
```
