Step 1: The user wants the average number of episodes per season for seasons that meet two conditions:
- At least 40 episodes.
- Premiered between October and December.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter rows based on:
- "Episodes" >= 40
- "Season Premiere" is between October and December (inclusive).

Step 3: Calculate the mean of the "Episodes" column for the filtered rows.

Step 4: Print the result.

Note: From the data, only Season 2 (52 episodes, premiered October 7, 2006) and Season 5 (40 episodes, premiered October 12, 2009) meet the episode condition. Season 2 premiered in October, which is within October–December. Season 5 also premiered in October. Season 6 has 20 episodes (less than 40), so excluded. Season 7 has 8 episodes, excluded.

So only Seasons 2 and 5 qualify.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where episodes >= 40 and premiere date is between October and December
filtered_df = df[
    (df['Episodes'] >= 40) &
    (df['Season Premiere'].str.contains('October|November|December', case=False))
]

# Calculate average episodes for the filtered seasons
average_episodes = filtered_df['Episodes'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_episodes:.1f}")
```
