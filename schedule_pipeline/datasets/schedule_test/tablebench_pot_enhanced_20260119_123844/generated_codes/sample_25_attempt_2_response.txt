Step 1: The user wants the average percentage of males across all states.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'males (%)' column, convert it to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of this column and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'males (%)' column to numeric and calculate mean
mean_males = df['males (%)'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_males:.1f}")
```
