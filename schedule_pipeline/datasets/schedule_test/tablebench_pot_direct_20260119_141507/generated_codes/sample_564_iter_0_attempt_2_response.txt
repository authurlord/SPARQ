Step 1: The user wants the average number of performances per year between 2004 and 2013.  
Step 2: Filter the data for years from 2004 to 2013 inclusive.  
Step 3: Extract the "No. of performances" column for the filtered rows.  
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the filtered "No. of performances" values.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years between 2004 and 2013 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['Year'].astype(int) >= 2004) & (df['Year'].astype(int) <= 2013)]
# Calculate the mean of 'No. of performances'
average_performances = filtered_df['No. of performances'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_performances:.1f}")
```
