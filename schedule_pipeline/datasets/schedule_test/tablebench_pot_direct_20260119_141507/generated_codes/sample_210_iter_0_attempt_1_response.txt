Step 1: The question asks about the relationship between the prominence and elevation of mountains in Papua New Guinea. We need to analyze whether there is a trend (e.g., positive correlation) between these two variables.

Step 2: Load the data into a DataFrame.

Step 3: Extract the 'elevation (m)' and 'prominence (m)' columns.

Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between elevation and prominence to determine if there's a positive or negative relationship.

Step 5: Interpret the result — a positive correlation suggests that as elevation increases, prominence tends to increase as well.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract elevation and prominence columns
elevation = df['elevation (m)'].astype(float)
prominence = df['prominence (m)'].astype(float)

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = elevation.corr(prominence)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
