Step 1: Understand the structure of the table. The table contains information about drivers in a racing competition, including their performance metrics.
Step 2: Identify and describe each column:
- 'position': The ranking of the driver in the standings.
- 'driver': The name of the driver.
- 'points': Total points accumulated by the driver.
- 'starts': Number of races the driver has participated in.
- 'wins': Number of race victories.
- 'top 5s': Number of times the driver finished in the top 5.
- 'top 10s': Number of times the driver finished in the top 10.
- 'winnings': Total monetary earnings from race winnings.
Step 3: Observe initial patterns:
- All drivers have participated in exactly 13 races ('starts' is constant).
- Points and winnings decrease as position drops.
- Andrew Ranger leads with the most points and highest winnings.
- Drivers with more wins and top finishes tend to have higher points and earnings.
