Step 1: The user wants the total viewers (in millions) for episodes that meet two conditions:
- Rating/share (1849) is 3.0 or higher (i.e., '3.0 / 9' or higher).
- Rank (timeslot) is 1.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where:
- 'rating / share (1849)' starts with a number ≥ 3.0 (we'll split by '/' and compare the first part).
- 'rank (timeslot)' is '1'.

Step 4: Sum the 'viewers (millions)' column for filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'rating / share (1849)' to float by taking the first part before '/'
df['rating_share_1849'] = df['rating / share (1849)'].str.split('/').str[0].astype(float)

# Filter rows where rating/share >= 3.0 and rank (timeslot) is 1
filtered_df = df[(df['rating_share_1849'] >= 3.0) & (df['rank (timeslot)'] == '1')]

# Sum viewers (millions)
total_viewers = filtered_df['viewers (millions)'].sum()

print(f"Final Answer: {total_viewers:.2f}")
```
