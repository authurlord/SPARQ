Step 1: The user wants two values:  
- The median total number of medals for nations in the top 5 rankings (ranks 1 to 5).  
- The median total number of medals for all countries listed.  

Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'total' column for all nations and compute the median for the top 5.  
Step 4: Compute the overall median for all nations.  
Step 5: Print both results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 ranked nations
top_5_medals = df['total'].head(5)
# Calculate median for top 5
median_top_5 = top_5_medals.median()

# Calculate median for all nations
median_all = df['total'].median()

print(f"Final Answer: {median_top_5}, {median_all}")
```
