The error occurred because the "total points" column contains string values that were not properly converted to numeric. We need to ensure that the column is converted to numeric before performing calculations.

Step 1: Identify couples who have performed more than 10 dances.  
Step 2: Filter the dataframe where "number of dances" > 10.  
Step 3: Convert "total points" to numeric (to avoid string conversion errors).  
Step 4: Calculate the average of "total points" for the filtered couples.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total points' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['total points'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total points'], errors='coerce')

# Filter couples with more than 10 dances
filtered_df = df[df['number of dances'] > 10]

# Calculate average total points for these couples
average_points = filtered_df['total points'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_points:.1f}")
```
