Step 1: The user wants the total number of locomotives at Doncaster and Pyewipe combined for the 'j17' and 'e4' classes.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'class' is either 'j17' or 'e4'.
Step 4: Sum the values in the 'number at doncaster' and 'number at pyewipe' columns for these filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the total.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for 'j17' and 'e4' classes
filtered_df = df[df['class'].isin(['j17', 'e4'])]
# Calculate total locomotives at Doncaster and Pyewipe
total_locomotives = filtered_df['number at doncaster'].sum() + filtered_df['number at pyewipe'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_locomotives}")
```
