Step 1: Identify the relevant columns: 'venue', 'crowd', and 'margin'. We are looking for matches where the venue is "waverley park" and crowd size is above 40,000.  
Step 2: Filter the data to include only rows where venue is "waverley park" and crowd > 40000.  
Step 3: Among those rows, find the team with the highest margin (since the margin is already given in the table).  
Step 4: The "winners" column represents the team that won the match in that year.  

Note: The question asks for the team that has the highest score margin under the given conditions. So we extract the "winners" of such matches and find the one with the maximum margin.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where venue is Waverley Park and crowd > 40000
filtered_df = df[(df['venue'] == 'waverley park') & (df['crowd'] > 40000)]
# Find the team (winners) with the highest margin
max_margin_row = filtered_df.loc[filtered_df['margin'].idxmax()]
final_team = max_margin_row['winners']
print(f"Final Answer: {final_team}")
```
