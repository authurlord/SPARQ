Step 1: Identify the relevant rows where the 'type' is either '2 - 4 - 0' or '0 - 6 - 0' and the 'introduced' year is between 1867 and 1873 (inclusive).  
Step 2: Extract the 'total' column values for these rows.  
Step 3: Sum the total number of locomotives.  

Note: The 'introduced' column contains strings, so we need to convert them to integers for comparison.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter rows where type is '2 - 4 - 0' or '0 - 6 - 0' and introduced between 1867 and 1873
filtered_df = df[
    (df['type'].str.contains('2 - 4 - 0') | df['type'].str.contains('0 - 6 - 0')) &
    (df['introduced'].astype(int) >= 1867) &
    (df['introduced'].astype(int) <= 1873)
]

# Sum the 'total' column of filtered rows
total_locomotives = filtered_df['total'].sum()

print(f"Final Answer: {total_locomotives}")
```
