Step 1: The user wants the difference in population between the 6th ranked city (valenza) and the city 'acqui terme'.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter the row where 'rank' is '6th' to get the population of valenza.  
Step 4: Filter the row where 'city' is 'acqui terme' to get its population.  
Step 5: Calculate the absolute difference between the two populations.  
Step 6: Print the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Get population of 6th ranked city (valenza)
pop_valenza = df[df['rank'] == '6th']['population'].values[0]
# Get population of acqui terme
pop_acqui = df[df['city'] == 'acqui terme']['population'].values[0]
# Calculate difference
difference = abs(int(pop_valenza) - int(pop_acqui))
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
