Step 1: The user wants the average issue price of coins released between 2002 and 2005 (inclusive).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'year' is between 2002 and 2005.  
Step 4: Convert 'issue price' column to float for numerical calculation.  
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the filtered 'issue price' values.  
Step 6: Print the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years between 2002 and 2005
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].astype(int) >= 2002) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2005)]
# Convert 'issue price' to float and calculate mean
average_price = filtered_df['issue price'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_price:.2f}")
```
