Step 1: The user wants to determine if the wife's age at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract 'her age' and 'of children' columns.  
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between the two variables to assess the relationship.  
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'her age' and 'of children' to numeric
df['her age'] = pd.to_numeric(df['her age'])
df['of children'] = pd.to_numeric(df['of children'])

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['her age'].corr(df['of children'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
