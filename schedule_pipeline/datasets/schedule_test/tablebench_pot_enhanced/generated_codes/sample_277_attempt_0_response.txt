Step 1: The user wants the average points scored by songs that meet two conditions:
- The song's language is not English.
- The song's points are greater than the median points value of all songs.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Calculate the median of the 'points' column.
Step 4: Filter rows where 'language' is not 'english' and 'points' > median.
Step 5: Compute the mean of the 'points' column for the filtered rows.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate median points
median_points = df['points'].median()

# Filter songs with non-English language and points > median
filtered_df = df[(df['language'] != 'english') & (df['points'] > median_points)]

# Calculate average points for filtered songs
average_points = filtered_df['points'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_points:.1f}")
```
