Step 1: The user wants the variance of goals scored by teams in the Third Division North.  
Step 2: Identify rows where the "Division" is "Third Division North".  
Step 3: Extract the "Goals" column for those rows.  
Step 4: Calculate the variance of the "Goals" values.  
Step 5: Print the result.

Note: The "Goals" column appears in multiple forms (e.g., "Goals", "Goals_1"), but based on context, the relevant column is "Goals" (the one under the "Goals" header). However, looking at the data, the "Goals" column is only present in the first row as a header and then appears in subsequent rows with the correct structure. For "Third Division North" teams, we have specific entries for Lincoln City and Oldham Athletic. We need to extract only the "Goals" values from these rows.

Looking at the data:
- Lincoln City (1946–47): Goals = 15
- Lincoln City (1947–48): Goals = 32
- Lincoln City (1948–49): Goals = 8
- Oldham Athletic (1948–49): Goals = 3
- Oldham Athletic (1949–50): Goals = 0

We will extract these goals and compute the variance.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Division is 'Third Division North'
third_div_north = df[df['Division'] == 'Third Division North']
# Extract the 'Goals' column (the last 'Goals' column in the data)
goals = third_div_north['Goals'].dropna()
# Calculate variance
variance = goals.var()
print(f"Final Answer: {variance:.2f}")
```
