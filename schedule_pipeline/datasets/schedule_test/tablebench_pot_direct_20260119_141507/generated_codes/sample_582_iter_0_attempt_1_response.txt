Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the percentage of Catholics decreased the most compared to the previous year. This means we need to calculate the difference in percentage between consecutive years and identify the year with the largest decrease.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and convert the 'Percentage' column to numeric (to ensure proper calculation).

Step 3: Compute the difference in percentage from one year to the next (i.e., current year's percentage minus previous year's percentage).

Step 4: Identify the year where this difference is the most negative (largest decrease).

Step 5: Print the corresponding year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert the 'Percentage' column to numeric
df['Percentage'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Percentage'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate the difference in percentage from year to year
df['diff'] = df['Percentage'].diff()

# Find the year with the maximum negative difference (largest decrease)
decrease_year = df[df['diff'] == df['diff'].min()]['year'].values[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {decrease_year}")
```
