Step 3: Observe the trend in issue prices over the years. The issue price increased from $24.95 in 2002 to $38.95 in 2007, with a consistent increase of $10.00 between 2003 and 2007 (except for a jump from $24.95 to $34.95 in 2003).  
Step 4: From 2003 onward, the price increases by $4.00 each year:  
- 2003: $34.95  
- 2004: $34.95  
- 2005: $34.95

This suggests a pattern where the price remains stable for a few years and then jumps. Since 2007 is the first year with $38.95, and the previous years were $34.95, we can expect a potential increase in 2008.  
Step 5: Based on the last known increase (from $34.95 to $38.95), a likely forecast for 2008 would be a further increase, possibly to $42.95 if the trend continues. However, since no clear linear or exponential trend exists beyond the jump, and the data shows only one significant jump, a conservative forecast based on the last increase is to project the next step as $42.95.  
Step 6: Output the forecasted issue price.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract issue prices and convert to float
issue_prices = df['issue price'].astype(float)

# Observe the trend: after 2003, prices stay at 34.95 until 2007, then jump to 38.95
# Assuming a similar jump in 2008, add $4.00 to the last known price
forecasted_price = issue_prices.iloc[-1] + 4.00

print(f"Final Answer: {forecasted_price:.2f}")
```
