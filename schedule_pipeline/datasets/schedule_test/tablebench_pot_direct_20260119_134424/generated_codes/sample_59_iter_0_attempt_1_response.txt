Step 1: The user wants to identify the nation that has 4 gold medals and is ranked 4th in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the row where 'Rank' is '4' and 'Gold' is '4'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Nation' from this row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for rank 4 and 4 gold medals
nation = df[(df['Rank'] == '4') & (df['Gold'] == '4')]['Nation'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation}")
```
