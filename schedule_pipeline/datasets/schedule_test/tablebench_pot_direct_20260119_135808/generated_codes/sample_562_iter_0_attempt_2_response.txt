Step 1: The user wants the total mintage of coins issued from 2002 to 2006.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the year is between 2002 and 2006 (inclusive).
Step 4: Sum the 'mintage' column values.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years 2002 to 2006
filtered_df = df[df['year'].astype(int).between(2002, 2006)]
# Calculate total mintage
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_mintage}")
```
