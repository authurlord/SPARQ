Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between 'revenue (millions)' and 'profit (millions)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'revenue (millions)' and 'profit (millions)' columns.
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation coefficient between revenue and profit
correlation = df['revenue (millions)'].corr(df['profit (millions)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
