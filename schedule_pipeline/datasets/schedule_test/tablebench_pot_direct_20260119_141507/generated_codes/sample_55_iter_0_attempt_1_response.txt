Step 1: The user wants to find the nation that won 1 gold and 4 silver medals.  
Step 2: Look through the 'gold' and 'silver' columns in the table to find a row where 'gold' = 1 and 'silver' = 4.  
Step 3: Match this with the corresponding 'nation' value.  

From the table:  
- Algeria has 1 gold and 4 silver medals.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the nation with 1 gold and 4 silver medals
result = df[(df['gold'] == 1) & (df['silver'] == 4)]['nation'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {result}")
```
