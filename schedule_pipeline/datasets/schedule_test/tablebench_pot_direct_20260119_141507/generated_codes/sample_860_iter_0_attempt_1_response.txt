Step 1: Identify the relevant columns for executions — "Executions in persona" and "Executions in effigie".  
Step 2: Find the total executions for Córdoba and Valencia by summing the respective values from the rows.  
Step 3: Compute the difference between the two totals.  

From the table:
- Córdoba: Executions in persona = 17, Executions in effigie = 19 → Total = 17 + 19 = 36  
- Valencia: Executions in persona = 2, Executions in effigie = 0 → Total = 2 + 0 = 2  

Difference = 36 - 2 = 34  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the relevant values for Córdoba and Valencia
cordoba_executions = df.loc[df['Tribunal'] == 'Córdoba', ['Executions in persona', 'Executions in effigie']].values[0]
valencia_executions = df.loc[df['Tribunal'] == 'Valencia', ['Executions in persona', 'Executions in effigie']].values[0]

total_cordoba = cordoba_executions[0] + cordoba_executions[1]
total_valencia = valencia_executions[0] + valencia_executions[1]

difference = total_cordoba - total_valencia
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
