Step 1: Identify the top 5 companies in the oil and gas industry from the table.
Step 2: Filter the data to include only companies in the "oil and gas" industry.
Step 3: Select the top 5 companies by market value (or rank, if available), but since the table does not have a clear ranking for market value, we take the first 5 companies in the oil and gas industry based on their order in the table.
Step 4: Extract the "market value (billion )" for these companies.
Step 5: Calculate the average of these market values.

Note: The table lists companies in order of rank, and "oil and gas" companies are listed from rank 4 onwards. The oil and gas companies in the dataset are:
- ExxonMobil (rank 4)
- Royal Dutch Shell (rank 5)
- PetroChina (rank 6)
- Petrobras (rank 8)
- Chevron (rank 16)
- Total (rank 19)

We take the first 5 oil and gas companies as per their order in the table.

Step 6: Compute the average market value.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter companies in the oil and gas industry
oil_gas_df = df[df['industry'] == 'oil and gas']

# Take the first 5 companies (by row order)
top_5_oil_gas = oil_gas_df.head(5)

# Calculate average market value
average_market_value = top_5_oil_gas['market value (billion )'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_market_value:.1f}")
```
