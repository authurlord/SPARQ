Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete won a gold medal (1st position) in the 200m event at the European Junior Championships.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter the data for the 'Event' column where the event is '200 m' and the 'Competition' is 'European Junior Championships', and the 'Position' is '1st'.

Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the filtered row and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for European Junior Championships, 200m event, and 1st position
filtered_data = df[(df['Competition'] == 'European Junior Championships') & 
                   (df['Event'] == '200 m') & 
                   (df['Position'] == '1st')]

# Extract the year
year = filtered_data['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
