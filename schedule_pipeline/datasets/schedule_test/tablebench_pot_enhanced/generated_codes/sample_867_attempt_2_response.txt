Step 1: The user wants to compare the number of days served by Calvin L. Rampton and Mike Leavitt to determine who served longer.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows for Calvin L. Rampton and Mike Leavitt.
Step 4: Compare their 'Days' values and print the governor with more days.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for the two governors
rampton_days = df[df['Governor'] == 'Calvin L. Rampton']['Days'].iloc[0]
leavitt_days = df[df['Governor'] == 'Mike Leavitt']['Days'].iloc[0]

# Compare and determine who served longer
if rampton_days > leavitt_days:
    print("Final Answer: Calvin L. Rampton")
else:
    print("Final Answer: Mike Leavitt")
```
