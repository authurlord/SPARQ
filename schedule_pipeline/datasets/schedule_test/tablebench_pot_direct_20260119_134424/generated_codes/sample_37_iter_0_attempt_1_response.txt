Step 1: The user wants the total average rating of all TV dramas, which means summing up the 'average' column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'average' column and convert it to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the sum of the 'average' column and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'average' column to numeric and calculate total
total_average = df['average'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_average}")
```
