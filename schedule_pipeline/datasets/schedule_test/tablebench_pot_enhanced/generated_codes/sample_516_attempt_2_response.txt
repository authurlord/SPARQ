Step 1: The user wants to calculate the longest locomotive service life, defined as the difference between 'entered service' and 'withdrawn' dates.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'entered service' and 'withdrawn' columns to integers (they are already in numeric form).
Step 4: Calculate the service life for each locomotive by subtracting 'entered service' from 'withdrawn'.
Step 5: Find the maximum service life and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate service life as withdrawn - entered service
df['service_life'] = df['withdrawn'] - df['entered service']
# Find the maximum service life
max_service_life = df['service_life'].max()
print(f"Final Answer: {max_service_life}")
```
