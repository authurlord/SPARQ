Step 1: The user wants to analyze the relationship between the percentage of votes for McCain (`mccain %`) and the total votes (`total`) across counties.  
Step 2: We need to examine whether there's a trend (e.g., increasing, decreasing, or stable) in McCain's percentage as total votes increase.  
Step 3: Since both columns are numerical, we can compute the correlation or simply visualize the relationship.  
Step 4: We will calculate the correlation coefficient between `mccain %` and `total` to determine the strength and direction of the relationship. A positive value indicates that as total votes increase, McCain's percentage tends to increase, and vice versa.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'mccain %' and 'total'
correlation = df['mccain %'].corr(df['total'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
