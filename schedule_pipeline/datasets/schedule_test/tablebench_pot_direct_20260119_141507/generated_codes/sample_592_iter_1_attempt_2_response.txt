Step 1: Identify the cities by rank and name. The 6th ranked city is 'valenza' (rank '6th'), and 'acqui terme' is the 5th ranked city.  
Step 2: Extract the population values for both cities.  
Step 3: Convert the population values from strings to integers and compute the difference.  
Step 4: Ensure proper indexing to avoid string-to-string subtraction errors.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the population of the 6th ranked city ('valenza') and 'acqui terme'
valenza_pop = df[df['city'] == 'valenza']['population'].values[0]
acqui_terme_pop = df[df['city'] == 'acqui terme']['population'].values[0]

# Calculate the difference
difference = valenza_pop - acqui_terme_pop
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
