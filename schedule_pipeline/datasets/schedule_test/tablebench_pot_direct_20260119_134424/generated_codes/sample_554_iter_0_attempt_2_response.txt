Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in issue price from 2007 to 2011.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'year' and 'issue price' columns, convert 'issue price' to float.  
Step 4: Group by year and calculate the average issue price per year (since multiple entries exist per year).  
Step 5: Compute the difference in issue prices between consecutive years.  
Step 6: Calculate the average of these yearly increases.  
Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'issue price' to float
df['issue price'] = df['issue price'].astype(float)
# Group by year and take the mean issue price for each year
annual_avg_price = df.groupby('year')['issue price'].mean()
# Calculate year-over-year increase
price_increase = annual_avg_price.diff().dropna()
# Calculate average annual increase
avg_increase = price_increase.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_increase:.2f}")
```
