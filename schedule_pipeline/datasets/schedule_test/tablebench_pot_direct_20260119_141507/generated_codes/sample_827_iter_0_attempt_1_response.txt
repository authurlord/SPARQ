Step 1: The user wants two values:
- The median total number of medals for the top 5 nations (rank 1 to 5).
- The median total number of medals for all countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'total' column.
Step 4: For the top 5 rankings, select rows where rank is from 1 to 5, then compute the median of the 'total' column.
Step 5: For all countries, compute the median of the 'total' column.
Step 6: Print both medians.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter top 5 nations (rank 1 to 5)
top_5 = df[df['rank'].between(1, 5)]
median_top_5 = top_5['total'].median()

# Median for all countries
median_all = df['total'].median()

print(f"Final Answer: {median_top_5}, {median_all}")
```
