Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total mintage from 2002 to 2005, then divide it equally among 5 people.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'year' is between 2002 and 2005 (inclusive).
Step 4: Sum the 'mintage' column for these rows.
Step 5: Divide the total mintage by 5 and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years 2002 to 2005
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].astype(int) >= 2002) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2005)]
# Calculate total mintage
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].astype(int).sum()
# Divide equally among 5 people
coins_per_person = total_mintage / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {int(coins_per_person)}")
```
