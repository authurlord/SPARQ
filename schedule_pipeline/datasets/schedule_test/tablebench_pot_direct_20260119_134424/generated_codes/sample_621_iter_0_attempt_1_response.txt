Step 1: The user wants to calculate the average age of the executives listed in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'Age' column, convert it to numeric (in case of any formatting issues).
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'Age' column and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Age' column to numeric and calculate the mean
average_age = df['Age'].astype(int).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_age:.1f}")
```
