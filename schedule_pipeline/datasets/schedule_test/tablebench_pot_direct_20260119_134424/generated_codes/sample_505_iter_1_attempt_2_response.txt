Step 1: The user wants to calculate the Hurricane Severity Index (HSI) for each year, which is defined as (number of hurricanes + number of major hurricanes) / number of tropical storms. This requires numerical operations, so we must ensure that all relevant columns are converted to integers.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Convert the 'number of hurricanes' and 'number of major hurricanes' columns to integers, and also convert 'number of tropical storms' to integer.

Step 4: Calculate HSI for each year.

Step 5: Find the year with the maximum HSI.

Step 6: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert necessary columns to integers
df['number of hurricanes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of hurricanes'])
df['number of major hurricanes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of major hurricanes'])
df['number of tropical storms'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of tropical storms'])

# Calculate HSI: (hurricanes + major hurricanes) / tropical storms
df['HSI'] = (df['number of hurricanes'] + df['number of major hurricanes']) / df['number of tropical storms']

# Find the year with the highest HSI
max_hsi_year = df.loc[df['HSI'].idxmax(), 'year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_hsi_year}")
```
