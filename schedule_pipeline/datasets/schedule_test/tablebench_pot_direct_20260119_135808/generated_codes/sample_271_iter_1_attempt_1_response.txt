Step 1: The question asks for the average number of episodes per season, but only for seasons that:
- Have at least 40 episodes (Episodes >= 40)
- Premiered between October and December (Season Premiere month is October, November, or December)

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'Season Premiere' to datetime to extract the month.
Step 4: Filter rows where Episodes >= 40 and the month of Season Premiere is between 10 (October) and 12 (December).
Step 5: Calculate the mean of Episodes for the filtered rows.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Season Premiere' to datetime
df['Season Premiere'] = pd.to_datetime(df['Season Premiere'])
# Extract month
df['month'] = df['Season Premiere'].dt.month
# Filter seasons with at least 40 episodes and premiered in October, November, or December
filtered_df = df[(df['Episodes'] >= 40) & (df['month'].isin([10, 11, 12]))]
# Calculate average episodes
avg_episodes = filtered_df['Episodes'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_episodes:.1f}")
```
