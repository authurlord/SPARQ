The question asks about the impact of an increase in eccentricity on periselene and aposelene distances. This is a conceptual or physical relationship question, not a direct numerical computation from the data.

From orbital mechanics:
- Eccentricity (e) measures how elongated the orbit is.
- Periselene (closest point to the central body) = a(1 - e)
- Aposelene (farthest point) = a(1 + e)
- So, as eccentricity increases:
  - Periselene decreases
  - Aposelene increases

The given table provides data points for different epochs with varying eccentricity, periselene, and aposelene values. We can analyze the trend by computing correlations between eccentricity and periselene/aposelene.

Analytical approach:
1. Extract the 'eccentricity', 'periselene', and 'aposelene' columns.
2. Compute the correlation between eccentricity and periselene, and between eccentricity and aposelene.
3. Interpret the results: negative correlation with periselene, positive with aposelene.

Note: The data does not include semi-major axis (a), so we cannot derive it directly, but we can still observe trends.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['eccentricity'] = pd.to_numeric(df['eccentricity'], errors='coerce')
df['periselene'] = pd.to_numeric(df['periselene'], errors='coerce')
df['aposelene'] = pd.to_numeric(df['aposelene'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation between eccentricity and periselene/aposelene
correlation_periselene = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['periselene'])
correlation_apselene = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['aposelene'])

print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and periselene: {correlation_periselene:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and aposelene: {correlation_apselene:.3f}")

Final Answer: negative, positive
```
