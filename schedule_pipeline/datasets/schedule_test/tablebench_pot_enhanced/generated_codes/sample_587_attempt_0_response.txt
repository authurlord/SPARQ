Step 1: The user wants the total field goals scored by players who played 15 or more games.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Games Played' is 15 or more.
Step 4: Sum the 'Field Goals' column for the filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter players who played 15 or more games
filtered_df = df[df['Games Played'] >= '15']
# Convert 'Field Goals' to integer and sum
total_field_goals = filtered_df['Field Goals'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_field_goals}")
```
