Step 1: The user wants the average percentage of national votes won by all leaders.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the '% of national vote' column, which contains values in string format with a '%' sign.  
Step 4: Convert these values to floats by removing the '%' sign and dividing by 100.  
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the converted values.  
Step 6: Print the result rounded to two decimal places.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Remove the '%' sign and convert to float
df['% of national vote'] = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate the average
average_national_vote = df['% of national vote'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_national_vote:.2f}")
```
