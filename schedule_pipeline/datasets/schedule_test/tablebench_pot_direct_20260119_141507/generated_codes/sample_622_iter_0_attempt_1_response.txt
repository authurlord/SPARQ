Step 1: Identify universities located in London from the 'location' column.  
Step 2: Filter the dataframe to include only rows where 'location' is 'london'.  
Step 3: Sum the 'research funding (000)' column for these filtered rows.  
Step 4: Print the total research funding.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter universities located in London
london_universities = df[df['location'] == 'london']
# Sum the research funding for these universities
total_funding = london_universities['research funding (000)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_funding}")
```
