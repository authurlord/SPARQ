Step 1: The user is asking whether there is a positive correlation between typhus and typhoid fever cases in the same year. This requires analyzing the relationship between the two variables across years.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the 'typhus' and 'typhoid fever' columns.

Step 3: Calculate the correlation coefficient between typhus and typhoid fever cases. A positive correlation (close to 1) would suggest that as typhus increases, typhoid fever also tends to increase.

Step 4: Print the correlation value as the answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the relevant columns
typhus = df['typhus']
typhoid_fever = df['typhoid fever']

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = typhus.corr(typhoid_fever)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
