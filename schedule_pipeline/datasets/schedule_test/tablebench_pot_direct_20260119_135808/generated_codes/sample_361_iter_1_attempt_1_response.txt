Step 1: The user wants to count the number of years where the number of hurricanes is 3 or fewer.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'number of hurricanes' column to integers, as it may be read as strings.
Step 4: Filter rows where 'number of hurricanes' <= 3.
Step 5: Count the number of such rows and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'number of hurricanes' to integer to avoid type errors
df['number of hurricanes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of hurricanes'], errors='coerce')
# Count years with 3 or fewer hurricanes
count_years = (df['number of hurricanes'] <= 3).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {count_years}")
```
