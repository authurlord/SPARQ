Step 1: The user wants to find the number of R class locomotives in service at the end of 1931.  
Step 2: We are given that at the start of 1931, there were 11 R class locomotives in service.  
Step 3: 1 locomotive was withdrawn during the year.  
Step 4: Therefore, the number at the end of 1931 is: 11 - 1 = 10.

This can be directly computed without complex analysis.

```python
# Given data from the table: R class in service at start of 1931 = 11, withdrawn = 1
r_class_start_1931 = 11
withdrawn_1931 = 1

r_class_end_1931 = r_class_start_1931 - withdrawn_1931
print(f"Final Answer: {r_class_end_1931}")
```
