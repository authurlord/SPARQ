Step 1: The user wants to find the season when the driver won 13 races.  
Step 2: Look at the 'wins' column and find the row where 'wins' equals 13.  
Step 3: Extract the corresponding 'season' value from that row.  

From the table, we see that in the row with '2011', 'german formula three championship', the 'wins' column is 13.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the season where wins = 13
season_with_13_wins = df[df['wins'] == 13]['season'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {season_with_13_wins}")
```
