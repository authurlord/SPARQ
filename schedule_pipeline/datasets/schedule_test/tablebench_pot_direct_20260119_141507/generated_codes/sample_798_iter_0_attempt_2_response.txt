Step 1: The user wants the average annual change in the number of Conservative councillors from 1947 to 1972.  
Step 2: Extract the "Conservative councillors" column and the corresponding years.  
Step 3: Compute the difference in Conservative councillors from 1947 to 1972 (i.e., the total change over the years).  
Step 4: Divide by the number of years (from 1947 to 1972 inclusive) to get the average annual change.  

Note: The change is calculated as (value in 1972 - value in 1947) / (number of years).  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract Conservative councillors and years
conservative_councillors = df['Conservative councillors']
years = df['Year'].astype(int)

# Calculate the total change from 1947 to 1972
start_year = 1947
end_year = 1972
change = conservative_councillors.iloc[-1] - conservative_councillors.iloc[0]
num_years = end_year - start_year + 1

# Average annual change
average_annual_change = change / num_years
print(f"Final Answer: {average_annual_change:.2f}")
```
