We are asked to find:

1. The year with the highest average issue price.
2. The difference between that year's average issue price and the average issue price of the lowest year (i.e., the year with the lowest average issue price).

However, note: the "issue price" is given per coin (each row), not per year. So we need to compute the average issue price **per year**, then compare the maximum and minimum of those yearly averages.

Step-by-step approach:
- Load the data.
- Group by 'year' and calculate the average issue price for each year.
- Find the year with the highest average issue price.
- Find the year with the lowest average issue price.
- Compute the difference between the highest and lowest.

Note: There are only 6 rows, so we can group by year and compute mean issue price per year.

Potential error in previous attempt: trying to apply `.mean()` on a column with non-numeric values or misinterpreting the grouping.

Let’s fix it by ensuring proper grouping and numeric calculation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Group by 'year' and compute average issue price per year
yearly_avg_price = df.groupby('year')['issue price'].mean()

# Find the year with highest and lowest average issue price
max_price_year = yearly_avg_price.idxmax()
min_price_year = yearly_avg_price.idxmin()

max_avg_price = yearly_avg_price.max()
min_avg_price = yearly_avg_price.min()

difference = max_avg_price - min_avg_price

print(f"Final Answer: {max_price_year}, {difference:.2f}")
```
