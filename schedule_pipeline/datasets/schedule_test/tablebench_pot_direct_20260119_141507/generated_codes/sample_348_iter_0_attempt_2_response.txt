Step 1: The user wants to count how many countries have a population projection over 50 million in July 2013.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'july 1 , 2013 projection' column and convert it to numeric.  
Step 4: Filter rows where the value is greater than 50,000,000.  
Step 5: Count such entries.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'july 1 , 2013 projection' to numeric, handling potential formatting issues
projection_column = df['july 1 , 2013 projection'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)
# Filter countries with population over 50 million
count_over_50m = (projection_column > 50000000).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {count_over_50m}")
```
