Step 1: Identify universities located in London by checking the 'location' column.
Step 2: Filter the rows where 'location' is 'london'.
Step 3: Sum the 'research funding (000)' column for these filtered rows.
Step 4: Print the total research funding.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter institutions located in London
london_institutions = df[df['location'] == 'london']
# Sum the research funding for London institutions
total_funding = london_institutions['research funding (000)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_funding}")
```
