Step 1: The user wants to find the first year when the urbanization rate (percentage of population living in urban areas) surpassed 50%.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'urban , %' is greater than 50.
Step 4: Select the earliest (first) year from the filtered results and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where urban percentage is greater than 50
urban_above_50 = df[df['urban , %'] > 50]
# Get the first year when urbanization surpassed 50%
first_year = urban_above_50['year (january)'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {first_year}")
```
