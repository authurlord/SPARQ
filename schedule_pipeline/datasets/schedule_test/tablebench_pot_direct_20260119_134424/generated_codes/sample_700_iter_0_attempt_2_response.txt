Step 1: The user wants to identify the year with the highest success rate.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'success rate' column to a numeric value by removing the '%' sign and converting to float.
Step 4: Find the row with the maximum success rate.
Step 5: Extract the corresponding year and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert success rate to numeric by removing % and converting to float
df['success rate'] = df['success rate'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Find the year with the highest success rate (excluding the 'total' row)
max_success_year = df.loc[df['success rate'].idxmax()]['year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_success_year}")
```
