Step 1: The user wants to identify the competition where the athlete won 1st place in the 3000 m steeplechase event, and the venue is a city that is also the capital of the Bahamas.

Step 2: From the table, filter rows where:
- The 'Event' is '3000 m steeplechase'
- The 'Position' is '1st'
- The 'Venue' is 'Nassau, Bahamas' (since Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas)

Step 3: Extract the 'Competition' from the matching row.

Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 1st place in 3000 m steeplechase in Nassau, Bahamas
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == '3000 m steeplechase') & 
                 (df['Position'] == '1st') & 
                 (df['Venue'] == 'Nassau, Bahamas')]
# Extract the competition
competition = filtered_df['Competition'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {competition}")
```
