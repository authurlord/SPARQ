Step 1: The user wants to find the season where the giant slalom score is 7.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'giant slalom' column equals '7'.
Step 4: Extract and print the corresponding 'season' value.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row where 'giant slalom' is 7
season_with_giant_slalom_7 = df[df['giant slalom'] == '7']['season'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {season_with_giant_slalom_7}")
```
