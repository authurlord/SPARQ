Step 1: The user wants the median number of aircraft movements for the top 10 busiest airports.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Select the 'aircraft movements' column and convert it to integers.  
Step 4: Extract the top 10 airports based on rank (first 10 rows).  
Step 5: Calculate the median of the 'aircraft movements' for these 10 airports.  
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'aircraft movements' to integer
df['aircraft movements'] = pd.to_numeric(df['aircraft movements'])
# Get top 10 airports (first 10 rows by rank)
top_10 = df.head(10)['aircraft movements']
# Calculate median
median_movements = top_10.median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_movements}")
```
