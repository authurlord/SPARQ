Step 1: The user is asking about the relationship between GDP (PPP) per capita and metropolitan population. Specifically, they want to know how GDP per capita changes as metropolitan population increases.

Step 2: We need to examine the correlation between "metropolitan population (2006) millions" and "gdp (ppp) us per capita".

Step 3: Since both columns are numerical, we can calculate the correlation coefficient (Pearson's r) to determine the strength and direction of the relationship.

Step 4: A positive correlation means that as population increases, GDP per capita tends to increase; a negative correlation means it decreases.

Step 5: We will compute the correlation and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between metropolitan population (millions) and GDP (PPP) per capita
correlation = df['metropolitan population (2006) millions'].corr(df['gdp (ppp) us per capita'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
