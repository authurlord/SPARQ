Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'Magnitude' and 'Depth' of earthquakes.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'Magnitude' and 'Depth' columns.  
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'Magnitude' and 'Depth'
correlation = df['Magnitude'].corr(df['Depth'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
