Step 1: The user wants to identify the years when the song with the highest US chart position and the song with the lowest US chart position were released.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'US Chart position' to numeric, handling non-numeric values (like 'R&B' entries) by skipping or converting them to NaN.
Step 4: Find the maximum and minimum US chart positions (ignoring non-numeric values).
Step 5: Retrieve the corresponding years for these positions.
Step 6: Print the two years.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'US Chart position' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['US Chart position'] = pd.to_numeric(df['US Chart position'], errors='coerce')
# Drop rows where US Chart position is NaN (e.g., R&B entries)
df_clean = df.dropna(subset=['US Chart position'])
# Find the year with the highest (worst) and lowest (best) US chart position
max_position_year = df_clean.loc[df_clean['US Chart position'].idxmax(), 'Year']
min_position_year = df_clean.loc[df_clean['US Chart position'].idxmin(), 'Year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_position_year}, {min_position_year}")
```
