Step 1: The user wants the percentage difference between the average annual growth rate of the values in the '2011' and '2010' columns for the top 5 ranked countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter the top 5 ranked countries (rank 1 to 5).

Step 3: Convert the '2010' and '2011' columns to numeric, then calculate the growth rate for each country as (2011 - 2010) / 2010.

Step 4: Compute the average of these growth rates for the top 5 countries.

Step 5: Calculate the percentage difference between the average growth rate of 2011 and 2010 (which is just the average growth rate itself, expressed as a percentage).

Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 ranked countries
top_5 = df[df['rank'].astype(float) <= 5]

# Convert 2010 and 2011 columns to numeric
top_5['2010'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2010'])
top_5['2011'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2011'])

# Calculate growth rate for each country
growth_rates = (top_5['2011'] - top_5['2010']) / top_5['2010']

# Average growth rate
avg_growth_rate = growth_rates.mean()

# Convert to percentage
percentage_difference = avg_growth_rate * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {percentage_difference:.2f}")
```
