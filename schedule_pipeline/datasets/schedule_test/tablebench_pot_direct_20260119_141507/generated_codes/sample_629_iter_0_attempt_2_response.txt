Step 1: The user wants the total sales of the top 5 albums (by position, i.e., the first 5 rows).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'sales' column for the first 5 rows.
Step 4: Sum these sales values and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Get the sales of the top 5 albums (first 5 rows)
top_5_sales = df.head(5)['sales'].sum()
```
