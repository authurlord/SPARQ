Step 1: The user wants two values:
- The median total number of medals won by nations in the top 5 rankings (ranks 1 to 5).
- The median total number of medals won by all countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'total' column for all nations and compute the overall median.
Step 4: Filter the data for the top 5 ranks (rank 1 to 5), extract their 'total' values, and compute the median.
Step 5: Print both results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate median total medals for top 5 nations
top_5_medals = df[df['rank'].astype(int) <= 5]['total'].astype(int)
median_top_5 = top_5_medals.median()

# Calculate median total medals for all nations
all_medals = df['total'].astype(int)
median_all = all_medals.median()

print(f"Final Answer: {median_top_5}, {median_all}")
```
