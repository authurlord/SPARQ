Step 1: The user wants the average mintage (proof) for coins issued in the first 5 years of the 2000s, i.e., from 2000 to 2004.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'year' is between 2000 and 2004 (inclusive).
Step 4: Convert 'mintage (proof)' column to numeric, handling 'n/a' as NaN.
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the filtered mintage (proof) values.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years 2000 to 2004
df_filtered = df[df['year'].astype(int).between(2000, 2004)]
# Convert 'mintage (proof)' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df_filtered['mintage (proof)'] = pd.to_numeric(df_filtered['mintage (proof)'], errors='coerce')
# Calculate average mintage (proof)
avg_mintage = df_filtered['mintage (proof)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_mintage:.1f}")
```
