Step 1: The user wants to calculate the new total excise tax after a 20% increase in the federal excise tax for Canada (average).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the federal excise tax value for 'canada (average)'.
Step 4: Increase it by 20%.
Step 5: Calculate the new total excise tax by adding the increased federal excise tax to the existing total excise tax (which already includes federal and provincial taxes).
Step 6: Print the result.

Note: Since the total excise tax already includes the federal excise tax, increasing the federal excise tax by 20% means we must recalculate the total as:
new_total = (original_federal_tax * 1.2) + (original_total_tax - original_federal_tax)

This ensures only the federal portion is increased while the rest remains unchanged.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Canada (average)
canada_row = df[df['government'] == 'canada (average)']
federal_tax = float(canada_row['federal excise tax ( cad / l )'].values[0])
total_tax = float(canada_row['total excise tax (cad / l)'].values[0])

# Increase federal excise tax by 20%
new_federal_tax = federal_tax * 1.2

# Recalculate total excise tax with increased federal tax
new_total_tax = new_federal_tax + (total_tax - federal_tax)

print(f"Final Answer: {new_total_tax:.2f}")
```
