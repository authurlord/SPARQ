Step 1: The user wants to determine which year has the highest average passenger traffic across all airports and the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.

Step 2: Load the dataframe from the provided JSON table.

Step 3: Extract the columns corresponding to the years (2008, 2009, 2010) and convert them to numeric type for analysis.

Step 4: Calculate the average passenger traffic per year (mean across all airports).

Step 5: Identify the year with the highest average.

Step 6: Compute the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.

Step 7: Output the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert year columns to numeric
year_columns = ['2008', '2009', '2010']
df[year_columns] = df[year_columns].apply(pd.to_numeric)

# Calculate average passenger traffic per year
avg_traffic_per_year = df[year_columns].mean()

# Find the year with the highest average
highest_avg_year = avg_traffic_per_year.idxmax()

# Get the standard deviation for that year
std_dev = df[highest_avg_year].std()

print(f"Final Answer: {highest_avg_year}, {std_dev:.1f}")
```
