Step 1: The question asks for the relationship between wind power generation and the percentage of renewable energy in total electricity demand.  
Step 2: We need to analyze the correlation between "wind power" and "% renewable of total electricity demand".  
Step 3: Since both are numerical, we can compute the correlation coefficient.  
Step 4: Use pandas to extract the relevant columns and calculate the correlation.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract wind power and % renewable of total electricity demand
wind_power = df['wind power']
renewable_percentage = df['% renewable of total electricity demand']

# Calculate correlation
correlation = wind_power.corr(renewable_percentage)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
