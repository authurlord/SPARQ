Step 1: The user wants the median population density of the European Union member states.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Exclude the "European Union" row since it represents the total, not individual member states.  
Step 4: Select the 'pop density people / km 2' column for member states.  
Step 5: Calculate the median and print it.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter out the 'European Union' row to focus on member states
df_member_states = df[df['member state'] != 'european union']
# Extract the population density column and calculate median
median_density = df_member_states['pop density people / km 2'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_density:.1f}")
```
