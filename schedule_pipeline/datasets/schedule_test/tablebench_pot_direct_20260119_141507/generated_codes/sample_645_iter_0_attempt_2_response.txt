Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'sales' and 'profits' columns.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'sales (billion )' and 'profits (billion )' columns as numeric values.
Step 4: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert sales and profits to numeric
sales = pd.to_numeric(df['sales (billion )'], errors='coerce')
profits = pd.to_numeric(df['profits (billion )'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = sales.corr(profits)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
