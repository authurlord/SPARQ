Step 1: The user wants to identify the university with the highest research funding and calculate the difference between the highest and lowest research funding (in thousands).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Extract the 'research funding (000)' column and find the maximum and minimum values.

Step 4: Identify the institution corresponding to the maximum funding.

Step 5: Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest funding.

Step 6: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the university with the highest research funding
max_funding_row = df.loc[df['research funding (000)'].idxmax()]
max_institution = max_funding_row['institution']
max_funding = max_funding_row['research funding (000)']

# Find the university with the lowest research funding
min_funding_row = df.loc[df['research funding (000)'].idxmin()]
min_funding = min_funding_row['research funding (000)']

# Calculate the difference
difference = max_funding - min_funding

print(f"Final Answer: {max_institution}, {difference}")
```
