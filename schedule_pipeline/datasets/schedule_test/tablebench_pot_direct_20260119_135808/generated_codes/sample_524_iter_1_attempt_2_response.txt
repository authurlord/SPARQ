Step 1: The user wants to calculate the change in total seating capacity from 2004 to 2008. We need to consider only aircraft that were introduced on or before 2008 and not retired before 2008.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter aircraft introduced in or before 2008 and not retired before 2008 (i.e., retired is '-' or after 2008).
Step 4: Sum the seating capacities of these aircraft.
Step 5: Since we are calculating the change from 2004 to 2008, we assume the fleet in 2004 only includes aircraft introduced in 2004 or earlier. But since no aircraft were retired before 2004, we can consider all aircraft introduced up to 2004 and compare with those introduced up to 2008.
Step 6: Calculate the difference between the total seating capacity in 2008 and 2004.
Step 7: Convert 'seating' to integer and handle missing values.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'introduced' to integer for comparison
df['introduced'] = pd.to_numeric(df['introduced'], errors='coerce')

# Filter aircraft introduced in or before 2004
fleet_2004 = df[df['introduced'] <= 2004]
total_capacity_2004 = fleet_2004['seating'].astype(int).sum()

# Filter aircraft introduced in or before 2008 and not retired before 2008
fleet_2008 = df[(df['introduced'] <= 2008) & ((df['retired'] == '-') | (pd.to_numeric(df['retired'], errors='coerce') >= 2008))]
total_capacity_2008 = fleet_2008['seating'].astype(int).sum()

# Calculate change in total seating capacity
capacity_change = total_capacity_2008 - total_capacity_2004

print(f"Final Answer: {capacity_change}")
```
