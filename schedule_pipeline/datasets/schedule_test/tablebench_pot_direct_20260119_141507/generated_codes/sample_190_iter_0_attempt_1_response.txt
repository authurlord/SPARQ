Step 1: The question asks for the top 2 factors (among 'innings', 'runs scored', 'balls faced') that significantly influence 'average' and 'sr' (strike rate) values.  
Step 2: Since 'average' and 'sr' are derived from 'runs scored' and 'balls faced', we can analyze correlations between these variables.  
Step 3: We will compute the correlation between each of the three factors and both 'average' and 'sr'.  
Step 4: Identify the two strongest correlations (in terms of magnitude) to determine the top 2 influencing factors.  

Note: "Significantly influence" is interpreted here as having the highest absolute correlation with 'average' and 'sr'.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert relevant columns to numeric (handle strings like '39.13')
df['runs scored'] = pd.to_numeric(df['runs scored'], errors='coerce')
df['balls faced'] = pd.to_numeric(df['balls faced'], errors='coerce')
df['average'] = pd.to_numeric(df['average'].str.replace('%', ''), errors='coerce')
df['sr'] = pd.to_numeric(df['sr'].str.replace('%', ''), errors='coerce')

# Select only numeric columns for correlation
numeric_cols = ['innings', 'runs scored', 'balls faced', 'average', 'sr']
df_numeric = df[numeric_cols]

# Compute correlation matrix
correlation_matrix = df_numeric.corr()

# Find the top 2 factors that have the highest absolute correlation with 'average' and 'sr'
# We consider all pairs and rank by absolute correlation value
correlations = []
for col in ['average', 'sr']:
    for factor in ['innings', 'runs scored', 'balls faced']:
        if factor != col:
            corr_val = abs(correlation_matrix.loc[col, factor])
            correlations.append((factor, col, corr_val))

# Sort by correlation strength (descending)
correlations.sort(key=lambda x: x[2], reverse=True)

# Top 2 factors (by correlation strength)
top_2_factors = [factor for factor, _, _ in correlations[:2]]

print(f"Final Answer: {top_2_factors[0]}, {top_2_factors[1]}")
```
