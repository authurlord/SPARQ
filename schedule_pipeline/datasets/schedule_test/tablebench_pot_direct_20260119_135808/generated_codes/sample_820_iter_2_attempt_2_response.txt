Step 1: The user wants the average melamine content for products where at least 5 samples were taken.
Step 2: Load the dataframe from the provided JSON table.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'samples taken' is greater than or equal to 5.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'melamine content (mg / kg)' column for the filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'samples taken' to integer and filter rows with at least 5 samples
filtered_df = df[df['samples taken'].astype(int) >= 5]
# Calculate the average melamine content for the filtered data
average_melamine = filtered_df['melamine content (mg / kg)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_melamine:.2f}")
```
