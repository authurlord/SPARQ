Step 1: Filter the data to include only mountain peaks in Morocco with an elevation of at least 3000 meters and a col elevation greater than 1500 meters.
Step 2: Extract the 'prominence (m)' values for the filtered peaks.
Step 3: Calculate the average of these prominence values.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for peaks in Morocco with elevation >= 3000m and col > 1500m
filtered_peaks = df[(df['country'] == 'morocco') & 
                   (df['elevation (m)'].astype(int) >= 3000) & 
                   (df['col (m)'].astype(int) > 1500)]
# Calculate average prominence
average_prominence = filtered_peaks['prominence (m)'].astype(int).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_prominence:.1f}")
```
