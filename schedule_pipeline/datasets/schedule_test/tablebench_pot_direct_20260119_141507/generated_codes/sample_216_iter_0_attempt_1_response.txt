Step 1: The user is asking whether the wife's age at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has. This involves checking for a correlation between "her age" and "of children".

Step 2: Load the data into a DataFrame.

Step 3: Compute the correlation coefficient between "her age" and "of children". A high absolute value (close to 1 or -1) suggests a strong relationship, while a value near 0 indicates no significant correlation.

Step 4: Print the correlation coefficient to assess the relationship.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between her age and number of children
correlation = df['her age'].corr(df['of children'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
