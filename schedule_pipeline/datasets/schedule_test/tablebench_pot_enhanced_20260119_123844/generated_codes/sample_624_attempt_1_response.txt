Step 1: The user wants to calculate the new total excise tax after a 20% increase in the federal excise tax for Canada (average).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Identify the row for 'canada (average)' and extract the current federal excise tax.
Step 4: Increase the federal excise tax by 20% and calculate the new total excise tax using the same proportion (since total excise tax is derived from federal excise tax and other components, but the question implies only the federal part is increased).
Step 5: However, since the total excise tax includes both federal and provincial components, and only the federal excise tax is being increased, we need to recalculate the total excise tax as:
   New total excise tax = (Increased federal excise tax) + (Provincial portion from original total)
   But the data does not provide the breakdown of the provincial portion. Therefore, we assume that the total excise tax increases proportionally based on the federal component's change.

But since the problem states "if the federal excise tax is increased by 20%", and the total excise tax is a combination of federal and provincial taxes, we must assume that only the federal part changes. However, without knowing the exact provincial contribution, we cannot compute the new total directly.

Alternatively, if the question assumes that the total excise tax scales with the federal excise tax (i.e., the ratio remains constant), then:
New total excise tax = Old total excise tax × (1 + 0.20) × (Old federal / Old total)

But this is not valid because the federal tax is only part of the total.

Correct approach: The federal excise tax is a fixed part. If only the federal excise tax increases by 20%, then:
New total excise tax = (Old federal excise tax × 1.20) + (Old provincial excise tax)

But the provincial excise tax is not directly available. However, it can be calculated as:
Provincial excise tax = Total excise tax - Federal excise tax

So:
- Old federal = 10 CAD/L
- Old total = 24.5 CAD/L
- Provincial portion = 24.5 - 10 = 14.5 CAD/L
- New federal = 10 × 1.20 = 12 CAD/L
- New total = 12 + 14.5 = 26.5 CAD/L

This is the correct way.

Step 6: Perform the calculation and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the row for 'canada (average)'
canada_row = df[df['government'] == 'canada (average)']
# Extract federal excise tax and total excise tax
old_federal = float(canada_row['federal excise tax ( cad / l )'].values[0])
old_total = float(canada_row['total excise tax (cad / l)'].values[0])
# Calculate provincial portion
provincial_portion = old_total - old_federal
# New federal excise tax after 20% increase
new_federal = old_federal * 1.20
# New total excise tax
new_total = new_federal + provincial_portion
print(f"Final Answer: {new_total:.1f}")
```
