Step 1: The user wants to identify the nation that has 4 gold medals and is ranked 4th.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the row where 'Rank' is '4' and 'Gold' is '4'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Nation' from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row where rank is 4 and gold medals are 4
filtered_row = df[(df['Rank'] == '4') & (df['Gold'] == '4')]
nation = filtered_row['Nation'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation}")
```
