Step 1: The user wants to calculate the number of national votes in 1965 after increasing it by 10%.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the row where 'election' is '1965'.
Step 4: Extract the 'of national votes' value, convert it to integer, increase by 10%, and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row for 1965
row_1965 = df[df['election'] == '1965']
# Extract the national votes and convert to integer
national_votes_1965 = int(row_1965['of national votes'].values[0])
# Increase by 10%
increased_votes = national_votes_1965 * 1.1
print(f"Final Answer: {increased_votes:.0f}")
```
