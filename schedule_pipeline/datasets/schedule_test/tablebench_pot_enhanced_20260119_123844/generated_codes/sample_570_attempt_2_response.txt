Step 1: The user wants to find the year in which the average speed of the vessels increased the most compared to the previous year.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Clean the 'speed' column by removing commas and converting to float.  
Step 4: Convert 'year' to integer for comparison.  
Step 5: Calculate the year-on-year increase in average speed.  
Step 6: Identify the year with the maximum increase.  
Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean the 'speed' column: remove commas and convert to float
df['speed'] = df['speed'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)

# Convert 'year' to integer
df['year'] = df['year'].astype(int)

# Sort by year to ensure chronological order
df = df.sort_values('year')

# Calculate year-on-year change in speed
df['speed_change'] = df['speed'].diff()

# Find the year with the maximum positive increase
max_increase_year = df.loc[df['speed_change'].idxmax(), 'year']

print(f"Final Answer: {max_increase_year}")
```
