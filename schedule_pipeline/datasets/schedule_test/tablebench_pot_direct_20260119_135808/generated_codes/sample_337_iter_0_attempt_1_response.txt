Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete achieved their personal best (PB) in the 200m event at the Asian Games.  
Step 2: Filter the data for rows where the 'Event' is '200 m' and the 'Competition' is 'Asian Games'.  
Step 3: Among these rows, identify the one with 'SF1–1st PB' in the 'Position' column, which indicates a personal best.  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding 'Year' and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 200m event at Asian Games with PB
pb_row = df[(df['Event'] == '200 m') & (df['Competition'] == 'Asian Games') & (df['Position'] == 'SF1–1st PB')]
# Extract the year
year_pb = pb_row['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year_pb}")
```
