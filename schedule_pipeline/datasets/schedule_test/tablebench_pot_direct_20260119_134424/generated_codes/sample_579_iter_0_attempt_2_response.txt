Step 1: The user wants the average quantity of buses manufactured in the period 2000–2007.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2000 and 2007 (inclusive).
Step 4: Convert 'quantity' to integer and calculate the mean.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years between 2000 and 2007
filtered_df = df[df['year'].astype(int).between(2000, 2007)]
# Calculate average quantity
avg_quantity = filtered_df['quantity'].astype(int).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_quantity:.1f}")
```
