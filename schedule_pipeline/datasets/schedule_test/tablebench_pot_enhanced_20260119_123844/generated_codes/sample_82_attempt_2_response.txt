Step 1: The user wants to find the team with 13 points and a goal difference of 17.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'points' is 13 and 'difference' is 17.
Step 4: Extract the team name from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the team with 13 points and goal difference of 17
team = df[(df['points'] == '13') & (df['difference'] == '17')]['team'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {team}")
```
