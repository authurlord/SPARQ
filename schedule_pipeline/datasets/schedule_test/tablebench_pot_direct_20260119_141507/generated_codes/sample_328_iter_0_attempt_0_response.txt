Step 1: Identify films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki from the table.  
Step 2: Check which of these films have "Bangladesh's submission to Academy Awards" or similar mentions in the "Notes" column.  
Step 3: Count how many such films exist.

From the data:
- "Third Person Singular Number" (2009): Notes mention "Meril Prothom Alo Awards", not Academy Awards.
- "Television" (2012): Notes say "Bangladesh's submission to Academy Awards" → qualifies.
- "Doob: No Bed of Roses" (2017): Notes say "Bangladesh's submission to 91st Academy Awards" → qualifies.

Thus, two films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki were submitted to the Academy Awards.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki and check notes for Academy Awards submission
filtered = df[(df['Director'] == 'Mostofa Sarwar Farooki') & 
              (df['Notes'].str.contains('submission to Academy Awards', case=False, na=False))]
count = len(filtered)
print(f"Final Answer: {count}")
```
