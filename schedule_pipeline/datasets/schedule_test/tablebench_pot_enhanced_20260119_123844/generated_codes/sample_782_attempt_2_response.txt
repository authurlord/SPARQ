Step 2: Observe that the price increased by $4.00 from 2006 to 2007.
- Since the trend shows a gradual increase with a larger jump at the end, it's reasonable to assume a similar or slightly smaller increase for 2008.

Step 3: Predict the issue price for 2008 based on the last known increase ($4.00).
- If we assume a conservative continuation of the trend, the next likely price would be $38.95 + $4.00 = $42.95.

Step 4: Output the forecasted issue price.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract the issue prices
issue_prices = df['issue price'].astype(float)

# Last known price
last_price = issue_prices.iloc[-1]

# Calculate the increase from 2006 to 2007
increase = last_price - issue_prices.iloc[-2]

# Forecast next price
forecasted_price = last_price + increase

print(f"Final Answer: {forecasted_price:.2f}")
```
