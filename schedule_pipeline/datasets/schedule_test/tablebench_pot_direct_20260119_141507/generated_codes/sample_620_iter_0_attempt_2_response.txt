Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total mintage from years 2002 to 2005, then divide it equally among 5 people.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where year is between 2002 and 2005 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Sum the 'mintage' column for these rows.  
Step 5: Divide the total by 5 to get the number of coins per person.  
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years 2002 to 2005
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2002) & (df['year'] <= 2005)]
# Sum the mintage and divide by 5
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].sum()
coins_per_person = total_mintage / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {coins_per_person}")
```
