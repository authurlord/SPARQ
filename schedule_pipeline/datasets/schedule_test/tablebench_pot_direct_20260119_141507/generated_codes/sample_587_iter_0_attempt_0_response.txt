Step 1: The user wants the total field goals scored by players who played 15 games or more.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Games Played' is 15 or more.  
Step 4: Sum the 'Field Goals' column for the filtered players.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter players who played 15 games or more
filtered_players = df[df['Games Played'] >= 15]
# Sum the field goals of these players
total_field_goals = filtered_players['Field Goals'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_field_goals}")
```
