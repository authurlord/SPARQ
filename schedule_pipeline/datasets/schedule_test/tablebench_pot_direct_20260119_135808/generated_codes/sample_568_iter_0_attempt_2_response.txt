Step 1: The user wants to identify the year when the European Union's total energy production exceeded that of the United States.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter data for the European Union and the United States.
Step 4: Compare their total energy production values by year.
Step 5: Identify the year where EU's total exceeds US's total.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total' column to float for comparison
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'], errors='coerce')

# Filter data for European Union and United States
eu_data = df[df['country'] == 'european union']
us_data = df[df['country'] == 'united states']

# Extract total energy production for each country
eu_total = eu_data['total'].values[0]
us_total = us_data['total'].values[0]

# Compare and determine the year
if eu_total > us_total:
    year = eu_data['year'].values[0]
else:
    year = None

print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
