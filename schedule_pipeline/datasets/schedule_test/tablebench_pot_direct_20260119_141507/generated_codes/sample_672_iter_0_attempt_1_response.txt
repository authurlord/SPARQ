Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the number of branches and total assets for banks.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the columns 'Number of Branches As of 30 September 2012' and 'Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'.  
Step 4: Convert the values to numeric (some entries may have formatting issues like commas).  
Step 5: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient.

Note: The column names in the table are:
- '# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'
- 'Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'

We will clean and convert the data appropriately.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean the columns: remove commas and convert to numeric
df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'] = df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)
df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'] = df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation_coefficient = df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'].corr(df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation_coefficient:.2f}")
```
