Step 1: The user wants the total number of viewers (in millions) for episodes that meet two conditions:
- Rating/share (1849) is 3.0 or higher
- Rank (timeslot) is 1

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where 'rating / share (1849)' has a value of 3.0 or higher (convert to float for comparison) and 'rank (timeslot)' is '1'.

Step 4: Sum the 'viewers (millions)' column for the filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'rating / share (1849)' to float for comparison
df['rating / share (1849)'] = df['rating / share (1849)'].str.split('/').str[0].astype(float)
# Filter rows where rating/share is >= 3.0 and rank (timeslot) is 1
filtered_df = df[(df['rating / share (1849)'] >= 3.0) & (df['rank (timeslot)'] == '1')]
# Sum the viewers (millions) for these episodes
total_viewers = filtered_df['viewers (millions)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_viewers:.2f}")
```
