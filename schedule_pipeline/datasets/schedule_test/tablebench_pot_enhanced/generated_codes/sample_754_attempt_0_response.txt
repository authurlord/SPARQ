Step 1: The user is asking about the trend in qualifying speed ('qual') from 1960 to 1971 and how this trend might influence finishing positions in the next year (1972).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'qual' column and plot it over the 'year' to visualize the trend.  
Step 4: Analyze the trend (increasing, decreasing, or stable) and infer its potential impact on finishing positions.  
Step 5: Since we don't have data for 1972, we can only speculate based on the observed trend.  
Step 6: Print the conclusion.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'year' to integer for proper plotting
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'])

# Plot the trend of qualifying speed over the years
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 5))
plt.plot(df['year'], df['qual'], marker='o', linestyle='-', color='b')
plt.title('Trend of Qualifying Speed (qual) from 1960 to 1971')
plt.xlabel('Year')
plt.ylabel('Qualifying Speed (mph)')
plt.grid(True)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

# Analyze the trend
trend = "increasing" if df['qual'].iloc[-1] > df['qual'].iloc[0] else "decreasing"
print(f"Final Answer: {trend}")
```
