Step 1: The user wants the average total value per year for the years 2005 to 2009.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'year' column to integer and 'total' column to float for numerical operations.
Step 4: Filter rows where the year is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the 'total' column for the filtered data.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'year' to integer and 'total' to float
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'], errors='coerce')
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'], errors='coerce')

# Filter data for years 2005 to 2009
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2005) & (df['year'] <= 2009)]

# Calculate the average total value
average_total = filtered_df['total'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_total:.2f}")
```
