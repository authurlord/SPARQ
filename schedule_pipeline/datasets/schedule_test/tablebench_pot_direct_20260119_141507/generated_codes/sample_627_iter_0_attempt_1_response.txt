Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total winnings from two separate games:
- One with "natural royal flush" and a 3-credit bet.
- One with "four of a kind" and a 3-credit bet.

Step 2: From the table, find the payout for each hand at 3 credits:
- "natural royal flush" at 3 credits = 900
- "four of a kind" at 3 credits = 15

Step 3: Multiply each payout by the bet amount (3 credits), since the payout is already given per credit.

Step 4: Sum the two winnings.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract payouts for "natural royal flush" and "four of a kind" at 3 credits
natural_royal_flush_3_credit = df.loc[df['hand'] == 'natural royal flush', '3 credits'].values[0]
four_of_a_kind_3_credit = df.loc[df['hand'] == 'four of a kind', '3 credits'].values[0]

# Calculate total winnings for two separate games with 3-credit bets
total_winnings = (natural_royal_flush_3_credit + four_of_a_kind_3_credit) * 3

print(f"Final Answer: {total_winnings}")
```
