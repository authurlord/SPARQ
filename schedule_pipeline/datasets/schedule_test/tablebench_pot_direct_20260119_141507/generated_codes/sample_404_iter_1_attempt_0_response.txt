- "scenario": The operating condition (e.g., vacuum, Mach 1, cruise).
- "sfc in lb/(lbf h)": Specific fuel consumption in imperial units.
- "sfc in g/(kn s)": Specific fuel consumption in metric units.
- "specific impulse (s)": A measure of efficiency, higher is better.
- "effective exhaust velocity (m/s)": How fast the exhaust gases are expelled; directly related to efficiency.
