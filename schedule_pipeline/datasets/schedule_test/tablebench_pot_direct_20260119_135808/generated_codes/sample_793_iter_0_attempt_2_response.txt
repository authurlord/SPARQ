Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of the percentage of national votes across all elections.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'percentage of national vote' column, which is in the form of strings with '%' signs.
Step 4: Convert this column to numeric by removing the '%' sign and converting to float.
Step 5: Calculate the standard deviation of this numeric column.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Clean the '% of national vote' column by removing '%' and converting to float
df['% of national vote'] = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate standard deviation
std_dev = df['% of national vote'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_dev:.2f}")
```
