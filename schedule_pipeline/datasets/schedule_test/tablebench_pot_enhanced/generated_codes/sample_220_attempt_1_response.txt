Step 1: The user wants to analyze how the urban percentage changes with increasing population size between 1959 and 1989.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter data for years between 1959 and 1989 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Extract 'population (000)' and 'urban , %' columns.  
Step 5: Since the question asks about the relationship, we can compute the correlation between population and urban percentage, or simply observe the trend.  
Step 6: Print the trend or correlation as the answer.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data between 1959 and 1989
filtered_df = df[(df['year (january)'] >= '1959') & (df['year (january)'] <= '1989')]
# Convert population to numeric for analysis
filtered_df['population (000)'] = pd.to_numeric(filtered_df['population (000)'])
# Compute correlation between population and urban percentage
correlation = filtered_df['population (000)'].corr(pd.to_numeric(filtered_df['urban , %']))
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
