Step 1: The user is asking for the year when the athlete won a gold medal in the 100 m event.  
Step 2: In the table, "position" indicates performance; a "1st" position means a gold medal win.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the "event" is "100 m" and "position" is "1st".  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding "year" from those rows.  

Note: The data shows "1st" in the "position" column for the 100 m event in 2007 (San Salvador) and 2008 (Beijing). However, in 2008, the position is listed as "2nd", not "1st". Only the 2007 entry in San Salvador has "1st" in the 100 m event.  

Therefore, the only year with a gold medal in the 100 m event is 2007.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where event is '100 m' and position is '1st'
gold_medal_years = df[(df['event'] == '100 m') & (df['position'] == '1st')]['year']
# Since there's only one such year, return it
print(f"Final Answer: {gold_medal_years.iloc[0]}")
```
