Step 1: The user wants the average value of the "Pin (Tip)" column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'Pin (Tip)' column and convert it to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the mean and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Pin (Tip)' column to numeric and calculate the mean
average_pin_tip = df['Pin (Tip)'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_pin_tip:.1f}")
```
