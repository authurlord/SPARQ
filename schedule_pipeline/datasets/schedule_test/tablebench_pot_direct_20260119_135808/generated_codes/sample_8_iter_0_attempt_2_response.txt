Step 1: The user wants the average percentage of national votes won by all leaders across all elections.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'of national votes' column, which contains percentages in string format (e.g., '39.7%').
Step 4: Convert the percentage strings to floats by removing the '%' sign and dividing by 100.
Step 5: Calculate the mean of these values.
Step 6: Print the result rounded to two decimal places.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Remove the '%' sign and convert to float
df['% of national vote'] = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate the average
avg_national_vote = df['% of national vote'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_national_vote:.2f}")
```
