Step 1: The user wants to compare the total medal counts of Australia and Russia.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Find the total medal count for Australia and Russia from the 'total' column.  
Step 4: Compare and determine which nation has a higher total.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Get total medal counts for Australia and Russia
australia_total = df[df['nation'] == 'australia']['total'].values[0]
russia_total = df[df['nation'] == 'russia']['total'].values[0]

# Compare and answer
if australia_total > russia_total:
    final_answer = "Australia"
else:
    final_answer = "Russia"
print(f"Final Answer: {final_answer}")
```
