Step 1: The user is asking for the likely average finish position and total winnings for the next season, based on historical data from 1990 to 2002. Since the last active year is 2002 (with 5 starts), we will use the average finish position and total winnings from all years where the number of starts is similar (i.e., 5 or more starts) as a proxy.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'starts' >= 5 (to focus on seasons with similar participation).
Step 4: Calculate the average of 'avg finish' and sum of 'winnings' for those filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where starts >= 5 to consider seasons with similar participation
filtered_df = df[df['starts'] >= 5]
# Calculate average finish position and total winnings
avg_finish = filtered_df['avg finish'].mean()
total_winnings = filtered_df['winnings'].astype(int).sum()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_finish:.1f}, {total_winnings}")
```
