Step 1: The user wants a forecast for the next 5 years based on historical data from 1850 to 1858. Since the dataset is small (only 9 years) and lacks clear numerical trends or statistical patterns, a precise forecast is not feasible. However, we can analyze the data for any observable patterns in tropical storms and hurricanes.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and examine the 'number of tropical storms' and 'number of hurricanes' columns.

Step 3: Compute the average number of tropical storms and hurricanes per year over the period.

Step 4: Use these averages as a simple baseline forecast for the next 5 years.

Step 5: Present the forecasted average values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate average number of tropical storms and hurricanes per year
avg_storms = df['number of tropical storms'].astype(int).mean()
avg_hurricanes = df['number of hurricanes'].astype(int).mean()

# Forecast for next 5 years using the average
forecast_storms = avg_storms * 5
forecast_hurricanes = avg_hurricanes * 5

print(f"Final Answer: {forecast_storms:.1f}, {forecast_hurricanes:.1f}")
```
