Step 1: The user wants the average rating of episodes that satisfy two conditions:
- Viewership of at least 10 million (viewers (m) ≥ 10)
- Ranked within the top 3 in their timeslot (timeslot rank ≤ 3)

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter rows based on the conditions.

Step 3: Compute the mean of the 'rating' column for the filtered rows.

Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter episodes with viewership >= 10 million and timeslot rank <= 3
filtered_episodes = df[(df['viewers (m)'] >= 10) & (df['timeslot rank'] <= 3)]
# Calculate the average rating of the filtered episodes
average_rating = filtered_episodes['rating'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_rating:.1f}")
```
