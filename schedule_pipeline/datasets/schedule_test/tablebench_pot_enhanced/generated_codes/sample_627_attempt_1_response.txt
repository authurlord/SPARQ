Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total winnings for two separate games: one with a "natural royal flush" and one with a "four of a kind", both with a 3-credit bet.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the payout for "natural royal flush" under the '3 credits' column.
Step 4: Extract the payout for "four of a kind" under the '3 credits' column.
Step 5: Sum the two payouts and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract payout for natural royal flush with 3 credits
natural_royal_flush_payout = int(df[df['hand'] == 'natural royal flush']['3 credits'].iloc[0])
# Extract payout for four of a kind with 3 credits
four_of_a_kind_payout = int(df[df['hand'] == 'four of a kind']['3 credits'].iloc[0])
# Calculate total winnings
total_winnings = natural_royal_flush_payout + four_of_a_kind_payout
print(f"Final Answer: {total_winnings}")
```
