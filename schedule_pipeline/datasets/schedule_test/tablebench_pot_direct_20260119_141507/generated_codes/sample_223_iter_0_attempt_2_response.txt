Step 1: The user wants to analyze how the percentage of votes for McCain (`mccain %`) changes as the total votes (`total`) increases across counties. This implies a correlation or trend analysis between these two variables.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the `mccain %` and `total` columns.

Step 3: Compute the correlation coefficient between `mccain %` and `total` to determine the strength and direction of the relationship.

Step 4: Print the correlation value as the answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'mccain %' and 'total'
correlation = df['mccain %'].corr(df['total'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
