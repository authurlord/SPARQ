Step 1: The user wants to forecast the issue price of a coin in 2013 based on the historical trend from 2002 to 2012.  
Step 2: Extract the 'issue price' column for years 2002 to 2012.  
Step 3: Assume a linear trend (simple linear regression) to predict the issue price for 2013.  
Step 4: Use the average increase per year to project the value.  

Note: Since the data is small and shows a steady increase, we can calculate the average annual increase and apply it to 2013.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert year to numeric and extract issue price
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'])
issue_prices = df['issue price'].astype(float)

# Calculate the trend: average increase per year
years = df['year']
prices = issue_prices
annual_increase = (prices.iloc[-1] - prices.iloc[0]) / (years.iloc[-1] - years.iloc[0])

# Forecast for 2013
forecast_2013 = prices.iloc[-1] + annual_increase
print(f"Final Answer: {forecast_2013:.2f}")
```
