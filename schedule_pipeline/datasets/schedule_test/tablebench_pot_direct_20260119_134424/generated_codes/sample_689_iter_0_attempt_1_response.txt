Step 1: The user wants to find the canton with the highest number of years of kindergarten legally required.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'years of kindergarten legally required' column to numeric for proper comparison.
Step 4: Find the maximum value in this column and identify the corresponding canton.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'years of kindergarten legally required' to numeric, handling possible non-numeric entries
df['years of kindergarten legally required'] = pd.to_numeric(df['years of kindergarten legally required'], errors='coerce')

# Find the canton with the maximum value in 'years of kindergarten legally required'
max_canton = df.loc[df['years of kindergarten legally required'].idxmax(), 'canton']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_canton}")
```
