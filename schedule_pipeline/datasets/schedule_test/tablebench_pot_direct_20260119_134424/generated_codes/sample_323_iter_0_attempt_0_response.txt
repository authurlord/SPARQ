Step 1: The user wants to find the year and venue where the athlete achieved a personal best (PB) time in the Irish National Marathon Championship.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Competition' is 'Irish National Marathon Championship' and 'Notes' contains 'PB'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' and 'Venue' columns for the matching row.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Irish National Marathon Championship with PB in Notes
pb_row = df[(df['Competition'] == 'Irish National Marathon Championship') & (df['Notes'].str.contains('PB', na=False))]
# Extract year and venue
year = pb_row['Year'].values[0]
venue = pb_row['Venue'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}, {venue}")
```
