Step 1: The user wants to understand the relationship between Peak Flow (m³/s) and water Level (m) at Trent Bridge.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'Level at Trent Bridge m' and 'Peak Flow m3/s' columns.  
Step 4: Since the data is ordered by rank (from highest to lowest), we can analyze the trend: as the level increases, does the peak flow increase?  
Step 5: We can compute the correlation coefficient to quantify the relationship.  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient to answer the question.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Drop the row with None in 'Rank' (the "Normal / Avg flow" row)
df_clean = df.dropna(subset=['Rank'])

# Convert relevant columns to numeric
df_clean['Level at Trent Bridge m'] = pd.to_numeric(df_clean['Level at Trent Bridge m'])
df_clean['Peak Flow m3/s'] = pd.to_numeric(df_clean['Peak Flow m3/s'].str.replace(',', ''))

# Calculate correlation between level and peak flow
correlation = df_clean['Level at Trent Bridge m'].corr(df_clean['Peak Flow m3/s'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
