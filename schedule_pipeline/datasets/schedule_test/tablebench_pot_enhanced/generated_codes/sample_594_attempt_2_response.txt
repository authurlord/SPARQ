Step 1: The user wants the total points earned by the top 5 singers based on their place (i.e., the 5 lowest place numbers, since lower place means better ranking).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Sort the data by 'place' in ascending order to get the top 5 performers.
Step 4: Select the 'points' of the top 5 and sum them.
Step 5: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by 'place' to get the top 5 performers
top_5 = df.sort_values(by='place').head(5)
# Sum the points of the top 5
total_points = top_5['points'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_points}")
```
