However, based on orbital mechanics:
- Eccentricity measures how much an orbit deviates from a perfect circle.
- Higher eccentricity means the orbit is more elongated.
- Periselene (closest point to the central body) decreases as eccentricity increases.
- Aposelene (farthest point) increases as eccentricity increases.

The provided table contains data points for various epochs with values for eccentricity, periselene, and aposelene. We can analyze the relationship between these variables by observing trends in the data.

Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the data into a DataFrame.
2. Extract the 'eccentricity', 'periselene', and 'aposelene' columns.
3. Observe if there's a clear trend: as eccentricity increases, does periselene decrease and aposelene increase?
4. Since the data is limited (only 7 rows), we can perform a simple correlation or visual inspection.

We will compute the correlation between eccentricity and both periselene and aposelene to assess the relationship.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract relevant columns
eccentricity = df['eccentricity'].astype(float)
periselene = df['periselene (km)'].astype(float)
aposelene = df['aposelene (km)'].astype(float)

# Compute correlations
correlation_periselene = periselene.corr(eccentricity)
correlation_apselene = aposelene.corr(eccentricity)

print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and periselene: {correlation_periselene:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and aposelene: {correlation_apselene:.3f}")
```
