Step 1: The user wants the difference in performance (COP values) between two specific pump types at 35 °C.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows for "High-efficiency air source heat pump (ASHP), air at 0 °C" and "Ground source heat pump (GSHP), water at 0 °C" since both are relevant to 35 °C output.
Step 4: Extract the COP value at 35 °C (column index 3) for each.
Step 5: Calculate the difference and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract COP values at 35°C for the two specified pumps
ashp_35c = df[df['Pump type and source'] == 'High efficiency ASHP, air at 0 °C']['35 °C (e.g. heated screed floor)'].values[0]
gshp_35c = df[df['Pump type and source'] == 'Ground source heat pump (GSHP), water at 0 °C']['35 °C (e.g. heated screed floor)'].values[0]

# Calculate difference
difference = float(ashp_35c) - float(gshp_35c)
print(f"Final Answer: {difference:.1f}")
```
