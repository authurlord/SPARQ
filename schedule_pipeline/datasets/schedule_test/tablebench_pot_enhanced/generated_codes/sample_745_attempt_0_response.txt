Step 1: The user wants the average finish position and total winnings for the driver based on historical data from 1990 to 2002.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter the data for years 1990 to 2002 (all available data).  
Step 4: Calculate the mean of 'avg finish' and 'winnings' columns.  
Step 5: Since the last active year is 2002 with 5 starts, we assume the next season will have a similar number of races, so we use the overall averages.  
Step 6: Print the results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate average finish and total winnings across all years
avg_finish = df['avg finish'].mean()
total_winnings = df['winnings'].sum()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_finish:.1f}, {total_winnings}")
```
