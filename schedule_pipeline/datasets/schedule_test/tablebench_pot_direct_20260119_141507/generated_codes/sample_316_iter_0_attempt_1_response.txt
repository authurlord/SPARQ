Step 1: Identify the team with the most titles in the period from 1968 to 1983.  
Step 2: Find the year when that team won their first title.  
Step 3: Retrieve the winning coach for that specific year.  

From the data:
- The "winner (number of titles)" column shows the number of titles won by each team.
- We need to find the team with the highest value in "winner (number of titles)".
- Then, locate the first occurrence (earliest year) of that team's win to determine the year they won their first title.
- From that year, extract the "winning coach".

Observing the data:
- "New York Cosmos" has titles: 1 (1969), 2 (1977), 3 (1978), 4 (1980), 5 (1982) → total of 5 titles.
- "Atlanta Chiefs" has 1 title (1968).
- "Rochester Lancers" has 1 title (1970).
- Others have only 1 title.

So, New York Cosmos has the most titles (5).

Their first title was in 1969.  
In 1969, the winning coach is listed as "phil woosnam".

Final Answer: New York Cosmos, phil woosnam

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Find the team with the most titles
max_titles = df['winner (number of titles)'].str.extract(r'(\w+ \w+)', expand=False).dropna()
title_counts = df['winner (number of titles)'].str.count(r'\(')  # Count parentheses to get title count
# Parse the winner name and count
df['title_count'] = df['winner (number of titles)'].str.extract(r'\((\d+)\)').astype(float)
df['winner_name'] = df['winner (number of titles)'].str.extract(r'(\w+ \w+)').str.strip()

# Find the team with the maximum titles
max_title_team = df.loc[df['title_count'].idxmax(), 'winner_name']
first_win_year = df[df['winner_name'] == max_title_team].iloc[0]['year']

# Get the winning coach in the year of their first title
coach_in_first_win = df[df['year'] == first_win_year]['winning coach'].values[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {max_title_team}, {coach_in_first_win}")
```
