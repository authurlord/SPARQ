Step 1: The user wants to find the year Barbara Stanwyck won an Emmy Award for a TV series.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Association' is 'Emmy Awards' and 'Category' contains 'Series' or 'TV Series'.
Step 4: Check if 'Work' is related to a series (e.g., 'The Barbara Stanwyck Show', 'The Big Valley').
Step 5: Extract the 'Year' when she won such an award.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Emmy Awards related to a TV series and won
emmy_series_wins = df[(df['Association'] == 'Emmy Awards') & 
                      (df['Result'] == 'Won') & 
                      (df['Work'].str.contains('The Barbara Stanwyck Show|The Big Valley', na=False))]

# Get the year of the win
year = emmy_series_wins['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
