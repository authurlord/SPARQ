Step 1: The user wants the median elevation of mountain peaks in Greece.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the country is 'greece' or 'greece ( crete )' or 'greece ( euboea )' or 'greece ( kefalonia )' or 'greece ( samothrace )' — all indicate Greece.  
Step 4: Convert 'elevation (m)' to numeric.  
Step 5: Calculate the median of the elevation values.  
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for mountains in Greece (including regions within Greece)
greece_mountains = df[df['country'].str.contains('greece', case=False, na=False)]
# Convert elevation to numeric
greece_mountains['elevation (m)'] = pd.to_numeric(greece_mountains['elevation (m)'])
# Calculate median elevation
median_elevation = greece_mountains['elevation (m)'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_elevation}")
```
