Step 1: Identify films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki.  
Step 2: Among those films, check which ones have "Bangladesh's submission to Academy Awards" or similar mention in the "Notes" column.  
Step 3: Count how many such films exist.

From the table:
- "Third Person Singular Number" (2009): Notes mention Meril Prothom Alo Awards, not Academy Awards.
- "Television" (2012): Notes say "Bangladesh's submission to Academy Awards" → matches.
- "Doob: No Bed of Roses" (2017): Notes say "Bangladesh's submission to 91st Academy Awards" → matches.

So, two films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki were submitted to the Academy Awards.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter films directed by Mostofa Sarwar Farooki and check notes for Academy Awards submission
filtered = df[(df['Director'] == 'Mostofa Sarwar Farooki') & 
              (df['Notes'].str.contains('Academy Awards', case=False, na=False))]
count = len(filtered)
print(f"Final Answer: {count}")
```
