Step 1: The user wants the total diameter of craters with a diameter greater than 40 km.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'diameter (km)' is greater than 40.  
Step 4: Sum the 'diameter (km)' values for those filtered rows.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter craters with diameter > 40 km and sum their diameters
total_diameter = df[df['diameter (km)'] > 40]['diameter (km)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_diameter}")
```
