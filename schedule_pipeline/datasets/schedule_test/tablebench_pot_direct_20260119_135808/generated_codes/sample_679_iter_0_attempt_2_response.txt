Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between two numerical columns: 'carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)' and 'avg emission per km² of its land (tons)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the two relevant columns.
Step 4: Compute the correlation coefficient using pandas' corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the two columns for correlation
emissions_per_person = df['carbon dioxide emissions per year (tons per person) (2007)']
emissions_per_km2 = df['avg emission per km 2 of its land (tons)']

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation_coefficient = emissions_per_person.corr(emissions_per_km2)
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation_coefficient:.3f}")
```
