Step 1: The user wants the total population of the top 5 cities, which are ranked 1st to 5th.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'population' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Sum the populations of the first 5 rows (top 5 cities).
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert population to integer and sum the top 5
total_population = df['population'].astype(int).head(5).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_population}")
```
