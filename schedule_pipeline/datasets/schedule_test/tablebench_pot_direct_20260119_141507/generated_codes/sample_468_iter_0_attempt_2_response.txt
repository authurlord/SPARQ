Step 1: The user is asking to identify earthquake data points that are abnormal compared to the overall trends. Abnormal data points can be identified by examining the "local magnitude" column, as it is likely the key variable for detecting outliers.

Step 2: We will calculate the mean and standard deviation of the local magnitude to establish a baseline. Any magnitude that is more than 2 standard deviations above or below the mean could be considered an outlier.

Step 3: Filter the dataset to find such extreme values and report them.

Step 4: Print the rows with abnormal magnitudes.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate mean and std of local magnitude
mean_mag = df['local magnitude'].mean()
std_mag = df['local magnitude'].std()

# Define threshold for outliers (2 standard deviations from mean)
threshold = 2 * std_mag
outliers = df[(df['local magnitude'] > mean_mag + threshold) | (df['local magnitude'] < mean_mag - threshold)]

print("Final Answer:", outliers.to_dict('records'))
```
