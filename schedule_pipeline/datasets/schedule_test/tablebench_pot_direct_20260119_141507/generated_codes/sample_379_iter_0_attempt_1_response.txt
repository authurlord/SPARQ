Step 1: Understand the question — we need to count the number of years where:
- Natural change > 150
- Deaths < 350

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the relevant columns: 'Natural change' and 'Deaths'.

Step 3: Filter rows where natural change > 150 and deaths < 350.

Step 4: Count the number of such rows.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where natural change > 150 and deaths < 350
filtered_rows = df[(df['Natural change'] > 150) & (df['Deaths'] < 350)]
count = len(filtered_rows)
print(f"Final Answer: {count}")
```
