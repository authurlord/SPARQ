Step 1: The user wants to analyze the relationship between the number of candidates nominated and the percentage of popular vote over time.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'of candidates nominated' and '% of popular vote' columns to numeric for analysis.
Step 4: Plot a line chart showing how the percentage of popular vote changes with the number of candidates nominated across elections.
Step 5: Use matplotlib to visualize the trend.
```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['of candidates nominated'] = pd.to_numeric(df['of candidates nominated'])
df['% of popular vote'] = pd.to_numeric(df['% of popular vote'])

# Plot the relationship
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
plt.plot(df['election'], df['% of popular vote'], marker='o', label='% of Popular Vote')
plt.plot(df['election'], df['of candidates nominated'], marker='s', label='Number of Candidates Nominated')
plt.title('Impact of Number of Candidates Nominated on Popular Vote Percentage')
plt.xlabel('Election')
plt.ylabel('Value')
plt.legend()
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.grid(True)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

# Final Answer: The data shows a positive correlation between the number of candidates nominated and the percentage of popular vote over time.
Final Answer: positive correlation
