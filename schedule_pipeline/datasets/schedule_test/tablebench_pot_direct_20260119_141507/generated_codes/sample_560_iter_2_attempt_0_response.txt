The error occurred because the year "total" is a string and was included in the filtering, which caused a comparison error when trying to filter years from 2005 to 2009.

Step 1: Identify that we need to calculate the average number of appearances (apps) per year for Castleford Tigers from 2005 to 2009, excluding the "total" row.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive), and the team is "Castleford Tigers".

Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'apps' column for those filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for Castleford Tigers from 2005 to 2009 (excluding 'total' row)
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].str.isdigit()) & (df['year'].astype(int) >= 2005) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2009) & (df['team'] == 'castleford tigers')]
# Calculate average appearances (apps)
avg_apps = filtered_df['apps'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_apps:.1f}")
```
