Step 1: The user wants to calculate the average and standard deviation of 'us viewers (millions)'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'us viewers (millions)' column.
Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation.
Step 5: Print both values.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation of 'us viewers (millions)'
mean_viewers = df['us viewers (millions)'].mean()
std_viewers = df['us viewers (millions)'].std()

print(f"Final Answer: {mean_viewers:.2f}, {std_viewers:.2f}")
```
