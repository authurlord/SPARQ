Step 1: The user wants the revenue growth rate from 2001 to 2002, which is calculated as:  
((Total revenue in 2002 - Total revenue in 2001) / Total revenue in 2001) * 100.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the 'total revenue' values for 2001 and 2002.

Step 3: Compute the growth rate using the formula.

Step 4: Print the result rounded to one decimal place.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total revenue' column to integers
df['total revenue'] = df['total revenue'].astype(int)

# Extract total revenue for 2001 and 2002
revenue_2001 = df[df['year'] == '2001']['total revenue'].values[0]
revenue_2002 = df[df['year'] == '2002']['total revenue'].values[0]

# Calculate growth rate
growth_rate = ((revenue_2002 - revenue_2001) / revenue_2001) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {growth_rate:.1f}")
```
