Step 1: The user wants to count the number of parties that have more than 10% of the total votes.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter out the 'Total' row and the 'Source' row since they are not actual parties.
Step 4: Convert the '%' column to float for comparison.
Step 5: Count how many parties have a percentage greater than 10.
Step 6: Print the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Remove the 'Total' and 'Source' rows
parties_df = df[df['Party'] != 'Total']
parties_df = parties_df[parties_df['Party'] != 'Source: http://www.stat.go.jp/data/chouki/27.htm']

# Convert '%' column to float
parties_df['%'] = pd.to_numeric(parties_df['%'])

# Count parties with more than 10% votes
count = (parties_df['%'] > 10).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {count}")
```
