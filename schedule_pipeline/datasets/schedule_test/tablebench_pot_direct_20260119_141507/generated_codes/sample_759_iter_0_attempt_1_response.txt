Step 1: The user wants to project the population of Tabriz in 2026 based on historical growth from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 2: Extract the population data for Tabriz from the years 1956 to 2006.  
Step 3: Calculate the annual growth rate from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 4: Use this growth rate to project the population in 2026.  
Step 5: Since the data is discrete and spans 50 years, we assume constant annual growth (geometric growth).  

We will:  
- Find the row for Tabriz (rank 4).  
- Extract the population values from 1956 to 2006.  
- Compute the growth rate using the formula:  
  $$
  r = \left( \frac{P_{2006}}{P_{1956}} \right)^{1/50}
  $$  
- Project population in 2026 using:  
  $$
  P_{2026} = P_{2006} \times (1 + r)^{2026 - 2006} = P_{2006} \times (1 + r)^{20}
  $$

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter row for Tabriz (rank 4)
tabriz_row = df[df['city'] == 'tabriz'].iloc[0]

# Extract population values from 1956 to 2006
years = ['1956', '1966', '1976', '1986', '1996', '2006']
populations = [tabriz_row[year] for year in years]

# Population in 1956 and 2006
p_1956 = populations[0]
p_2006 = populations[5]

# Calculate annual growth rate
growth_rate = (p_2006 / p_1956) ** (1/50)

# Project population in 2026 (20 years after 2006)
p_2026 = p_2006 * (1 + growth_rate) ** 20

print(f"Final Answer: {p_2026:.0f}")
```
