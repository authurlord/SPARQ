Step 1: The user wants the percentage difference between the average annual growth rate of the values in 2011 and 2010 for the top 5 ranked countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter the top 5 ranked countries (rank 1 to 5).

Step 3: Convert the columns '2010' and '2011' to numeric type.

Step 4: Calculate the annual growth rate for each country as (2011 - 2010) / 2010.

Step 5: Compute the average of these growth rates for the top 5 countries.

Step 6: Calculate the percentage difference between the average growth rate of 2011 and 2010. Since it's a single year, the "average annual growth rate" is just the growth from 2010 to 2011. The percentage difference is not meaningful unless comparing two different periods. But interpreting the question as asking for the average growth rate from 2010 to 2011 for the top 5 countries, we compute that.

Step 7: Present the result as a percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 ranked countries
top_5 = df[df['rank'].astype(float) <= 5]
# Convert '2010' and '2011' columns to numeric
top_5['2010'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2010'])
top_5['2011'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2011'])
# Calculate growth rate from 2010 to 2011
growth_rate = (top_5['2011'] - top_5['2010']) / top_5['2010']
# Average growth rate
avg_growth_rate = growth_rate.mean()
# Convert to percentage
avg_growth_percentage = avg_growth_rate * 100
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_growth_percentage:.2f}")
```
