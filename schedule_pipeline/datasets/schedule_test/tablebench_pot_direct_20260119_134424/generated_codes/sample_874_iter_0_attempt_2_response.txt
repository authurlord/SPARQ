Step 1: The user wants to find the difference between the number of males in the 10–29 age group and the number of males in the 60+ age group.

Step 2: From the table, extract the male population for:
- 10–19 age group: 21
- 20–29 age group: 12
- 60–69 age group: 6
- 70–79 age group: 8
- 80+ age group: 3

Step 3: Sum the male population in the 10–29 age group (21 + 12 = 33) and sum the male population in the 60+ age group (6 + 8 + 3 = 17).

Step 4: Calculate the difference: 33 - 17 = 16.

Step 5: Print the final answer.

```python
# Extract male population data from the table
males_10_19 = 21
males_20_29 = 12
males_60_69 = 6
males_70_79 = 8
males_80_plus = 3

# Calculate total males in 10-29 age group
total_males_10_29 = males_10_19 + males_20_29

# Calculate total males in 60+ age group
total_males_60_plus = males_60_69 + males_70_79 + males_80_plus

# Find the difference
difference = total_males_10_29 - total_males_60_plus

print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
