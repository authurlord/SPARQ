Step 1: Filter the data to include only couples who have performed more than 7 dances (i.e., 'number of dances' > 7).
Step 2: Extract the 'average' column for these couples.
Step 3: Calculate the variance of the 'average' values.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter couples who performed more than 7 dances
filtered_df = df[df['number of dances'] > 7]
# Calculate variance of the 'average' column
variance = filtered_df['average'].var()
print(f"Final Answer: {variance:.2f}")
```
