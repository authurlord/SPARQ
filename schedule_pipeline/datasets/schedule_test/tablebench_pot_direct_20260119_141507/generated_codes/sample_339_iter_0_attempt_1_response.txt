Step 1: The user is asking for the year when the athlete won their first gold medal in the 100 m hurdles event.  
Step 2: A "gold medal" corresponds to a "1st" position in the "Position" column.  
Step 3: We need to filter rows where the "Event" is "100 m hurdles" and the "Position" is "1st".  
Step 4: Among those, find the earliest (first) year.  

Note: The first occurrence of a "1st" in the 100 m hurdles is in 2010 at the Oceania Youth Championships.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 100 m hurdles with 1st position
gold_medals = df[(df['Event'] == '100 m hurdles') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Get the first year (earliest)
first_gold_year = gold_medals.iloc[0]['Year']
print(f"Final Answer: {first_gold_year}")
```
