Step 1: The user wants to find the year in which the athlete won a gold medal (1st position) in the 100 m event.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'event' is '100 m' and the 'position' is '1st'.
Step 4: Extract the 'year' from the filtered row and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 100 m event and 1st position
gold_medal_year = df[(df['event'] == '100 m') & (df['position'] == '1st')]['year']
print(f"Final Answer: {gold_medal_year.iloc[0]}")
```
