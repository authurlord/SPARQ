Step 1: The user wants to identify the city with the highest population density and calculate the difference between the highest and lowest densities.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'density (inhabitants / km 2 )' column to numeric for comparison.
Step 4: Find the city with the maximum density and the city with the minimum density.
Step 5: Compute the difference between the two densities.
Step 6: Print the city with the highest density and the difference.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert density column to numeric
df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'] = pd.to_numeric(df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'])
# Find city with highest density
max_density_city = df.loc[df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].idxmax(), 'city']
# Find city with lowest density
min_density_city = df.loc[df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].idxmin(), 'city']
# Calculate difference
density_difference = df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].max() - df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].min()
print(f"Final Answer: {max_density_city}, {density_difference:.1f}")
```
