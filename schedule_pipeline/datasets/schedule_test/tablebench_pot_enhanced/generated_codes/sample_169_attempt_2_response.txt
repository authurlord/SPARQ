Since 'year ended' is a categorical/time variable and not a continuous predictor, it's unlikely to have a direct influence in a statistical sense. Instead, we should analyze the correlation between 'revenue (million)' and 'earnings per share (p)' to determine if revenue has a significant influence.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'revenue (million)' and 'earnings per share (p)' to numeric for correlation analysis.
Step 4: Compute the correlation coefficient between 'revenue (million)' and 'earnings per share (p)'.
Step 5: Interpret the result — if the correlation is high (close to 1 or -1), then revenue is a strong influencing factor.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['revenue (million)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['revenue (million)'])
df['earnings per share (p)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['earnings per share (p)'])

# Calculate correlation
correlation = df['revenue (million)'].corr(df['earnings per share (p)'])

# Print the correlation value
print(f"Final Answer: revenue (million)")
```
