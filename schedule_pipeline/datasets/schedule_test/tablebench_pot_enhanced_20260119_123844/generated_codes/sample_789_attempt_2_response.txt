Step 1: The user wants the mean and standard deviation of the 'Year built' column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'Year built' column to numeric (in case it's not already).
Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation.
Step 5: Print both values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Year built' to numeric
df['Year built'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Year built'])
# Calculate mean and standard deviation
mean_year = df['Year built'].mean()
std_year = df['Year built'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_year:.1f}, {std_year:.1f}")
```
