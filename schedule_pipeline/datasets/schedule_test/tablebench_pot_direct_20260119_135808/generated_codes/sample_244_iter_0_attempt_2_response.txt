Step 1: The user wants the difference in total days of inclement weather (snow + storms + fog) between the year with the most sunshine and the year with the least sunshine.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Clean the 'sunshine (hrs / year)' column by removing any non-numeric characters (like spaces or '1 630' → '1630').

Step 4: Convert 'sunshine (hrs / year)' to numeric for comparison.

Step 5: Compute total inclement weather (snow + storms + fog) for each row.

Step 6: Identify the year with the most sunshine and the year with the least sunshine.

Step 7: Find the corresponding inclement weather days for both years.

Step 8: Calculate the difference and print.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean and convert 'sunshine (hrs / year)' to numeric
df['sunshine (hrs / year)'] = df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].str.replace(' ', '').astype(int)

# Calculate total inclement weather (snow + storms + fog)
df['inclement_days'] = df['snow (days / year)'].astype(int) + df['storms (days / year)'].astype(int) + df['fog (days / year)'].astype(int)

# Find the year with most and least sunshine
max_sunshine_year = df.loc[df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].idxmax()]
min_sunshine_year = df.loc[df['sunshine (hrs / year)'].idxmin()]

# Get inclement days for both years
max_sunshine_inclement = max_sunshine_year['inclement_days']
min_sunshine_inclement = min_sunshine_year['inclement_days']

# Calculate difference
difference = abs(max_sunshine_inclement - min_sunshine_inclement)

print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
