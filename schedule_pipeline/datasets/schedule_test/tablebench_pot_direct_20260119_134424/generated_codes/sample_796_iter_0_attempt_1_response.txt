Step 1: The user wants the percentage difference between the average annual growth rate of values in 2011 and 2010 for the top 5 ranked countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter the top 5 ranked countries (rank 1 to 5).

Step 3: Convert the '2010' and '2011' columns to numeric.

Step 4: Calculate the annual growth rate for each country as (2011 - 2010) / 2010 * 100.

Step 5: Compute the average of these growth rates for the top 5 countries.

Step 6: Since the question asks for the percentage difference between the average growth rates of 2011 and 2010, we interpret this as the average growth rate itself (as it's a single value representing growth from 2010 to 2011). However, if interpreted strictly, "percentage difference" between two values is |(A - B)| / ((A + B)/2) * 100. But since we are calculating growth from 2010 to 2011, the correct interpretation is the average growth rate over the top 5 countries.

Step 7: Return the average growth rate as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 ranked countries
top_5 = df.head(5)
# Convert 2010 and 2011 columns to numeric
top_5['2010'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2010'])
top_5['2011'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2011'])
# Calculate growth rate for each country
growth_rates = (top_5['2011'] - top_5['2010']) / top_5['2010'] * 100
# Average growth rate
avg_growth_rate = growth_rates.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_growth_rate:.2f}")
```
