Step 1: Identify the relevant rows where the event is "Heptathlon" and the competition is "South American Championships" held in "Lima, Peru".  
Step 2: Check the "Year" and "Notes" columns to find the year and corresponding points score.  
Step 3: From the data, we see that in 2009, the athlete achieved 1st position in Heptathlon at South American Championships in Lima, Peru, with 5578 points.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Event is 'Heptathlon' and Competition is 'South American Championships' and Venue is 'Lima, Peru'
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == 'Heptathlon') & (df['Competition'] == 'South American Championships') & (df['Venue'] == 'Lima, Peru')]
# Extract the year and points score
year = filtered_df.iloc[0]['Year']
points = filtered_df.iloc[0]['Notes']
print(f"Final Answer: {year}, {points}")
```
