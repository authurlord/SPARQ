Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total winnings from two separate games: one with a "natural royal flush" and one with a "four of a kind", both with a 3-credit bet.  
Step 2: From the table, find the payout for "natural royal flush" and "four of a kind" when the bet is 3 credits.  
Step 3: Multiply each payout by the bet amount (3 credits) and sum them.  
Step 4: Output the total winnings.

From the table:
- "natural royal flush" at 3 credits = 900
- "four of a kind" at 3 credits = 15

Total winnings = 900 + 15 = 915

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract payouts for "natural royal flush" and "four of a kind" at 3 credits
natural_royal_flush = df.loc[df['hand'] == 'natural royal flush', '3 credits'].values[0]
four_of_a_kind = df.loc[df['hand'] == 'four of a kind', '3 credits'].values[0]

# Bet amount
bet = 3

# Total winnings
total_winnings = (natural_royal_flush + four_of_a_kind) * bet
print(f"Final Answer: {total_winnings}")
```
