The error occurred because the 'year' column contains strings (e.g., '2000'), and we need to convert them to integers before performing numerical operations like calculating differences or averages.

Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in 'property taxes' from 2000 to 2005.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'year' column to integers for proper numerical comparison and sorting.
Step 4: Extract the 'property taxes' values for years 2000 to 2005.
Step 5: Compute the annual increases (differences between consecutive years).
Step 6: Calculate the average of these increases.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert year to integer for proper numerical operations
df['year'] = df['year'].astype(int)
# Filter data for years 2000 to 2005
filtered_df = df[df['year'].between(2000, 2005)]
# Extract property taxes for these years
property_taxes = filtered_df['property taxes'].values
# Compute annual increases
increases = [property_taxes[i] - property_taxes[i-1] for i in range(1, len(property_taxes))]
# Calculate average increase
average_increase = sum(increases) / len(increases)
print(f"Final Answer: {average_increase:.0f}")
```
