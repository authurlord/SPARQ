Step 1: The user wants the mean height of buildings in Winnipeg with more than 10 floors.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where 'floors' > 10.  
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'height' column for those filtered buildings.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter buildings with more than 10 floors
filtered_buildings = df[df['floors'] > 10]
# Calculate the mean height of these buildings
mean_height = filtered_buildings['height'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_height:.1f}")
```
