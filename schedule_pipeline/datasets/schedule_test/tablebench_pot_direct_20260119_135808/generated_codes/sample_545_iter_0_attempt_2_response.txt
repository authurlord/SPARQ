Step 1: The user wants the average of the 'total' column for years between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2005 and 2009.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of the 'total' column for the filtered data.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'year' to integer and filter data from 2005 to 2009
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2005) & (df['year'] <= 2009)]
# Calculate average total
avg_total = filtered_df['total'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_total:.2f}")
```
