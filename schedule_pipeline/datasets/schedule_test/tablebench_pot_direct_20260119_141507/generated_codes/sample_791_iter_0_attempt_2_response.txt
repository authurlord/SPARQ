Step 1: The user wants the median number of aircraft movements among the top 10 busiest airports.  
Step 2: Identify the top 10 busiest airports by passengers (since "passengers" is the most direct measure of busyness).  
Step 3: Extract the "aircraft movements" for these top 10 airports.  
Step 4: Compute the median of the "aircraft movements" values.  

Note: The table has a "rank" column, where rank 1 is the busiest. So the top 10 busiest airports are those with ranks 1 through 10.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Select top 10 busiest airports (ranks 1 to 10)
top_10 = df.head(10)
# Extract aircraft movements for these airports
aircraft_movements = top_10['aircraft movements'].astype(float)
# Calculate median
median_movements = aircraft_movements.median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_movements}")
```
