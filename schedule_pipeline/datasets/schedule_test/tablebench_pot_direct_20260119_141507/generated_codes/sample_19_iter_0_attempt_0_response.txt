Step 1: The user wants the total revenue of all companies in the "oil" industry.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'industry' column is "oil".  
Step 4: Sum the 'revenue (millions)' column for those filtered rows.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter companies in the 'oil' industry and sum their revenue
oil_revenue = df[df['industry'] == 'oil']['revenue (millions)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {oil_revenue}")
```
