Step 1: Identify parties that have at least 2 seats in the "agricultural panel".  
Step 2: Sum the total seats for those parties.  
Step 3: Calculate the percentage of total seats they represent (using the "total" column).  
Step 4: Print both the total number of seats and the percentage.

Note: The "total" row is the sum of all seats, so we exclude it when filtering parties. We only consider rows where party is not "total".

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter parties with at least 2 seats in agricultural panel
filtered_parties = df[df['agricultural panel'] >= 2]

# Sum the total seats for these parties
total_seats = filtered_parties['total'].sum()

# Total seats across all parties (from the last row)
total_seats_all = df.loc[df['party'] == 'total', 'total'].values[0]

# Calculate percentage
percentage = (total_seats / total_seats_all) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {total_seats}, {percentage:.1f}%")
```
