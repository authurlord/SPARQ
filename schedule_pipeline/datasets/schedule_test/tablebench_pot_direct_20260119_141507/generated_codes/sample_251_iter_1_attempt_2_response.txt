Step 1: The user wants the total number of goals scored by Lincoln City in the Third Division North.

- The columns are: ['Club', 'Season', 'Division', 'Apps', 'Goals', 'Apps', 'Goals', 'Apps', 'Goals']
- The "Goals" appears to be in the 5th column (index 4), and the second "Goals" (index 6) and third (index 8) are for different competitions.

But the question is about total goals in the Third Division North — which should be the sum of goals from all seasons in that division.

- Lincoln City plays in "Third Division North" in seasons: 1946–47, 1947–48, 1948–49.
- In each row, the "Goals" column (index 4) is the total goals in the league (i.e., "League").
- The "Goals" in index 4 is the relevant one for the league.

So we need to:
- Filter rows where Club == "Lincoln City" and Division == "Third Division North"
- Sum the values in the "Goals" column (index 4)

Note: There is a row labeled "Lincoln City, Total" which may be misleading — we should exclude totals and only consider individual season rows.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Club is 'Lincoln City' and Division is 'Third Division North'
filtered_df = df[(df['Club'] == 'Lincoln City') & (df['Division'] == 'Third Division North')]
# Sum the 'Goals' column (index 4)
total_goals = filtered_df['Goals'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_goals}")
```
