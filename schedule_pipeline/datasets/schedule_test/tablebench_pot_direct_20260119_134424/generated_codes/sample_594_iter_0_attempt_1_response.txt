Step 1: The user wants the total points earned by the top 5 singers based on their place (ranked 1 to 5).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'place' is between 1 and 5 (inclusive).
Step 4: Sum the 'points' column for these rows.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 singers by place (1st to 5th)
top_5 = df[df['place'].astype(int) <= 5]
total_points = top_5['points'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_points}")
```
