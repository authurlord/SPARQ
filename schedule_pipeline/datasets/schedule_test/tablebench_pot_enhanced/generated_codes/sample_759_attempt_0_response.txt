Step 1: The user wants a projection of Tabriz's population in 2026 based on historical growth from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 2: Extract the population data for Tabriz (row 4) across the years 1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, and 2006.  
Step 3: Calculate the average annual growth rate between 1956 and 2006.  
Step 4: Use this rate to project the population for 2026 (20 years after 2006).  
Step 5: Assume exponential growth using the formula: P = P0 * (1 + r)^t, where P0 is the 2006 population, r is the average annual growth rate, and t is the number of years (20).  
Step 6: Print the projected population.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract Tabriz's population data
tabriz_data = df[df['city'] == 'tabriz']
populations = [int(tabriz_data[f'{year}'].values[0]) for year in [1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, 2006]]
years = [1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, 2006]

# Calculate average annual growth rate
total_growth_rate = (populations[-1] / populations[0]) ** (1 / len(years)) - 1
# Project population for 2026 (20 years after 2006)
projected_pop_2026 = populations[-1] * (1 + total_growth_rate) ** 20

print(f"Final Answer: {int(projected_pop_2026)}")
```
