Step 1: The user wants the payout for a specific hand ("four of a kind, 2-4") when betting 3 credits.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Locate the row where 'hand' is "four of a kind , 2 - 4".
Step 4: Extract the value in the '3 credits' column for that row.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the row where hand is 'four of a kind , 2 - 4'
payout = df[df['hand'] == 'four of a kind , 2 - 4']['3 credits'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {payout}")
```
