Step 1: The question asks to determine which factor—temperature T (°C) or density of air ρ (kg/m³)—has a greater causal influence on the speed of sound c (m/s).  
Step 2: Since this is a physical relationship, we know from physics that the speed of sound in air is primarily dependent on temperature, with the formula:  
c ≈ 331.3 + 0.606 × T (in °C),  
where temperature has a direct linear effect. Density also affects sound speed, but indirectly via temperature and pressure.  
Step 3: To analyze causality from the data, we can compute the correlation between c and T, and between c and ρ. A higher absolute correlation suggests stronger influence.  
Step 4: We will calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient between c and T, and between c and ρ.  
Step 5: Compare the two correlation values to determine which has a greater influence.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['Temperature T (°C)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficients
corr_temp = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].corr(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
corr_density = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].corr(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Compare correlations
if abs(corr_temp) > abs(corr_density):
    influence = 'Temperature T (°C)'
else:
    influence = 'Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'

print(f"Final Answer: {influence}")
```
