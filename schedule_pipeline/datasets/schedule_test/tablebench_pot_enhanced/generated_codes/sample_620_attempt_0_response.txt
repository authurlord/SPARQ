Step 1: The user wants to calculate how many coins each person would receive if the total mintage from 2002 to 2005 is divided equally among 5 people.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the data for years 2002 to 2005.
Step 4: Sum the 'mintage' values for these years.
Step 5: Divide the total mintage by 5 and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for years 2002 to 2005
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= '2002') & (df['year'] <= '2005')]
# Sum the mintage
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].sum()
# Divide by 5 people
coins_per_person = total_mintage / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {int(coins_per_person)}")
```
