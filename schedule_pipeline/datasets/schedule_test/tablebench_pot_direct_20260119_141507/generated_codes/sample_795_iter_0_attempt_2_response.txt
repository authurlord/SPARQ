Step 1: The user wants to find the year with the highest average passenger traffic across all airports, and then compute the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the passenger traffic columns (2008, 2009, 2010) and convert them to numeric.
Step 4: Compute the average passenger traffic per year for each year.
Step 5: Identify the year with the highest average.
Step 6: Calculate the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.
Step 7: Print the year and the standard deviation.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert passenger traffic columns to numeric
traffic_cols = ['2008', '2009', '2010']
df[traffic_cols] = df[traffic_cols].apply(pd.to_numeric, errors='coerce')

# Calculate average passenger traffic per year
year_avg = df[traffic_cols].mean(axis=1)

# Find the year with the highest average
max_avg_year = year_avg.idxmax()

# Get the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year
std_dev = df[traffic_cols].loc[year_avg.idxmax()].std()

print(f"Final Answer: {max_avg_year}, {std_dev:.1f}")
```
