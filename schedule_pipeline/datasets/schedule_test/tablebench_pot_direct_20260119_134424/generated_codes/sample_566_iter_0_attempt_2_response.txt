Step 1: Identify the earliest two years in the "Year" column. From the data, the earliest two years are 1893 and 1894.
Step 2: Filter the rows where the Year is either 1893 or 1894.
Step 3: Sum the "Quantity" column for these filtered rows.
Step 4: Print the total quantity.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data for the earliest two years: 1893 and 1894
earliest_years = df[df['Year'].isin(['1893', '1894'])]
# Calculate the total quantity
total_quantity = earliest_years['Quantity'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_quantity}")
```
