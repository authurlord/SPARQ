Step 1: The user wants to find the election year with the highest percentage of popular vote.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the '% of popular vote' column to a numeric value by removing the '%' sign and converting to float.
Step 4: Find the row with the maximum value in the '% of popular vote' column.
Step 5: Extract and print the corresponding election year.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Remove '%' and convert to float for proper comparison
df['% of popular vote'] = df['% of popular vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Find the election year with the highest percentage of popular vote
max_vote_year = df.loc[df['% of popular vote'].idxmax(), 'election']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_vote_year}")
```
