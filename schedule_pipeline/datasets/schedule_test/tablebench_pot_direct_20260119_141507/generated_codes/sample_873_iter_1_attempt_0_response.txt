The error occurred because the area values in the table are stored as strings (e.g., '2350.72'), and we cannot directly subtract them. We need to convert the area values to floats before performing the calculation.

Step 1: Identify the rows for "remainder of the municipality" and "ladysmith".
Step 2: Extract the area values from the respective rows and convert them to float.
Step 3: Compute the difference between the two areas.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert area column to float for numerical operations
df['area (km 2 )'] = df['area (km 2 )'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)

# Find the area of "remainder of the municipality" and "ladysmith"
remainder_area = df[df['place'] == 'remainder of the municipality']['area (km 2 )'].values[0]
ladysmith_area = df[df['place'] == 'ladysmith']['area (km 2 )'].values[0]

# Calculate the difference
difference = remainder_area - ladysmith_area
print(f"Final Answer: {difference:.2f}")
```
