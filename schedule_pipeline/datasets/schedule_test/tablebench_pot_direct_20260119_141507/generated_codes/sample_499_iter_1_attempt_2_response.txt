We are asked to calculate the **Goal Percentage** for Liverpool in the **1995–1996 season**. Goal percentage is defined as:

$$
\text{Goal Percentage} = \frac{\text{Goals}}{\text{Total Appearances}} \times 100
$$

From the table:
- For Liverpool in the 1995–1996 season, we can find:
  - Goals: 6 (from "Goals" column under "League" or "Total")
  - Total appearances: 38 (from "Apps" column under "League")

Note: The row for Liverpool in 1995–96 is:
`['Liverpool', '1995–96', 'Premier League', '40', '7', '7', '2', '4', '1', '4', '1', '53', '10']`

Breakdown:
- Apps (League): 40
- Goals (League): 7
- Apps (National Cup): 7
- Goals (National Cup): 2
- Apps (League Cup): 4
- Goals (League Cup): 1
- Apps (Europe): 4
- Goals (Europe): 1
- Total Apps: 53
- Total Goals: 10

But the question says: "Goal Percentage is defined as the ratio of goals scored to the total number of appearances made by a player or a team in a specific season or overall career."

It does not specify whether it's per competition or total. However, since it says "in a specific season", and the data includes a "Total" column, we should use the **total goals and total appearances**.

From the row:
- Total Apps: 53
- Total Goals: 10

So Goal Percentage = 10 / 53

We will compute this value.

Step-by-step:
1. Load the DataFrame.
2. Filter the row for Liverpool in the 1995–96 season.
3. Extract total goals and total appearances from the "Total" column (which is the last two columns: "Total" and "Total_1").
4. Compute the ratio.
5. Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter the row for Liverpool in 1995–96 season
filtered_row = df[(df['Club'] == 'Liverpool') & (df['Season'] == '1995–96')]
# Extract total goals and total appearances
total_goals = filtered_row['Total_1'].values[0]
total_apps = filtered_row['Total'].values[0]
# Calculate goal percentage
goal_percentage = (total_goals / total_apps) * 100
print(f"Final Answer: {goal_percentage:.2f}")
```
