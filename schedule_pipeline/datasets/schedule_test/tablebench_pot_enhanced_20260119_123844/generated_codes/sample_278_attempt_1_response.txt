Step 1: Filter the data to include only earthquakes with a death toll of at least 1,000 and depth less than 30 km.
Step 2: Extract the 'Magnitude' column from the filtered data.
Step 3: Calculate the average of the 'Magnitude' values.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Death toll' to numeric, removing commas
df['Death toll'] = df['Death toll'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)
# Filter rows where death toll >= 1000 and depth < 30
filtered_df = df[(df['Death toll'] >= 1000) & (df['Depth (km)'].astype(float) < 30)]
# Calculate average magnitude
avg_magnitude = filtered_df['Magnitude'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_magnitude:.1f}")
```
