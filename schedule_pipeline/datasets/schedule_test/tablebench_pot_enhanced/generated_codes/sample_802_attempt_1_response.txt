Step 1: The user wants the median number of televotes for performers with rank 10 or higher.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'rank' is greater than or equal to 10.
Step 4: Extract the 'televotes' column from the filtered data.
Step 5: Calculate the median of the 'televotes' and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where rank is >= 10
filtered_df = df[df['rank'] >= 10]
# Calculate median of televotes
median_televotes = filtered_df['televotes'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_televotes}")
```
