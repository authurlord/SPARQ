Step 1: The user is asking which award the individual won in 2003 for their performance in the movie "Road to Perdition".  
Step 2: Look through the table for rows where the Year is 2003 and the Work is "Road to Perdition".  
Step 3: Among those rows, identify the one where the Result is "Won" — that will be the award the individual won.  

From the data:
- In 2003, there are multiple nominations for "Road to Perdition", but only one has "Won": the "Young Artist Award" under "Best Performance in a Feature Film – Leading Young Actor".

Thus, the answer is "Young Artist Award".

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Year is 2003 and Work is 'Road to Perdition' and Result is 'Won'
award = df[(df['Year'] == '2003') & (df['Work'] == 'Road to Perdition') & (df['Result'] == 'Won')]['Award'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {award}")
```
