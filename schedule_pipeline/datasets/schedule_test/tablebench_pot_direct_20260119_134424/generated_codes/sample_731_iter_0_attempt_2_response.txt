Step 1: The user wants to identify the place with the largest area and calculate the difference between the largest and smallest areas.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'area (km 2 )' column to float for numerical operations.
Step 4: Find the place with the maximum area and the place with the minimum area.
Step 5: Calculate the difference between the two.
Step 6: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'area (km 2 )' to float for numerical comparison
df['area (km 2 )'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km 2 )'], errors='coerce')

# Find the place with the largest and smallest area
max_area_place = df.loc[df['area (km 2 )'].idxmax(), 'place']
min_area_place = df.loc[df['area (km 2 )'].idxmin(), 'place']
max_area = df['area (km 2 )'].max()
min_area = df['area (km 2 )'].min()

# Calculate the difference
difference = max_area - min_area

print(f"Final Answer: {max_area_place}, {difference:.2f}")
```
