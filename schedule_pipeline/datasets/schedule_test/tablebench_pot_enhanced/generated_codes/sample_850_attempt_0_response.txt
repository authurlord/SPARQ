Step 1: Identify the year with the highest percentage of national votes.
Step 2: Find the percentage of national votes for that year and the previous year.
Step 3: Calculate the difference between the two percentages.
Step 4: Return the year and the difference.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert '% of national vote' to float by removing '%' and dividing by 100
df['% of national vote'] = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)

# Find the row with the highest % of national vote
max_vote_row = df.loc[df['% of national vote'].idxmax()]
year_max = max_vote_row['election']
max_vote = max_vote_row['% of national vote']

# Find the previous year's percentage (based on sorted election years)
sorted_df = df.sort_values('election')
prev_year_idx = sorted_df[sorted_df['election'] == year_max].index[0] - 1
if prev_year_idx >= 0:
    prev_vote = sorted_df.iloc[prev_year_idx]['% of national vote']
else:
    prev_vote = 0  # No previous year

# Calculate the difference
difference = max_vote - prev_vote

print(f"Final Answer: {year_max}, {difference:.2f}")
```
