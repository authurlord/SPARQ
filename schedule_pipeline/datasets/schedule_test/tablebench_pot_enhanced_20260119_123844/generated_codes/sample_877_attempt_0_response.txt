Step 1: The user wants the difference between the average July high temperature and the average January high temperature across all cities.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'january (avg high degree)' and 'july (avg high degree)' columns to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of both columns.
Step 5: Subtract the mean January high from the mean July high.
Step 6: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert temperature columns to float
df['january (avg high degree)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['january (avg high degree)'])
df['july (avg high degree)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['july (avg high degree)'])

# Calculate average high temperatures
avg_jan_high = df['january (avg high degree)'].mean()
avg_jul_high = df['july (avg high degree)'].mean()

# Calculate the difference
difference = avg_jul_high - avg_jan_high
print(f"Final Answer: {difference:.1f}")
```
