The table contains information about metro lines in Chengdu, China. Each row represents a different line or summary, and the columns provide key details:

- **Line**: The line number or identifier (e.g., 1, 2, 3, 7 loop line, 10).
- **Terminals (District)**: The starting terminal and its district (e.g., Weijianian in Jinniu District).
- **Terminals (District)_1**: The ending terminal and its district (e.g., Science City in Shuangliu District).
- **Commencement**: The year the line first opened.
- **Newest Extension**: The year of the most recent extension; "-" indicates no extension.
- **Length km**: The total length of the line in kilometers.
- **Stations**: The number of stations on the line.

The last row provides totals across all lines.
