Step 1: The user wants to calculate the difference in years of service between two locomotives: 'Empress of Austria' and 'Cyclops'.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'Introduced' and 'Withdrawn' years for both locomotives.  
Step 4: Calculate the service duration (Withdrawn - Introduced) for each.  
Step 5: Compute the difference between the two durations.  
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for 'Empress of Austria' and 'Cyclops'
empress_row = df[df['Name'] == 'Empress of Austria']
cyclops_row = df[df['Name'] == 'Cyclops']

# Extract introduced and withdrawn years
empress_intro = int(empress_row['Introduced'].values[0])
empress_withdraw = int(empress_row['Withdrawn'].values[0])
cyclops_intro = int(cyclops_row['Introduced'].values[0])
cyclops_withdraw = int(cyclops_row['Withdrawn'].values[0])

# Calculate service years
empress_service = empress_withdraw - empress_intro
cyclops_service = cyclops_withdraw - cyclops_intro

# Compute difference
difference = empress_service - cyclops_service
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
