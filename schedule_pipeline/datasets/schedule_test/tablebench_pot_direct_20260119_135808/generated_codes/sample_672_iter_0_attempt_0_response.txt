Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the number of branches and total assets for banks.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'number of branches' and 'total assets' columns to numeric (removing commas and handling non-numeric values).
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Clean and convert 'number of branches' and 'total assets' to numeric
df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'] = df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)
df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'] = df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['# of Branches As of 30 September 2012'].corr(df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
