Step 1: The user wants to count the number of banks with total assets greater than 10,000 million TL.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012' column to numeric, handling the comma in the numbers.
Step 4: Filter banks where total assets > 10,000 million TL.
Step 5: Count the number of such banks and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012' to numeric, removing commas
df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'] = df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)
# Count banks with assets > 10,000 million TL
count_banks = df[df['Total Assets (million TL) As of 30 September 2012'] > 10000].shape[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {count_banks}")
```
