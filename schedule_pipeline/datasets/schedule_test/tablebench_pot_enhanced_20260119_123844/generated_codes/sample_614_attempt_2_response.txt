Step 1: The user wants to calculate how many more games Harold Snepsts played compared to the average of 300 regular season games.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the row where the player is 'harold snepsts' and extract his 'reg gp' value.
Step 4: Subtract the average (300) from his games played.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Harold Snepsts and get his reg gp
harold_games = df[df['player'] == 'harold snepsts']['reg gp'].values[0]
# Calculate difference from average
difference = int(harold_games) - 300
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
