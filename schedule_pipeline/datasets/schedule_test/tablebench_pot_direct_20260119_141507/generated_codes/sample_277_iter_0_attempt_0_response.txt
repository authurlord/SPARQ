Step 1: Identify songs with non-English language titles.  
Step 2: Filter songs where the points are greater than the median points of all songs.  
Step 3: Compute the average points for the filtered songs.  

First, load the data and extract the relevant columns. Then calculate the median points. Filter based on language (non-English) and points > median. Finally, compute the mean of points for the filtered rows.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate median points of all songs
median_points = df['points'].median()

# Filter songs: non-English language and points > median
filtered_df = df[
    (df['language'] != 'english') & 
    (df['points'] > median_points)
]

# Compute average points of the filtered songs
average_points = filtered_df['points'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_points:.1f}")
```
