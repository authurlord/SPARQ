Step 1: The question asks for an expected viewership of the next episode based on trends from the given data.  
Step 2: Viewership values are provided for episodes in chronological order. We can analyze the trend by observing if viewership is increasing, decreasing, or fluctuating.  
Step 3: Since the data only spans 6 episodes, we can calculate the average viewership or identify a pattern (e.g., increasing or decreasing trend).

However, looking at the viewership values:  
2.01, 1.45, 1.54, 1.88, 1.73, 1.58 — there is some fluctuation, but the overall trend appears slightly upward and then stabilizes. The average gives a neutral baseline.  

We will compute the average of the viewership values and use that as the expected viewership for the next episode.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the average viewership
average_viewership = df['viewers (in millions)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_viewership:.2f}")
```
