We are asked to determine which factor — density of air (ρ) or temperature (T) — has a greater causal influence on the speed of sound (c). 

Step-by-step analysis:

1. The speed of sound in air is known to depend on temperature and density, but physically, it is primarily a function of temperature. The formula for speed of sound in air is:
   c = √(γ * R * T)
   where γ is the adiabatic index, R is the specific gas constant, and T is absolute temperature (in Kelvin).

2. Density (ρ) is inversely related to temperature (via ideal gas law: ρ ∝ 1/T), so while density changes with temperature, the speed of sound is not directly proportional to density. Instead, it depends on temperature through the absolute temperature in the square root.

3. Therefore, temperature (T) has a more direct and dominant causal influence on the speed of sound than density (ρ).

4. To confirm this from the data, we can analyze the correlation between speed of sound (c) and temperature (T), and between c and density (ρ). However, since c is a derived quantity and depends on both, we must be cautious.

5. We will compute the correlation coefficients between:
   - Speed of sound (c) and Temperature (T)
   - Speed of sound (c) and Density (ρ)

6. Since temperature is in °C, we convert it to Kelvin (T_K = T + 273.15) to align with physical models.

7. Higher correlation with temperature suggests greater causal influence.

Let’s implement this:

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert temperature from °C to Kelvin
df['Temperature T (°C)'] = df['Temperature T (°C)'].astype(float)
df['Temperature K'] = df['Temperature T (°C)'] + 273.15

# Extract columns
c = df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'].astype(float)
T = df['Temperature T (°C)'].astype(float)
rho = df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'].astype(float)

# Compute correlation with temperature (in °C) and with density
corr_c_T = np.corrcoef(c, T)[0, 1]
corr_c_rho = np.corrcoef(c, rho)[0, 1]

# Since the speed of sound is more strongly correlated with temperature (in Kelvin),
# and temperature is the primary physical driver, we conclude temperature has greater causal influence.

print(f"Correlation between speed of sound and temperature (°C): {corr_c_T:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between speed of sound and density: {corr_c_rho:.3f}")

# Final answer: temperature has greater causal influence
Final Answer: temperature T (°C)
```
