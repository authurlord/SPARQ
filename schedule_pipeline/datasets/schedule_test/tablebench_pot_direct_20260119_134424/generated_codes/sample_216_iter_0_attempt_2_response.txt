Step 1: The user wants to determine if the wife's age at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has. To analyze this, we can calculate the correlation between 'her age' (at marriage) and 'of children' (number of children). A strong positive or negative correlation would suggest a relationship.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Convert 'her age' and 'of children' to numeric types (if not already).

Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between the two variables.

Step 5: Print the correlation value as the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'her age' and 'of children' to numeric
df['her age'] = pd.to_numeric(df['her age'])
df['of children'] = pd.to_numeric(df['of children'])

# Calculate correlation
correlation = df['her age'].corr(df['of children'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
