Step 1: The user wants the average number of episodes for seasons that meet two conditions:
- Have at least 40 episodes (numeric comparison).
- Premiered between October and December (date-based filtering).

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Convert the 'Season Premiere' column to datetime format to enable date comparisons.

Step 4: Filter rows where:
- 'Episodes' >= 40.
- The month of 'Season Premiere' is between 10 (October) and 12 (December).

Step 5: Calculate the mean of the 'Episodes' column for the filtered data.

Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Season Premiere' to datetime
df['Season Premiere'] = pd.to_datetime(df['Season Premiere'])

# Filter seasons with at least 40 episodes and premiered between October and December
filtered_df = df[(df['Episodes'] >= 40) & (df['Season Premiere'].dt.month >= 10) & (df['Season Premiere'].dt.month <= 12)]

# Calculate average episodes for filtered seasons
average_episodes = filtered_df['Episodes'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_episodes:.1f}")
```
