Step 1: The user wants to find the nation that has a total of 8 medals and 3 gold medals.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Total' is 8 and 'Gold' is 3.
Step 4: Extract the 'Nation' from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for nations with total 8 medals and 3 gold medals
filtered_nation = df[(df['Total'] == 8) & (df['Gold'] == 3)]
nation = filtered_nation['Nation'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation}")
```
