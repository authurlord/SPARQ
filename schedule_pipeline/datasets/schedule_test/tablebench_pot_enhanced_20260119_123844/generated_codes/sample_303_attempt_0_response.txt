Step 1: The user wants to find the competition where the athlete won 1st place in the 3000 m steeplechase event, and the venue is a city that is also the capital of the Bahamas.

Step 2: From the data, identify the row where the event is "3000 m steeplechase" and the position is "1st".

Step 3: Check the venue for that row. The venue is "Nassau, Bahamas". Since Nassau is the capital of the Bahamas, this satisfies the condition.

Step 4: Extract the competition name from that row.

Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where event is '3000 m steeplechase' and position is '1st'
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == '3000 m steeplechase') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Get the competition from the filtered row
competition = filtered_df['Competition'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {competition}")
```
