Step 1: The user wants the average US Chart position of songs released between 1942 and 1946.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'Year' is between 1942 and 1946 (inclusive).  
Step 4: Extract the 'US Chart position' column from the filtered data.  
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the 'US Chart position' values and print it.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Year is between 1942 and 1946 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['Year'] >= 1942) & (df['Year'] <= 1946)]
# Calculate the mean of 'US Chart position'
average_position = filtered_df['US Chart position'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_position:.1f}")
```
