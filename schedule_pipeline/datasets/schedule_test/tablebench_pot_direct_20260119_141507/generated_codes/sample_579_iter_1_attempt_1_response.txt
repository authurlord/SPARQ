Better approach:

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'year' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'], errors='coerce')
# Filter rows where year is between 2000 and 2007 (inclusive)
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2000) & (df['year'] <= 2007)]
# Ensure quantity is numeric and compute mean
quantities = pd.to_numeric(filtered_df['quantity'], errors='coerce')
mean_quantity = quantities.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_quantity:.1f}")
```
