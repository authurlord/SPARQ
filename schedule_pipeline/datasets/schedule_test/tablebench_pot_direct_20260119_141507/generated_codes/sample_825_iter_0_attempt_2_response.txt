Step 1: The user wants the mean and standard deviation of `p max (bar)` across all ammunition types.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the `p max (bar)` column.
Step 4: Compute the mean and standard deviation using pandas functions.
Step 5: Print the results.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation of 'p max ( bar )'
mean_pmax = df['p max ( bar )'].mean()
std_pmax = df['p max ( bar )'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_pmax:.2f}, {std_pmax:.2f}")
```
