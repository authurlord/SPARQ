Step 1: Identify the relevant criteria from the question:
- The work must have won an award.
- The award must be related to a song that was nominated for "Song of the Year" at the Grammy Awards.

Step 2: Filter rows where:
- The 'Association' is 'Grammy Awards'.
- The 'Category' is 'Song of the Year'.
- The 'Result' is 'Won'.

Step 3: Extract the 'Year' from the matching row.

Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Grammy Awards, Song of the Year category, and Won result
winning_song_of_year = df[(df['Association'] == 'Grammy Awards') & 
                          (df['Category'] == 'Song of the Year') & 
                          (df['Result'] == 'Won')]

# Extract the year
year = winning_song_of_year['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
