Step 1: Identify the school with the highest and lowest total medals.  
Step 2: Find the difference between the highest and lowest total medals.  
Step 3: Load the dataframe and extract the 'total medals' column.  
Step 4: Compute the maximum and minimum values, then calculate their difference.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract total medals column and find max and min
max_medals = df['total medals'].max()
min_medals = df['total medals'].min()
difference = max_medals - min_medals
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
