Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of the "% of national vote" column across all elections.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the "% of national vote" column, convert the values from strings (e.g., "39.7%") to numeric (removing the % sign and converting to float).  
Step 4: Compute the standard deviation of the cleaned values.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the '% of national vote' column and clean it by removing '%' and converting to float
national_vote_percent = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate the standard deviation
std_national_vote = national_vote_percent.std()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_national_vote:.2f}")
```
