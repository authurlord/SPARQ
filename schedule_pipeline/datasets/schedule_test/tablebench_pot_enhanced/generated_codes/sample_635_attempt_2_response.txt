Step 1: Identify intervals with a just ratio involving the number 11. These ratios are: '11:8', '14:11', '11:9', '13:11', and '12:11'.  
Step 2: Extract the 'size (cents)' values for these intervals.  
Step 3: Calculate the average of these values.  
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where just ratio contains '11'
filtered_df = df[df['just ratio'].str.contains('11', na=False)]
# Convert 'size (cents)' to float and calculate mean
average_size_cents = filtered_df['size (cents)'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_size_cents:.2f}")
```
