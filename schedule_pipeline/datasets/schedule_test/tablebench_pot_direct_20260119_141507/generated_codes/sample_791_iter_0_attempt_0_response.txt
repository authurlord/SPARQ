Step 1: The user wants the median number of aircraft movements among the top 10 busiest airports.  
Step 2: The "passengers" column can be used to determine the busiest airports (higher passengers = busier).  
Step 3: Sort the airports by passengers in descending order and take the top 10.  
Step 4: Extract the "aircraft movements" for these top 10 airports.  
Step 5: Compute the median of the "aircraft movements" values.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by passengers in descending order and take top 10
top_10 = df.sort_values(by='passengers', ascending=False).head(10)
# Extract aircraft movements for top 10
movements = top_10['aircraft movements'].astype(float)
# Calculate median
median_movements = movements.median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_movements}")
```
