Step 1: The user wants two values:
- The median total number of medals for the top 5 nations (rank 1 to 5).
- The median total number of medals for all countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the top 5 nations (rank <= 5) and compute the median of the 'total' column.
Step 4: Compute the median of the 'total' column for all nations.
Step 5: Print both medians.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Median total for top 5 nations (rank <= 5)
top_5_median = df[df['rank'].le(5)]['total'].median()

# Median total for all nations
all_median = df['total'].median()

print(f"Final Answer: {top_5_median}, {all_median}")
```
