Step 1: Filter the data for locomotives introduced between 1867 and 1873 (inclusive).
Step 2: Further filter for only the '2 - 4 - 0' and '0 - 6 - 0' types.
Step 3: Sum the 'total' column for the filtered rows.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where introduced is between 1867 and 1873 (inclusive)
filtered_df = df[(df['introduced'].astype(int) >= 1867) & (df['introduced'].astype(int) <= 1873)]
# Filter for '2 - 4 - 0' and '0 - 6 - 0' types
filtered_df = filtered_df[filtered_df['type'].isin(['2 - 4 - 0', '0 - 6 - 0'])]
# Sum the 'total' column
total_locomotives = filtered_df['total'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_locomotives}")
```
