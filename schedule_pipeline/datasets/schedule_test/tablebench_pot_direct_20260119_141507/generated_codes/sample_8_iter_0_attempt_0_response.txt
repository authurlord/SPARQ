Step 1: The user wants the average of the "% of national vote" column across all leaders.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the "% of national vote" column, which contains percentage values (e.g., "39.7%").
Step 4: Remove the '%' symbol and convert to float for calculation.
Step 5: Compute the mean and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the '% of national vote' column and remove '%' to convert to float
national_vote_percent = df['% of national vote'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Calculate the mean
avg_national_vote = national_vote_percent.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_national_vote:.2f}")
```
