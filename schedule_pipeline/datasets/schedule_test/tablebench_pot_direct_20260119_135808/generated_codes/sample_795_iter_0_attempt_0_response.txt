Step 1: The user wants to determine which year has the highest average passenger traffic across all airports and the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the columns '2008', '2009', '2010' and convert them to numeric type.
Step 4: Calculate the mean passenger traffic for each year.
Step 5: Identify the year with the highest average.
Step 6: Compute the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.
Step 7: Print both the year and the standard deviation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert the years to numeric
years = ['2008', '2009', '2010']
df[years] = df[years].apply(pd.to_numeric)

# Calculate the average passenger traffic per year
avg_traffic = df[years].mean(axis=0)
max_avg_year = avg_traffic.idxmax()

# Calculate standard deviation for the year with highest average
std_dev = df[max_avg_year].std()

print(f"Final Answer: {max_avg_year}, {std_dev:.2f}")
```
