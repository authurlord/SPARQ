Step 1: The user wants to find the year when a work won an award for a song that was nominated for Song of the Year at the Grammy Awards.

Step 2: Filter the data to find rows where:
- The 'Association' is 'Grammy Awards'
- The 'Category' is 'Song of the Year'
- The 'Result' is 'Won'

Step 3: Extract the 'Year' from the matching row.

Step 4: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Grammy Awards, Song of the Year category, and Won result
filtered_df = df[(df['Association'] == 'Grammy Awards') & 
                 (df['Category'] == 'Song of the Year') & 
                 (df['Result'] == 'Won')]

# Extract the year
year = filtered_df['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
