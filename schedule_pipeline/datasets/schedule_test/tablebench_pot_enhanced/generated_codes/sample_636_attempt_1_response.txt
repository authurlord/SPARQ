Step 1: The user wants to determine if there is a significant correlation between the area (km²) and GDP (billion USD) of the member countries.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'area (km square)' and 'gdp (billion us)' columns to numeric types.
Step 4: Calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient between the two columns.
Step 5: Interpret the result: a correlation coefficient close to 1 or -1 indicates a strong relationship, while values near 0 indicate weak or no correlation.
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient and provide a conclusion based on its magnitude.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['area (km square)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square)'])
df['gdp (billion us)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['gdp (billion us)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km square)'].corr(df['gdp (billion us)'])

# Print the correlation coefficient and conclusion
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
