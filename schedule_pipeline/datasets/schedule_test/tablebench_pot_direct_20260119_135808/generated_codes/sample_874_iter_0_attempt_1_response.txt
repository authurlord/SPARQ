Step 1: The user wants to find the difference between the number of males in the 10–29 age group and the 60+ age group.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the male population data for the 10–19 and 20–29 age groups (from row 5, columns '10–19' and '20–29') and sum them to get the total males in the 10–29 age group.
Step 4: Extract the male population for the 60–69 and 80+ age groups (from row 5, columns '60–69' and '80+') and sum them to get the total males in the 60+ age group.
Step 5: Subtract the 60+ total from the 10–29 total and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract male population for 10-29 age group (10-19 and 20-29)
males_10_29 = df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_2'] + df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_3']
# Extract male population for 60+ age group (60-69 and 80+)
males_60_plus = df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_6'] + df.iloc[5]['POPULATION (by age group in 2002)_9']
# Calculate the difference
difference = males_10_29 - males_60_plus
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
