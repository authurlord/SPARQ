We are given a table of aircraft with their introduction, retirement, seating, and notes. The question asks for the **change in total seating capacity** of the airline's fleet from 2004 to 2008.

Step-by-step analysis:

1. The "seating" column contains the seating capacity of each aircraft model.
2. We need to identify which aircraft were in service during the period 2004–2008.
3. The "introduced" and "retired" columns indicate when an aircraft entered or left service.
4. Only aircraft that were introduced **before or in 2004** and retired **after or in 2008** contribute to the fleet during that period.
5. However, the question is asking for the **total seating capacity change** from 2004 to 2008. This means:
   - Total seating capacity in 2004 (fleet at start)
   - Total seating capacity in 2008 (fleet at end)
   - Then compute the difference: (capacity in 2008) - (capacity in 2004)

But note: some aircraft were retired before 2008, and new ones introduced after 2004.

So we need to:
- Extract all aircraft with 'introduced' ≤ 2008 (i.e., available by 2008)
- Exclude those retired before 2004 (i.e., not in service in 2004)
- For 2004: only aircraft introduced before or in 2004 and retired after or in 2004
- For 2008: only aircraft introduced before or in 2008 and retired after or in 2008

However, since the data doesn't specify exact years of retirement beyond that, and many have "-" (no retirement), we interpret:
- If retired is "-", it means still in service (in service until now).
- So for 2004: include aircraft introduced ≤ 2004 and retired ≥ 2004 (or retired is "-")
- For 2008: include aircraft introduced ≤ 2008 and retired ≥ 2008 (or retired is "-")

But note: the aircraft introduced in 2004 or later are only available from their introduction year onward.

Therefore:
- In 2004: only aircraft introduced ≤ 2004 and not retired before 2004 → so retired ≥ 2004 or retired is "-"
- In 2008: only aircraft introduced ≤ 2008 and not retired before 2008 → retired ≥ 2008 or retired is "-"

Let’s define:
- Total seating in 2004 = sum of seating where introduced ≤ 2004 and (retired ≥ 2004 or retired == '-')
- Total seating in 2008 = sum of seating where introduced ≤ 2008 and (retired ≥ 2008 or retired == '-')

Then:
Change = total_seating_2008 - total_seating_2004
