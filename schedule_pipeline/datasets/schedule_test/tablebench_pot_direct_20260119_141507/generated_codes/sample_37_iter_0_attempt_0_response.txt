Step 1: The user wants the total average rating of all TV dramas.  
Step 2: The "average" column contains the average ratings.  
Step 3: Sum all values in the "average" column and divide by the number of rows to get the mean.  
Step 4: Since the question asks for "total average rating", it is interpreted as the mean (average) of the average ratings, not the sum.  

