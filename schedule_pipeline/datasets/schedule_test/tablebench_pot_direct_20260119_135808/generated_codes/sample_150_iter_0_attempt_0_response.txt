Step 1: The user is asking about the impact of urbanization on population growth rate between 1939 and 2008. To analyze this, we need to:
- Calculate the overall population growth rate from 1939 to 2008.
- Observe the trend in urban population percentage over the same period.
- Correlate urbanization (increase in urban %) with population growth.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the population values for 1939 and 2008, compute the growth rate.
Step 4: Observe the change in urban population percentage.
Step 5: Since the question asks for the "impact," we interpret this as whether urbanization increased alongside population growth, indicating a possible correlation.

Step 6: Compute population growth rate: ((Population_2008 - Population_1939) / Population_1939) * 100.
Step 7: Note the urban percentage change: from 28% in 1939 to 53% in 2008.

Step 8: Present the growth rate and urbanization trend as the answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract population in 1939 and 2008
pop_1939 = int(df[df['year (january)'] == '1939']['population (000)'].iloc[0])
pop_2008 = int(df[df['year (january)'] == '2008']['population (000)'].iloc[0])

# Calculate population growth rate
growth_rate = ((pop_2008 - pop_1939) / pop_1939) * 100

# Urban percentage change
urban_1939 = int(df[df['year (january)'] == '1939']['urban , %'].iloc[0])
urban_2008 = int(df[df['year (january)'] == '2008']['urban , %'].iloc[0])

# Output the results
print(f"Final Answer: {growth_rate:.1f}, {urban_2008 - urban_1939}")
```
