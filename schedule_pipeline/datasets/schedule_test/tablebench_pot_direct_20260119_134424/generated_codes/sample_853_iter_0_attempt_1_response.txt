Step 1: The user wants to find the years when the song with the highest and lowest US chart positions were released.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Identify the maximum and minimum values in the 'US Chart position' column.
Step 4: Find the corresponding 'Year' for both the highest and lowest US chart positions.
Step 5: Print the two years as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'US Chart position' to numeric, handling potential non-numeric entries
df['US Chart position'] = pd.to_numeric(df['US Chart position'], errors='coerce')

# Find the year with the highest US chart position (lowest numerical value)
max_position_year = df.loc[df['US Chart position'].idxmax()]['Year']

# Find the year with the lowest US chart position (highest numerical value)
min_position_year = df.loc[df['US Chart position'].idxmin()]['Year']

print(f"Final Answer: {max_position_year}, {min_position_year}")
```
