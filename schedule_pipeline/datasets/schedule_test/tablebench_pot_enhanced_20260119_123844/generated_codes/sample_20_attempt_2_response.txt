Step 1: The user wants the average total points of couples who have performed more than 10 dances.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'number of dances' > 10.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of 'total points' for the filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter couples who performed more than 10 dances
filtered_df = df[df['number of dances'] > 10]
# Calculate average total points
average_points = filtered_df['total points'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_points:.1f}")
```
