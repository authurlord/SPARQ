Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between 'density (pop / km square)' and '% change' in population.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the '% change' column to numeric, handling the '-' sign properly.
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between 'density (pop / km square)' and '% change'.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert '% change' to numeric, handling the '-' sign
df['% change'] = pd.to_numeric(df['% change'].str.replace(' - ', '-'), errors='coerce')
# Calculate correlation between 'density (pop / km square)' and '% change'
correlation = df['density (pop / km square)'].corr(df['% change'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
