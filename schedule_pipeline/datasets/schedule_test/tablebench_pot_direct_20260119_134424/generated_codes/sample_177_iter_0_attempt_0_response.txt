Step 1: The user wants to determine the impact of adding one more line on annual ridership. To assess this, we need to analyze the relationship between the number of lines and annual ridership.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'annual ridership (2012)' and 'lines' columns to numeric types for analysis.
Step 4: Compute the correlation between 'lines' and 'annual ridership (2012)' to determine the nature of the relationship.
Step 5: Interpret the correlation coefficient to decide if the impact is positive, negative, or unclear.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['annual ridership (2012)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['annual ridership (2012)'])
df['lines'] = pd.to_numeric(df['lines'])

# Calculate correlation between lines and annual ridership
correlation = df['lines'].corr(df['annual ridership (2012)'])

# Determine the impact based on correlation
if correlation > 0:
    impact = "positive"
elif correlation < 0:
    impact = "negative"
else:
    impact = "no clear impact"

print(f"Final Answer: {impact}")
```
