Step 1: Identify the parties that have at least 2 seats in the agricultural panel. From the data, these are:
- Fianna Fáil: 4 seats
- Fine Gael: 4 seats
- Labour Party: 1 seat (does not meet the threshold)
- Clann na Talmhan: 1 seat (does not meet the threshold)
- Independent: 0 seats (does not meet the threshold)

So only Fianna Fáil and Fine Gael qualify.

Step 2: Sum their total seats (from the 'total' column):
- Fianna Fáil: 25
- Fine Gael: 16
Total = 25 + 16 = 41

Step 3: Calculate the percentage of total seats (total is 60):
Percentage = (41 / 60) * 100 ≈ 68.33%

Step 4: Output the total number of seats and the percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where 'agricultural panel' >= 2
qualified_parties = df[df['agricultural panel'] >= 2]
# Sum the 'total' seats for these parties
total_seats = qualified_parties['total'].sum()
# Total seats in the system
total_system_seats = 60
# Calculate percentage
percentage = (total_seats / total_system_seats) * 100
print(f"Final Answer: {total_seats}, {percentage:.2f}")
```
