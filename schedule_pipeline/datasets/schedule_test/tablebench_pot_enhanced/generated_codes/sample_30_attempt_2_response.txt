Step 1: The user wants the total mintage of all coins from 2002 to 2012.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'mintage' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Sum the values in the 'mintage' column.
Step 5: Print the total mintage.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'mintage' column to integer and calculate total
total_mintage = df['mintage'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_mintage}")
```
