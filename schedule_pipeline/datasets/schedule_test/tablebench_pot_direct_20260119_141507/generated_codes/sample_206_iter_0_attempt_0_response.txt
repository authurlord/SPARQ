Step 1: The question asks whether an increase in eccentricity corresponds to an increase in periselene or inclination. This requires analyzing the relationship between these variables across the data points.

Step 2: We will examine the correlation between eccentricity and periselene, and between eccentricity and inclination using Pearson correlation coefficients.

Step 3: If the correlation coefficient is positive and significant, it suggests a corresponding increase. A negative correlation would suggest the opposite.

Step 4: Use pandas to compute the correlation between 'eccentricity' and 'periselene', and between 'eccentricity' and 'inclination'.

Step 5: Print the results to determine if there's a corresponding increase.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate correlation between eccentricity and periselene
corr_periselene = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['periselene'])

# Calculate correlation between eccentricity and inclination
corr_inclination = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['inclination'])

print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and periselene: {corr_periselene:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and inclination: {corr_inclination:.3f}")
Final Answer: no
```
