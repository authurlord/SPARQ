We are given a table with episode data, and we need to find the average rating of episodes that satisfy two conditions:
1. Viewership of at least 10 million (viewers in millions).
2. Ranked within the top 3 in their timeslot (timeslot rank ≤ 3).

Step-by-step approach:
- Load the dataframe.
- Convert the 'viewers (m)' column to numeric (to handle string values properly).
- Filter rows where viewership ≥ 10 and timeslot rank ≤ 3.
- Compute the average of the 'rating' column for the filtered rows.
- Print the result.

Note: The error occurred because 'viewers (m)' was being treated as string. We must convert it to float.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'viewers (m)' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['viewers (m)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['viewers (m)'], errors='coerce')

# Filter episodes with viewership >= 10 million and timeslot rank <= 3
filtered_df = df[(df['viewers (m)'] >= 10) & (df['timeslot rank'].astype(int) <= 3)]

# Calculate average rating of filtered episodes
average_rating = filtered_df['rating'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_rating:.1f}")
```
