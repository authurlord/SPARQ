Step 1: The user wants the total commissioned capacity (in MW) of power plants located in Himachal Pradesh.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'state' column is 'himachal pradesh'.
Step 4: Convert the 'commissioned capacity (mw)' column to numeric and sum the values.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where state is 'himachal pradesh'
himachal_plants = df[df['state'] == 'himachal pradesh']
# Convert 'commissioned capacity (mw)' to numeric and sum
total_capacity = himachal_plants['commissioned capacity (mw)'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_capacity}")
```
