Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between a country's UN budget and its international trade (in millions of USD).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'un budget' and 'international trade (millions of usd) 2011' columns.  
Step 4: Convert the 'un budget' column from percentage to decimal (e.g., '4.999%' → 0.04999) to ensure proper numerical correlation.  
Step 5: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient between the two columns.  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'un budget' from percentage string to decimal
df['un budget'] = df['un budget'].str.rstrip('%').astype(float) / 100.0

# Extract the two columns for correlation
un_budget = df['un budget']
international_trade = df['international trade (millions of usd) 2011']

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation_coefficient = un_budget.corr(international_trade)

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation_coefficient:.3f}")
```
