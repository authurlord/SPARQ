Step 1: The question asks to determine whether temperature T (°C) or density of air ρ (kg/m³) has a greater causal influence on the speed of sound c (m/s).  
Step 2: Since this is a physical relationship, we can analyze the correlation between each independent variable (T and ρ) and the dependent variable (c). A higher absolute correlation coefficient indicates a stronger influence.  
Step 3: Load the data, convert relevant columns to numeric, compute correlation coefficients between T and c, and between ρ and c.  
Step 4: Compare the absolute values of the correlation coefficients to determine which has greater influence.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['Temperature T (°C)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficients
corr_T_c = df['Temperature T (°C)'].corr(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
corr_rho_c = df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'].corr(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])

# Compare absolute values of correlations
if abs(corr_T_c) > abs(corr_rho_c):
    influence = 'Temperature T (°C)'
else:
    influence = 'Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'

print(f"Final Answer: {influence}")
```
