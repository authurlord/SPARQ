Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of metabolic rates for female animals and identification of any animal with a metabolic rate more than one standard deviation away from the mean.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract only the rows corresponding to female animals (excluding the last row which contains summary statistics).

Step 3: Convert 'Metabolic rate' to numeric for calculation.

Step 4: Compute the standard deviation using the formula or pandas method.

Step 5: Identify animals where the absolute difference between metabolic rate and mean is greater than one standard deviation.

Step 6: Print the standard deviation and the animal(s) that meet the condition.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for female animals (exclude the summary row)
female_data = df[df['Sex'] == 'Female'].copy()
# Convert metabolic rate to float
female_data['Metabolic rate'] = pd.to_numeric(female_data['Metabolic rate'])
# Calculate mean and standard deviation
mean_metabolic_rate = female_data['Metabolic rate'].mean()
std_dev = female_data['Metabolic rate'].std()
# Find animals more than one standard deviation away from the mean
outliers = female_data[abs(female_data['Metabolic rate'] - mean_metabolic_rate) > std_dev]
# Extract animal IDs
outlier_animals = outliers['Animal'].tolist()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_dev:.2f}, {', '.join(outlier_animals)}")
```
