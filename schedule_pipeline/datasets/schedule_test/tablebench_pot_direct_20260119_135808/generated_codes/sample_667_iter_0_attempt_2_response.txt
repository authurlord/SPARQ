Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between population growth and the decline in the number of Catholics from 1970 to 2017.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'population' and 'Catholics (based on registration by the church itself)' columns.  
Step 4: Convert population and Catholic numbers from strings to numeric (removing commas).  
Step 5: Compute the correlation coefficient between population and Catholic numbers.  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert population and Catholic numbers to numeric, removing commas
df['population'] = df['population'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)
df['Catholics (based on registration by the church itself)'] = df['Catholics (based on registration by the church itself)'].str.replace(',', '').astype(int)

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = df['population'].corr(df['Catholics (based on registration by the church itself)'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
