Step 1: Understand the question. We are looking for rivers that converge with the Lahn river at a confluence point below 100 km. The "Confluence [by Lahn-km]" column represents the distance from the Lahn river where the river meets it. We need to filter rows where this value is less than 100 km.

Step 2: Extract the "Length [km]" column and compute the average of those rivers that meet the condition (confluence < 100 km).

Step 3: Filter the data based on the confluence value and calculate the mean of the "Length [km]" for the filtered rows.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rivers where confluence is below 100 km
filtered_rivers = df[df['Confluence [by Lahn-km]'].fillna(0).astype(float) < 100]
# Calculate the average length of these rivers
average_length = filtered_rivers['Length [km]'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_length:.1f}")
```
