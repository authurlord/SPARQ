Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in 'property taxes' from 2000 to 2005.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'property taxes' column to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the difference between the property taxes in 2005 and 2000.
Step 5: Divide by the number of years (5) to get the average annual increase.
Step 6: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'property taxes' to numeric
df['property taxes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['property taxes'])
# Calculate the average annual increase
start_value = df[df['year'] == '2000']['property taxes'].values[0]
end_value = df[df['year'] == '2005']['property taxes'].values[0]
average_increase = (end_value - start_value) / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {average_increase:.0f}")
```
