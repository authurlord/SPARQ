Step 1: Extract the GDP per capita values for West Germany and France from the table.
Step 2: Calculate the difference between France's GDP per capita and West Germany's GDP per capita.
Step 3: Since the question asks for the minimum increase required for West Germany to surpass France, the increase needed is the difference plus a small amount (just enough to exceed). However, since we are dealing with exact values, the minimum increase is simply the difference between the two values.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract GDP per capita for West Germany and France
gdp_per_capita_west_germany = float(df[df['member countries'] == 'west germany']['gdp per capita (us)'].values[0])
gdp_per_capita_france = float(df[df['member countries'] == 'france']['gdp per capita (us)'].values[0])

# Calculate the minimum increase required
min_increase = gdp_per_capita_france - gdp_per_capita_west_germany

print(f"Final Answer: {min_increase:.3f}")
```
