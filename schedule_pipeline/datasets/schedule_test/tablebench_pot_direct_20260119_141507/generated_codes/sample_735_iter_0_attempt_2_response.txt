Step 1: The user wants to find the city with the highest density and compare it to the city with the lowest density.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Identify the city with the maximum and minimum density values.  
Step 4: Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest density.  
Step 5: Print the city with the highest density and the difference.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the city with the highest and lowest density
max_density_row = df.loc[df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].idxmax()]
min_density_row = df.loc[df['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'].idxmin()]

highest_density_city = max_density_row['city']
lowest_density_city = min_density_row['city']
density_difference = max_density_row['density (inhabitants / km 2 )'] - min_density_row['density (inhabitants / km 2 )']

print(f"Final Answer: {highest_density_city}, {density_difference:.1f}")
```
