Step 1: The question asks to identify earthquakes with abnormal data points, specifically in terms of local magnitude, which may deviate significantly from the overall trend.  
Step 2: We will analyze the "local magnitude" column and look for outliers — values that are unusually high or low compared to the rest.  
Step 3: Use statistical methods such as the interquartile range (IQR) to detect outliers.  
Step 4: Identify rows where the local magnitude is an outlier and list them.  

Note: Since the dataset has only one "local magnitude" column, we focus on identifying extreme values (e.g., > 10 or < 3) that might indicate abnormality. Also, note that the highest recorded magnitude is 10.5 (on May 3, 1949), which is unusually high.  

We will compute the IQR and flag any magnitude outside the range [Q1 - 1.5*IQR, Q3 + 1.5*IQR].  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert local magnitude to float
df['local magnitude'] = pd.to_numeric(df['local magnitude'], errors='coerce')

# Compute IQR-based outliers
Q1 = df['local magnitude'].quantile(0.25)
Q3 = df['local magnitude'].quantile(0.75)
IQR = Q3 - Q1
lower_bound = Q1 - 1.5 * IQR
upper_bound = Q3 + 1.5 * IQR

# Find outliers
outliers = df[(df['local magnitude'] < lower_bound) | (df['local magnitude'] > upper_bound)]

# Display the rows with abnormal data points
print(outliers[['date', 'local magnitude', 'location']])
```
