Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Check for correlation between 'area (km²)' and 'population'.
Step 4: If the correlation is strong (e.g., Pearson correlation > 0.7), then 'area (km²)' significantly influences population; otherwise, conclude no clear impact.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert 'area (km 2 )' and 'population' to numeric
df['area (km 2 )'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km 2 )'])
df['population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population'])

# Calculate correlation between area and population
correlation = df['area (km 2 )'].corr(df['population'])

# Check if correlation is significant (absolute value > 0.7)
if abs(correlation) > 0.7:
    print("Final Answer: area (km 2 )")
else:
    print("Final Answer: no clear impact")
```
