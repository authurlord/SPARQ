Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the crude birth rate exceeded the crude death rate by the largest margin.  
Step 2: Extract the "Crude birth rate (per 1000)" and "Crude death rate (per 1000)" columns.  
Step 3: Calculate the difference (birth rate - death rate) for each year.  
Step 4: Identify the year with the maximum positive difference.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the difference between crude birth rate and crude death rate
df['difference'] = df['Crude birth rate (per 1000)'] - df['Crude death rate (per 1000)']
# Find the year with the largest positive difference
max_diff_year = df[df['difference'] == df['difference'].max()]['Unnamed: 0'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {max_diff_year}")
```
