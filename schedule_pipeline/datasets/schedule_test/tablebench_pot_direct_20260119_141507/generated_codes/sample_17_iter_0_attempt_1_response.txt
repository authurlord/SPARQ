Step 1: The user wants the total number of medals won by all nations, which is the sum of the 'total' column in the table.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Sum the values in the 'total' column.  
Step 4: Print the result.  

```python

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
```
