Step 1: The user is asking about the relationship between GDP (PPP) per capita and metropolitan population. Specifically, they want to know how GDP per capita changes as population increases.

Step 2: We need to analyze the trend — whether GDP per capita increases, decreases, or remains stable as metropolitan population increases.

Step 3: Load the data and examine the correlation between "metropolitan population (2006) millions" and "gdp (ppp) us per capita".

Step 4: Since the question is about a trend (how it changes), we can compute the correlation coefficient to determine the nature of the relationship.

Step 5: Print the correlation value to indicate the strength and direction of the relationship.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between metropolitan population and GDP per capita
correlation = df['metropolitan population (2006) millions'].corr(df['gdp (ppp) us per capita'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
