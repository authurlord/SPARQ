Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the crude birth rate exceeded the crude death rate by the largest margin.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Calculate the difference between 'Crude birth rate (per 1000)' and 'Crude death rate (per 1000)' for each row.
Step 4: Identify the year with the maximum positive difference.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the difference between birth rate and death rate
df['difference'] = df['Crude birth rate (per 1000)'] - df['Crude death rate (per 1000)']
# Find the year with the maximum positive difference
max_diff_year = df.loc[df['difference'].idxmax(), 'Unnamed: 0']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_diff_year}")
```
