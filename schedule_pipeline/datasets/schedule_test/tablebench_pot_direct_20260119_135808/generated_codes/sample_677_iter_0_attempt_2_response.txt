Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand' across the autonomous communities.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand' columns to numeric, handling any non-numeric values (like percentages).
Step 4: Calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient between these two columns.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total renewable generation' and 'total electricity demand' to numeric
df['total renewable generation'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total renewable generation'], errors='coerce')
df['total electricity demand'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total electricity demand'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with NaN values in either column
df.dropna(subset=['total renewable generation', 'total electricity demand'], inplace=True)

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = df['total renewable generation'].corr(df['total electricity demand'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
