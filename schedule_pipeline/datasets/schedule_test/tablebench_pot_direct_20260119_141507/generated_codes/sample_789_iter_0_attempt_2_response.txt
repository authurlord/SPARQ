Step 1: The user wants the mean and standard deviation of the "Year built" column.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the "Year built" column.  
Step 4: Calculate the mean and standard deviation using pandas.  
Step 5: Print both values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate mean and standard deviation of 'Year built'
mean_year = df['Year built'].mean()
std_year = df['Year built'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_year:.1f}, {std_year:.1f}")
```
