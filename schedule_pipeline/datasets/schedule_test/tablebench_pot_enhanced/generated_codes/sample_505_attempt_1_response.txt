Step 1: The Hurricane Severity Index (HSI) is defined as (number of hurricanes + number of major hurricanes) / number of tropical storms.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Calculate HSI for each year.  
Step 4: Identify the year with the highest HSI.  
Step 5: Output the year with the highest HSI.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate HSI: (hurricanes + major hurricanes) / tropical storms
df['HSI'] = (df['number of hurricanes'] + df['number of major hurricanes']) / df['number of tropical storms']
# Find the year with the maximum HSI
max_hsi_year = df.loc[df['HSI'].idxmax(), 'year']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_hsi_year}")
```
