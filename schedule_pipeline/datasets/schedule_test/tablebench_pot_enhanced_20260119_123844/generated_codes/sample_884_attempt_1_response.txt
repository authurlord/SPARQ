Step 1: The user wants to identify the year with a higher percentage increase in domestic steel tonnage compared to foreign total steel tonnage. This requires calculating the percentage change from one year to the next for both "domestic s ton" and "foreign total s ton", then comparing them.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the relevant columns to numeric (they are stored as strings).
Step 4: Calculate the year-over-year percentage increase for both "domestic s ton" and "foreign total s ton".
Step 5: Compare the two percentage increases for each year and find the year where domestic increase is greater than foreign increase.
Step 6: Return the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['domestic s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['domestic s ton'])
df['foreign total s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['foreign total s ton'])

# Calculate percentage increase year-over-year
df['domestic_pct_increase'] = df['domestic s ton'].pct_change() * 100
df['foreign_total_pct_increase'] = df['foreign total s ton'].pct_change() * 100

# Find the year where domestic increase > foreign total increase
# Skip the first row (no previous year for comparison)
result_year = df.loc[df['domestic_pct_increase'] > df['foreign_total_pct_increase'], 'year'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {result_year}")
```
