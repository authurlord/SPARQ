Step 1: The user wants to find the season in which Liverpool scored the highest total number of goals in the Premier League.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where Club is 'Liverpool' and Season is not a total row (i.e., exclude 'Liverpool Total').  
Step 4: Focus on the 'League_2' column (Premier League goals) and find the maximum value.  
Step 5: Identify the corresponding season for that maximum value.  
Step 6: Print the season with the highest Premier League goals.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Liverpool and exclude the total row
liverpool_data = df[df['Club'] == 'Liverpool']
liverpool_data = liverpool_data[liverpool_data['Season'] != 'Liverpool Total']

# Find the season with the highest goals in Premier League (League_2)
max_goals_season = liverpool_data.loc[liverpool_data['League_2'].idxmax()]['Season']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_goals_season}")
```
