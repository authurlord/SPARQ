Step 1: The user wants to find the first year where the 'Political Rights' score decreased by at least 2 points compared to the previous year.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'Year' and 'Political Rights' columns to numeric for comparison.
Step 4: Calculate the difference between consecutive years' 'Political Rights' scores.
Step 5: Identify the first year where this difference is less than or equal to -2 (a decrease of at least 2 points).
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Year' and 'Political Rights' to numeric
df['Year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Year'])
df['Political Rights'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Political Rights'])

# Calculate the difference in Political Rights from the previous year
df['diff'] = df['Political Rights'].diff()

# Find the first year where the score decreased by at least 2
first_decrease_year = df[df['diff'] <= -2]['Year'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {first_decrease_year}")
```
