Step 1: The user wants to calculate the correlation coefficient between the UN budget (as a percentage) and international trade (in millions of USD).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'un budget' column from percentage strings to float values.
Step 4: Use pandas to compute the correlation coefficient between 'un budget' and 'international trade (millions of usd) 2011'.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'un budget' from percentage string to float
df['un budget'] = df['un budget'].str.rstrip('%').astype(float)
# Calculate correlation between 'un budget' and 'international trade (millions of usd) 2011'
correlation = df['un budget'].corr(df['international trade (millions of usd) 2011'].astype(float))
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
