Step 1: The user is asking whether there is a positive correlation between the total number of USAAF personnel and the number of personnel stationed overseas.  
Step 2: We will examine the relationship between "total usaaf" and "overseas" across the given dates.  
Step 3: Compute the correlation coefficient between these two variables to determine if an increase in total personnel corresponds to an increase in overseas personnel.  
Step 4: A positive correlation coefficient (close to 1) indicates that as total personnel increase, overseas personnel also increase.  

We will use pandas to load the data and calculate the correlation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between total usaaf and overseas personnel
correlation = df['total usaaf'].corr(df['overseas'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
