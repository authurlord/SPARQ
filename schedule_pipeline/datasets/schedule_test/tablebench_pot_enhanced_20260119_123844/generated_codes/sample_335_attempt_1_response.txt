Step 1: The user wants to find the year and points score when the athlete won a gold medal (1st position) in the Heptathlon event at the South American Championships in Lima, Peru.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter the data where:
- 'Event' is 'Heptathlon'
- 'Venue' is 'Lima, Peru'
- 'Position' is '1st'

Step 4: Extract the 'Year' and 'Notes' (which contains the points score).

Step 5: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Heptathlon, Lima, Peru, and 1st position
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == 'Heptathlon') & (df['Venue'] == 'Lima, Peru') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Extract the year and points score
year = filtered_df['Year'].iloc[0]
points = filtered_df['Notes'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}, {points}")
```
