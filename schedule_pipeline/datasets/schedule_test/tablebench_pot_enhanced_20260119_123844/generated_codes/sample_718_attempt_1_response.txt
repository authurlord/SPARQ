Step 1: The user wants to identify the university with the highest research funding and calculate the difference between the highest and lowest research funding.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'research funding (000)' column and find the maximum and minimum values.
Step 4: Identify the university with the highest research funding.
Step 5: Calculate the difference between the highest and lowest funding.
Step 6: Print the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the university with the highest research funding
max_funding = df['research funding (000)'].max()
min_funding = df['research funding (000)'].min()
difference = max_funding - min_funding
# Find the institution with the highest research funding
highest_institution = df.loc[df['research funding (000)'] == max_funding, 'institution'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {highest_institution}, {difference}")
```
