Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'magnitude' and 'depth' of earthquakes.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'magnitude' and 'depth (km)' columns to numeric types.
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Magnitude' and 'Depth (km)' to float
df['Magnitude'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Magnitude'], errors='coerce')
df['Depth (km)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Depth (km)'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['Magnitude'].corr(df['Depth (km)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
