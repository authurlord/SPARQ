Step 1: The question asks whether the age of the wife at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has. This requires analyzing the relationship between "her age" and "of children".

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the relevant columns: "her age" and "of children".

Step 3: Perform a simple correlation analysis to check if there is a linear relationship between the two variables.

Step 4: Since the dataset is small, we can compute the correlation coefficient (Pearson) to assess the strength and direction of the relationship. A high absolute value (close to 1 or -1) indicates a strong relationship.

Step 5: Interpret the result — if the correlation is significantly high, it suggests a meaningful impact.

Note: We do not have statistical significance testing (like p-values) in this small dataset without additional assumptions, so we rely on correlation as an indicator.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract the columns of interest
wife_age = df['her age']
children = df['of children']

# Calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient
correlation = wife_age.corr(children)

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
