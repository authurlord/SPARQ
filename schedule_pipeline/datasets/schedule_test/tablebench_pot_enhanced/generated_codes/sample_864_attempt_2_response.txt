Step 1: The user wants to find the difference in years of service between 'Empress of Austria' and 'Cyclops'.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows for 'Empress of Austria' and 'Cyclops' based on the 'Name' column.
Step 4: Extract the 'Introduced' and 'Withdrawn' years for both locomotives.
Step 5: Calculate the service duration (Withdrawn - Introduced) for each.
Step 6: Compute the difference in service years and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for the two locomotives
empress = df[df['Name'] == 'Empress of Austria']
cyclops = df[df['Name'] == 'Cyclops']

# Extract introduced and withdrawn years
empress_intro = int(empress['Introduced'].values[0])
empress_withdrawn = int(empress['Withdrawn'].values[0])
cyclops_intro = int(cyclops['Introduced'].values[0])
cyclops_withdrawn = int(cyclops['Withdrawn'].values[0])

# Calculate service years
empress_service = empress_withdrawn - empress_intro
cyclops_service = cyclops_withdrawn - cyclops_intro

# Find the difference
difference = empress_service - cyclops_service
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
