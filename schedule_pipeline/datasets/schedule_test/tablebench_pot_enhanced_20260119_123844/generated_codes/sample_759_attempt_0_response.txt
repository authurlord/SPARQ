Step 1: The user wants to project the population of Tabriz in 2026 based on historical growth from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 2: Extract the population data for Tabriz (rank 4) from the years 1956 and 2006.  
Step 3: Calculate the average annual growth rate between 1956 and 2006.  
Step 4: Use this growth rate to project the population for 2026 (20 years after 2006).  
Step 5: Use the formula: projected population = current population × (1 + growth rate)^number of years.  
Step 6: Print the final projected value.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract population data for Tabriz (row with rank 4)
tabriz_data = df[df['rank'] == '4']
pop_1956 = int(tabriz_data['1956'].values[0])
pop_2006 = int(tabriz_data['2006'].values[0])

# Calculate average annual growth rate
years = 2006 - 1956
growth_rate = (pop_2006 / pop_1956) ** (1 / years) - 1

# Project population for 2026 (20 years after 2006)
projected_pop_2026 = pop_2006 * (1 + growth_rate) ** 20

print(f"Final Answer: {projected_pop_2026:.0f}")
```
