Step 1: Filter the data for 'The Wire' and years between 2002 and 2004 (inclusive).
Step 2: Group by 'season' and count the number of episodes per season.
Step 3: Calculate the average number of episodes per season.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 'The Wire' and years between 2002 and 2004
the_wire_2002_2004 = df[(df['show'] == 'the wire') & (df['year'].astype(int) >= 2002) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2004)]
# Group by season and count episodes
episodes_per_season = the_wire_2002_2004.groupby('season').size()
# Calculate average episodes per season
avg_episodes = episodes_per_season.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_episodes:.1f}")
```
