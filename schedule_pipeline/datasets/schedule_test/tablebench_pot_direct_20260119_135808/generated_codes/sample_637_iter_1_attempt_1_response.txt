Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the number of applications and the offer rate from 2013 to 2017.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Clean the 'Applications' and 'Offer Rate (%)' columns by removing commas and converting to numeric.
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient between these two columns.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Clean and convert 'Applications' and 'Offer Rate (%)' columns to numeric
df['Applications'] = df['Applications'].str.replace(',', '').astype(float)
df['Offer Rate (%)'] = df['Offer Rate (%)'].astype(float)

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['Applications'].corr(df['Offer Rate (%)'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.3f}")
```
