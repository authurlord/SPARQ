Step 1: The user is asking to predict viewership and BBC Three weekly ranking for a hypothetical episode 9 based on historical data from episodes 1 to 8.  
Step 2: Since this is a prediction, we need to analyze trends in viewership and ranking over time.  
Step 3: We can calculate the average viewership and average BBC Three weekly ranking from the available data (episodes 1–8) and use those as a baseline for episode 9.  
Step 4: This is a simple linear extrapolation using mean values — no complex modeling is needed given the lack of clear trend patterns or external variables.  

We will:
- Load the dataframe.
- Extract viewership and BBC Three weekly ranking.
- Compute the mean of viewership and ranking.
- Present the predicted values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate average viewership and BBC Three weekly ranking
avg_viewership = df['viewers'].mean()
avg_ranking = df['bbc three weekly ranking'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_viewership:.0f}, {avg_ranking:.0f}")
```
