The error occurred because the row labeled "total" contains a string "total" in the "year" column, and we are trying to filter rows from 2005 to 2009. We need to exclude the "total" row when filtering.

Step 1: Load the dataframe.
Step 2: Filter rows where the year is between 2005 and 2009 (inclusive), excluding the "total" row.
Step 3: Calculate the average of the 'apps' column for those filtered rows.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where year is between 2005 and 2009 (exclusive of 'total' row)
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].str.isdigit()) & (df['year'].astype(int) >= 2005) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 2009)]
# Calculate average appearances (apps)
avg_apps = filtered_df['apps'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_apps:.1f}")
```
