Step 1: The user wants to determine which year has the highest average passenger traffic across all airports, and then compute the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the passenger traffic columns (2008, 2009, 2010) and convert them to numeric.
Step 4: Compute the average passenger traffic per year for all airports.
Step 5: Identify the year with the highest average.
Step 6: Calculate the standard deviation of passenger traffic for that year.
Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert the traffic columns to numeric
traffic_cols = ['2008', '2009', '2010']
df[traffic_cols] = df[traffic_cols].apply(pd.to_numeric, errors='coerce')

# Calculate average passenger traffic per year
annual_avg = df[traffic_cols].mean(axis=0)

# Find the year with the highest average
max_year = annual_avg.idxmax()
max_avg = annual_avg[max_year]

# Calculate standard deviation for that year
std_dev = df[max_year].std()

print(f"Final Answer: {max_year}, {std_dev:.1f}")
```
