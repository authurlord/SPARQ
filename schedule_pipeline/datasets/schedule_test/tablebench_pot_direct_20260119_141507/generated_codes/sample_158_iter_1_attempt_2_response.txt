The error occurs because the "capacity in use" column contains string values with spaces (e.g., '126 , 06%'), which cannot be directly converted to float. We need to clean this column by removing the space and comma, then converting to float.

Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the dataframe.
2. Clean the "capacity in use" column by removing spaces and commas and converting to float.
3. Extract the "annual change" and "capacity in use" columns.
4. Analyze the relationship: we are looking for a general impact — is there a trend? For example, does higher annual change correlate with higher capacity in use?
5. Since the question asks for "impact", we can compute the correlation between "annual change" and "capacity in use" as a quantitative measure of the relationship.

Note: The data has some entries like '126 , 06%' — this appears to be a typo or formatting issue. We assume it's meant to be '126.06%' or '126.06'. We'll remove the space and comma and convert to float (interpreting as percentage value).

We will:
- Convert "annual change" to float (already in %, so just remove % sign).
- Convert "capacity in use" to float after cleaning.
- Compute the correlation between them.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean 'capacity in use' column: remove space and comma, and convert to float
df['capacity in use'] = df['capacity in use'].str.replace(' ', '').str.replace(',', '.').str.rstrip('%').astype(float)

# Convert 'annual change' to float (remove %)
df['annual change'] = df['annual change'].str.rstrip('%').astype(float)

# Compute correlation between annual change and capacity in use
correlation = df['annual change'].corr(df['capacity in use'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
