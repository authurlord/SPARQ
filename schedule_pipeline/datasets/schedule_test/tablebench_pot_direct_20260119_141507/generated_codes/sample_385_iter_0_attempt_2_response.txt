Step 1: The user wants the total number of coins issued between the years 2000 and 2005 (inclusive).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'year' is between 2000 and 2005.  
Step 4: Sum the 'mintage' column for those filtered rows.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years between 2000 and 2005 inclusive
filtered_df = df[(df['year'] >= 2000) & (df['year'] <= 2005)]
# Sum the mintage
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_mintage}")
```
