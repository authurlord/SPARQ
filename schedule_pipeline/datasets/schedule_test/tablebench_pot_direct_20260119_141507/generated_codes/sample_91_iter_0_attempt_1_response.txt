Step 1: The user wants to identify the team with the lowest points.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Look at the 'points' column and find the team with the minimum value.  
Step 4: Print the name of that team.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the team with the least points
least_points_team = df.loc[df['points'].idxmin(), 'team']
print(f"Final Answer: {least_points_team}")
```
