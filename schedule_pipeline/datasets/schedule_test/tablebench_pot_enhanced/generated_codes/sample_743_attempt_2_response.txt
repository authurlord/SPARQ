Step 1: The user is asking for a prediction of viewership and 18-49 average rating for season 9 based on the trend from season 1 to season 8.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the 'viewers (in millions)' and '18 - 49 average' columns.

Step 3: Perform a simple linear regression or observe the trend (e.g., decreasing trend) to estimate the next values.

Step 4: Since the data shows a clear downward trend, we can calculate the average rate of decrease per season and project it to season 9.

Step 5: Use the last known values and subtract the average decrease to estimate season 9.

Step 6: Output the predicted values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract the relevant columns
viewers = df['viewers (in millions)'].astype(float)
avg_18_49 = df['18 - 49 average'].astype(float)

# Calculate the average decrease per season
viewers_decrease = viewers.diff().mean()
avg_18_49_decrease = avg_18_49.diff().mean()

# Predict season 9 values
predicted_viewers = viewers.iloc[-1] + viewers_decrease
predicted_avg_18_49 = avg_18_49.iloc[-1] + avg_18_49_decrease

print(f"Final Answer: {predicted_viewers:.2f}, {predicted_avg_18_49:.2f}")
```
