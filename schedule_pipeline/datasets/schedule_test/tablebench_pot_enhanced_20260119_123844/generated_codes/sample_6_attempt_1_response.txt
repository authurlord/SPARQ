Step 1: The user wants the total number of years served by all mayors.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Convert 'Taking Office' and 'Leaving' columns to integers (years).  
Step 4: Calculate the difference between 'Leaving' and 'Taking Office' for each mayor.  
Step 5: Sum all the years served.  
Step 6: Print the total.  

Note: For the current mayor (Michel Angers), "Current" is treated as the current year (2024) for calculation purposes.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Taking Office' and 'Leaving' to integers; treat 'Current' as 2024
df['Taking Office'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Taking Office'], errors='coerce')
df['Leaving'] = df['Leaving'].replace('Current', 2024)
df['Leaving'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Leaving'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate years served
df['Years Served'] = df['Leaving'] - df['Taking Office']

# Sum total years served
total_years = df['Years Served'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {int(total_years)}")
```
