Step 1: The user is asking whether there is a clear relationship (positive, negative, or no clear impact) between the area of a region and its population.  
Step 2: We need to analyze the correlation between "area (km square)" and "population".  
Step 3: Load the dataframe and compute the correlation coefficient between these two columns.  
Step 4: Based on the correlation value, determine if it indicates a positive, negative, or no clear impact. A correlation close to 1 indicates a strong positive relationship, close to -1 indicates a strong negative relationship, and close to 0 indicates no clear relationship.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between area (km square) and population
correlation = df['area (km square)'].corr(df['population'])
print(f"Final Answer: {'positive' if correlation > 0.5 else 'negative' if correlation < -0.5 else 'no clear impact'}")
```
