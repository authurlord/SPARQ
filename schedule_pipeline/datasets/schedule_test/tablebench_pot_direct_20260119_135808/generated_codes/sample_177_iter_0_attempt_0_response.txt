Step 1: The user wants to determine the impact of adding one more line on annual ridership. To analyze this, we need to examine the relationship between the number of lines and annual ridership.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'annual ridership (2012)' and 'lines' columns to numeric for analysis.
Step 4: Calculate the correlation between 'lines' and 'annual ridership (2012)' to assess the relationship.
Step 5: Based on the correlation coefficient, determine if the impact is positive, negative, or no clear impact.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['annual ridership (2012)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['annual ridership (2012)'])
df['lines'] = pd.to_numeric(df['lines'])

# Calculate correlation between lines and annual ridership
correlation = df['lines'].corr(df['annual ridership (2012)'])

# Determine impact based on correlation
if correlation > 0:
    impact = "positive"
elif correlation < 0:
    impact = "negative"
else:
    impact = "no clear impact"

print(f"Final Answer: {impact}")
```
