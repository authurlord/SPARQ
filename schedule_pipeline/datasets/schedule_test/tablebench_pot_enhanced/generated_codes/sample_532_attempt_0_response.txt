Step 1: The question asks for the average length of rivers that converge with the Lahn river at a confluence point below 100 km.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Confluence [by Lahn-km]' is less than 100 and not null.
Step 4: Extract the 'Length [km]' column, convert to float, and calculate the mean.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where confluence is below 100 km and not null
filtered_df = df[df['Confluence [by Lahn-km]'].notna() & (df['Confluence [by Lahn-km]'] < 100)]
# Convert Length [km] to float and calculate average
average_length = filtered_df['Length [km]'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_length:.1f}")
```
