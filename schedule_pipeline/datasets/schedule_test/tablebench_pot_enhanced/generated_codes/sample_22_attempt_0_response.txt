Step 1: The user wants the total number of people deported from all regions.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Select the 'number of people' column and convert it to integers.  
Step 4: Sum the values and print the total.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'number of people' to integer and sum
total_people = df['number of people'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_people}")
```
