Step 1: The user wants to identify the trend in the issue price of coins over time.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'year' and 'issue price' columns.
Step 4: Convert 'issue price' to float for numerical analysis.
Step 5: Plot the issue price over the years to observe the trend.
Step 6: Based on the trend, infer what might happen in the next 5 years.
Step 7: Since the data shows a consistent increase in issue price each year, we can expect the trend to continue.

```python
import pandas as pd
import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'issue price' to float
df['issue price'] = pd.to_numeric(df['issue price'], errors='coerce')

# Plot the trend of issue price over the years
plt.figure(figsize=(10, 6))
plt.plot(df['year'], df['issue price'], marker='o', linestyle='-', color='b')
plt.title('Trend of Issue Price Over the Years')
plt.xlabel('Year')
plt.ylabel('Issue Price ($)')
plt.grid(True)
plt.xticks(rotation=45)
plt.tight_layout()
plt.show()

# Analyze the trend: The issue price increases steadily
# Based on historical data, the trend is upward
print("Final Answer: increasing")
```
