Step 1: Identify unusual patterns or outliers in the data.  
The key observation is in the "viewers" column. Most episodes have viewers in the range of 300,000 to 500,000, but one episode — "don't walk on the grass" (episode 117) — has 2,000,000 viewers, which is significantly higher than others. This appears to be an outlier.  
Additionally, the timeslot for episode 125 ("the glamorous life") is unusual — it airs at "3:00 am - 4:00 am", which is very early and different from the typical 8:30 pm - 9:30 pm slot. This could also be considered a pattern or anomaly.

Step 2: Analyze the data to confirm these anomalies.  
We will:
- Check the "viewers" column for values far above the average.
- Check the "timeslot" column for unusual entries (e.g., non-standard times).

Step 3: Use Python to detect outliers and flag them.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Identify the episode with unusually high viewers
max_viewers = df['viewers'].max()
outlier_episode = df[df['viewers'] == max_viewers]['title'].values[0]

# Identify unusual timeslot (not in the standard 8:30 pm - 9:30 pm range)
standard_timeslot = '8:30 pm - 9:30 pm'
unusual_timeslot = df[df['timeslot'] != standard_timeslot]['timeslot'].values[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {outlier_episode}, {unusual_timeslot}")
```
