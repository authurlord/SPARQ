Step 1: The question asks for the year with the highest proportion of AIDS-related orphans among all orphans. This can be determined by analyzing the 'aids orphans as % of orphans' column.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'country' and 'aids orphans as % of orphans' columns.
Step 4: Identify the maximum percentage in the 'aids orphans as % of orphans' column and determine the corresponding year from the 'country' column (e.g., 'botswana (2001)', 'lesotho (2001)', etc.).
Step 5: Return the year (the part in parentheses) corresponding to the maximum percentage.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'aids orphans as % of orphans' to float for comparison
df['aids orphans as % of orphans'] = pd.to_numeric(df['aids orphans as % of orphans'], errors='coerce')
# Find the row with the maximum percentage
max_row = df.loc[df['aids orphans as % of orphans'].idxmax()]
# Extract the year from the country column
year = max_row['country'].split('(')[1].split(')')[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
