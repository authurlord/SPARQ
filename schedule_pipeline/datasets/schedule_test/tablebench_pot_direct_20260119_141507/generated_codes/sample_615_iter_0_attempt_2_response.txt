Step 1: Identify universities located in London from the "location" column.  
Step 2: Filter the rows where "location" is "london".  
Step 3: Sum the "total number of students" for those filtered rows.  
Step 4: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter universities located in London and sum the total number of students
london_students = df[df['location'] == 'london']['total number of students'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {london_students}")
```
