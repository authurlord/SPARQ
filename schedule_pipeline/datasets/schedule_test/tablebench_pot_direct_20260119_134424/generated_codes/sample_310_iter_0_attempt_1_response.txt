Step 1: The user wants to find the year Barbara Stanwyck won an Emmy Award for a TV series.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Association' is 'Emmy Awards', 'Category' contains 'Series', and 'Result' is 'Won'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the filtered row.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Emmy Awards for a TV series where she won
filtered_df = df[(df['Association'] == 'Emmy Awards') & 
                 (df['Category'].str.contains('Series', na=False)) & 
                 (df['Result'] == 'Won')]
# Get the year
year = filtered_df['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
