The error occurred because the "total points" column contains string values (like '433', '397') that were not properly converted to numeric when summed or averaged. We need to ensure that the "total points" column is converted to numeric before computing the average.

Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the dataframe.
2. Convert the "total points" column to numeric, handling any parsing errors.
3. Filter couples who have performed more than 10 dances (i.e., "number of dances" > 10).
4. Compute the average total points of the filtered couples.
5. Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total points' to numeric, coercing errors to NaN
df['total points'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total points'], errors='coerce')

# Filter couples with more than 10 dances
filtered_couples = df[df['number of dances'] > 10]

# Calculate average total points of filtered couples
average_points = filtered_couples['total points'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {average_points:.1f}")
```
