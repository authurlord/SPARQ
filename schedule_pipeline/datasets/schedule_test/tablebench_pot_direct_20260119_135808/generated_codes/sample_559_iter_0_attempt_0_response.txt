Step 1: Filter the buildings constructed between 1895 and 1980 (inclusive) based on the 'year' column.  
Step 2: Extract the 'floors' column for these buildings.  
Step 3: Convert the 'floors' values to integers and calculate the average.  
Step 4: Print the result as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where year is between 1895 and 1980 (inclusive)
filtered_df = df[(df['year'].astype(int) >= 1895) & (df['year'].astype(int) <= 1980)]
# Convert 'floors' to integer and calculate average
avg_floors = filtered_df['floors'].astype(int).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_floors:.1f}")
```
