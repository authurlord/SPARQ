Step 1: The user wants the total commissioned capacity of all power plants located in Himachal Pradesh.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'state' column is 'himachal pradesh'.  
Step 4: Sum the 'commissioned capacity (mw)' for these filtered rows.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter power plants in Himachal Pradesh and sum their commissioned capacity
total_capacity_hp = df[df['state'] == 'himachal pradesh']['commissioned capacity (mw)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_capacity_hp}")
```
