Step 1: The user wants to find the year with a higher percentage increase in domestic steel tonnage compared to foreign total steel tonnage. This requires calculating the year-over-year percentage increase for both 'domestic s ton' and 'foreign total s ton', then comparing them.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and convert relevant columns to numeric.

Step 3: Calculate the year-over-year percentage increase for both domestic and foreign total steel tonnage.

Step 4: Compare the percentage increases and identify the year where domestic had a higher increase than foreign.

Step 5: Output the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert necessary columns to numeric
df['domestic s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['domestic s ton'])
df['foreign total s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['foreign total s ton'])

# Calculate year-over-year percentage increase
df['domestic_pct_increase'] = df['domestic s ton'].pct_change() * 100
df['foreign_total_pct_increase'] = df['foreign total s ton'].pct_change() * 100

# Identify the year where domestic increase > foreign total increase (excluding first year)
comparison = df[df['year'] != '2000']  # Skip the first year (no prior year for comparison)
result_year = comparison[comparison['domestic_pct_increase'] > comparison['foreign_total_pct_increase']]['year'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {result_year}")
```
