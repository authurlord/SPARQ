Step 1: The user wants the total average viewership of the top 5 TV shows.  
Step 2: The "hk viewers" column contains the viewership data.  
Step 3: We need to sum the "hk viewers" for the top 5 shows (rank 1 to 5).  
Step 4: Extract the relevant rows and sum the viewership values.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Select top 5 shows (rank 1 to 5) and sum their hk viewers
total_viewership = df[df['rank'].isin(range(1, 6))]['hk viewers'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_viewership}")
```
