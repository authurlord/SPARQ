Step 1: The user wants the average melamine content for products where at least 5 samples were taken.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'samples taken' >= 5.
Step 4: Calculate the mean of 'melamine content (mg/kg)' for the filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where samples taken is at least 5
filtered_df = df[df['samples taken'] >= 5]
# Calculate average melamine content
avg_melamine = filtered_df['melamine content (mg / kg)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_melamine:.2f}")
```
