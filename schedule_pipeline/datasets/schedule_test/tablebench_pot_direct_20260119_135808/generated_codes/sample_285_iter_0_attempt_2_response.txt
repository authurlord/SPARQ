Step 1: The user wants the average revenue of the top 5 companies in the 'banking' industry.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the rows where the 'industry' column is 'banking'.
Step 4: Select the 'revenue (millions)' column, convert it to numeric, and calculate the mean of the top 5 companies by rank.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for banking industry
banking_companies = df[df['industry'] == 'banking']
# Sort by rank to get the top 5
top_5_bankers = banking_companies.nsmallest(5, 'rank')
# Calculate average revenue
avg_revenue = top_5_bankers['revenue (millions)'].astype(float).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_revenue:.1f}")
```
