Step 1: The user is asking which nation has a total of 57 medals.  
Step 2: Look at the "Total" column in the table and find the row where the total is 57.  
Step 3: The corresponding "Nation" in that row is the answer.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the nation with total medals equal to 57
nation_with_57 = df[df['Total'] == 57]['Nation'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation_with_57}")
```
