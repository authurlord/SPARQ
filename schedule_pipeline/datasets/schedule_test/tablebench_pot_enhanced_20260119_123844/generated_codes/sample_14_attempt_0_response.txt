Step 1: The user wants the total elevation of all mountains listed in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'elevation (m)' column, convert it to integers, and sum the values.
Step 4: Print the total elevation.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'elevation (m)' to integer and calculate total
total_elevation = df['elevation (m)'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_elevation}")
```
