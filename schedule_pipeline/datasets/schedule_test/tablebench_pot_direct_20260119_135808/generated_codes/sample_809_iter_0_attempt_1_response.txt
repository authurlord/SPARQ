Step 1: The user wants the mean height of buildings in Winnipeg that have more than 10 floors.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'floors' > 10.
Step 4: Convert 'height' column to numeric (it's currently stored as string).
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the filtered 'height' values.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter buildings with more than 10 floors
filtered_df = df[df['floors'] > 10]
# Convert height to numeric for calculation
filtered_df['height'] = pd.to_numeric(filtered_df['height'])
# Calculate mean height
mean_height = filtered_df['height'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_height:.1f}")
```
