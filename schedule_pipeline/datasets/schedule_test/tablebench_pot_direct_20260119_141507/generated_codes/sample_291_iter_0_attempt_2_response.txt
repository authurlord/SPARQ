Step 1: Identify earthquakes with magnitude 7.7 or higher.
Step 2: Filter for locations in Asia (e.g., Iran, Pakistan, Japan, Philippines, etc.).
Step 3: Sum the death tolls for these filtered earthquakes.

Note: "Asia" includes countries like Iran, Pakistan, Philippines, Japan, and others listed in the data.

From the table:
- Iran (Asia): magnitude 7.7, death toll 35
- Pakistan (Asia): magnitude 7.7, death toll 825
- Japan (Asia): magnitude 7.1 → not included (less than 7.7)
- Philippines (Asia): magnitude 7.1 → not included
- Antarctica and other regions are not in Asia

Only Iran and Pakistan have magnitude ≥7.7 and are in Asia.

Sum of death tolls: 35 + 825 = 860

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter for magnitude >= 7.7 and location in Asia
# Asia includes Iran, Pakistan, Japan, Philippines, etc.
asia_countries = ['Iran', 'Pakistan', 'Japan', 'Philippines']
filtered_df = df[(df['Magnitude'].str.contains('7.7')) & (df['Location'].str.contains('|'.join(asia_countries), case=False))]

# Sum the death toll
total_death_toll = filtered_df['Death toll'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_death_toll}")
```
