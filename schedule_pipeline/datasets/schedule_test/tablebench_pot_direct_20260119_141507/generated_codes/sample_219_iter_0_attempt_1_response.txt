Step 1: The user is asking whether there is a positive correlation between the total number of USAAF personnel and the number of personnel stationed overseas.  
Step 2: We will examine the trend by analyzing the relationship between "total usaaf" and "overseas" across the given dates.  
Step 3: Since the data is time-ordered, we can check if as total USAAF increases, the overseas personnel also increase.  
Step 4: We will calculate the correlation coefficient between "total usaaf" and "overseas" to determine the strength and direction of the relationship.  
Step 5: A positive correlation coefficient (close to 1) indicates that as total personnel increase, overseas personnel also increase.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between total usaaf and overseas personnel
correlation = df['total usaaf'].corr(df['overseas'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
