Step 1: The user wants the percentage difference between the average annual growth rate of the values in 2011 and 2010 for the top 5 ranked countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the top 5 ranked countries (rank 1 to 5).
Step 4: Convert the columns '2010' and '2011' to numeric.
Step 5: Calculate the growth rate from 2010 to 2011 for each country: (2011 - 2010) / 2010 * 100.
Step 6: Compute the average of these growth rates.
Step 7: Since the question asks for the percentage difference between the average growth rate of 2011 and 2010, we interpret it as the average growth rate itself (as a percentage), which is the result.

Note: The phrasing "percentage difference between average annual growth rate of the values in the `2011` and the `2010`" is ambiguous, but the most logical interpretation is the average growth rate from 2010 to 2011 across the top 5 countries.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter top 5 ranked countries
top_5 = df.head(5)

# Convert 2010 and 2011 columns to numeric
top_5['2010'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2010'])
top_5['2011'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2011'])

# Calculate growth rate from 2010 to 2011
growth_rate = (top_5['2011'] - top_5['2010']) / top_5['2010'] * 100

# Average growth rate
avg_growth_rate = growth_rate.mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_growth_rate:.2f}")
```
