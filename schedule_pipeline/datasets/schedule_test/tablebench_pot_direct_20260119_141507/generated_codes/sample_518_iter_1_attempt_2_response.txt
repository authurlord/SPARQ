The error occurred because the GDP (nominal) and population columns are stored as strings, not numeric values. We need to convert them to numeric types before performing division.

Step 1: Load the dataframe.
Step 2: Convert 'gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011' and 'population' columns to numeric (removing any non-numeric characters if needed).
Step 3: Calculate GDP per capita as nominal GDP divided by population.
Step 4: Find the country with the highest value and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert the relevant columns to numeric, removing any non-numeric characters
df['gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011'] = pd.to_numeric(df['gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011'].str.replace(',', ''), errors='coerce')
df['population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate GDP per capita
df['gdp_per_capita'] = df['gdp (nominal) (millions of usd) 2011'] / df['population']

# Find the country with the highest GDP per capita
highest_gdp_per_capita_country = df.loc[df['gdp_per_capita'].idxmax(), 'country']

print(f"Final Answer: {highest_gdp_per_capita_country}")
```
