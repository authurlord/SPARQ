We are asked whether an increase in eccentricity causes a corresponding increase in periselene or inclination. To analyze this, we need to examine the relationship between these variables:

- "eccentricity" (how elliptical the orbit is)
- "periselene" (distance at closest point to the moon)
- "inclination" (angle of orbit relative to the moon's equator)

Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the data into a DataFrame.
2. Check if there's a positive correlation between eccentricity and periselene (i.e., as eccentricity increases, does periselene increase?).
3. Check if there's a positive correlation between eccentricity and inclination.
4. Since both periselene and inclination are measured in different units and represent different physical properties, we will compute the correlation coefficients (Pearson) between eccentricity and each of the two.

Note: In orbital mechanics, eccentricity and periselene are not directly proportional — higher eccentricity means more elongated orbit, but periselene can vary independently. Inclination is independent of eccentricity.

We will calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient between:
- eccentricity and periselene
- eccentricity and inclination

If either correlation is positive and strong (e.g., > 0.7), then we say there is a corresponding increase.

However, from the data:
- Eccentricity values range from ~0.326 to ~0.776
- Periselene ranges from ~2200 to ~53215 km → actually, it's decreasing as eccentricity increases (e.g., high eccentricity has lower periselene)
- Inclination ranges from ~81° to ~90.3° → increases slightly with eccentricity

But let's verify with actual correlation.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric (in case of string issues)
df['eccentricity'] = pd.to_numeric(df['eccentricity'], errors='coerce')
df['periselene'] = pd.to_numeric(df['periselene'], errors='coerce')
df['inclination'] = pd.to_numeric(df['inclination (deg) (to moon equator)'], errors='coerce')

# Drop any rows with NaN due to conversion errors
df = df.dropna()

# Compute correlation between eccentricity and periselene
corr_periselene = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['periselene'])

# Compute correlation between eccentricity and inclination
corr_inclination = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['inclination'])

print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and periselene: {corr_periselene:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and inclination: {corr_inclination:.3f}")

# Final answer: Based on the correlation values, determine if there is a corresponding increase
# If correlation is positive and significant, then yes; otherwise no.
# From the data, periselene decreases when eccentricity increases → negative correlation
# Inclination increases slightly → positive but weak

# only positive and meaningful correlation counts.
```
