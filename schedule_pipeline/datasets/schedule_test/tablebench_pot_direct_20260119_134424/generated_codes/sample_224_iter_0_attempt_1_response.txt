Step 1: The user wants to analyze the relationship between the maximum pressure (p_max) and the projectile diameter (p1 diameter).  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'p1 diameter (mm)' and 'p max (bar)' columns.  
Step 4: Sort the data by 'p1 diameter (mm)' to observe the trend.  
Step 5: Print the sorted data to show how p_max changes with increasing diameter.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by p1 diameter (mm) to observe the trend
sorted_df = df.sort_values(by='p1 diameter (mm)')
print(sorted_df[['p1 diameter (mm)', 'p max ( bar )']])
```
