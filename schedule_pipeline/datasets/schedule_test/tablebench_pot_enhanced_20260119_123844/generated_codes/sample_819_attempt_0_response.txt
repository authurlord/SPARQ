Step 1: The user wants the variance of the 'average' points for couples who have performed more than 7 dances.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'number of dances' > 7.
Step 4: Extract the 'average' column from filtered data.
Step 5: Calculate the variance of the 'average' values.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter couples who performed more than 7 dances
filtered_df = df[df['number of dances'] > 7]
# Calculate variance of the 'average' column
variance = filtered_df['average'].var()
print(f"Final Answer: {variance:.2f}")
```
