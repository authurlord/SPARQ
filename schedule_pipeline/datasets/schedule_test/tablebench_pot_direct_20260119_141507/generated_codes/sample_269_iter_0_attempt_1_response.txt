Step 1: Understand the question — we are to calculate the percentage change in 'l (mh / km)' when frequency increases from 1 Hz to 100k Hz, assuming the ratio of 'g (î¼s / km)' to 'c (nf / km)' remains constant. However, note that in the data, the ratio of g to c is constant (all rows have g/c = 0.0 / 51.57 = 0.0, or 0.072 / 51.57 ≈ 0.0014, etc.), but since 'c' is constant at 51.57 across all rows, and 'g' varies slightly, the ratio is not exactly constant. Step 2: Extract the values of 'l (mh / km)' at frequency 1 Hz and 100k Hz.

- At frequency 1 Hz: l = 0.6129
- At frequency 100k Hz: l = 0.5807

Step 3: Compute the percentage change using the formula:
Percentage change = ((new_value - old_value) / old_value) * 100

Step 4: Perform the calculation.

Note: The assumption about the ratio being constant is not strictly valid due to variation in g, but per the question, we proceed with the given data and the specified condition.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the dataframe
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract the values of 'l (mh / km)' at frequency 1 Hz and 100k Hz
l_1_hz = df.loc[df['frequency (hz)'] == '1', 'l (mh / km)'].values[0]
l_100k_hz = df.loc[df['frequency (hz)'] == '100k', 'l (mh / km)'].values[0]

# Calculate percentage change
percentage_change = ((l_100k_hz - l_1_hz) / l_1_hz) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {percentage_change:.2f}")
```
