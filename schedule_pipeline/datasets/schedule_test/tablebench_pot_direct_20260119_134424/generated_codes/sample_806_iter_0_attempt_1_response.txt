Step 1: The user wants to calculate the mean, median, and standard deviation of the boiling points for the five noble gases (helium, neon, argon, krypton, xenon). Additionally, determine which gas has the most extreme boiling point relative to the mean.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the boiling point values from the 'boiling point (degree)' row.
Step 4: Convert the values to float for numerical operations.
Step 5: Compute the mean, median, and standard deviation.
Step 6: Calculate the absolute difference between each boiling point and the mean to determine the most extreme value.
Step 7: Identify the gas with the largest deviation from the mean.
Step 8: Print the results and the gas with the most extreme boiling point.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract boiling point values and convert to float
boiling_points = df[df['physical property'] == 'boiling point (degree)'].iloc[0, 1:].astype(float)

# Calculate mean, median, and standard deviation
mean_bp = boiling_points.mean()
median_bp = boiling_points.median()
std_bp = boiling_points.std()

# Find the gas with the most extreme boiling point relative to the mean
deviations = np.abs(boiling_points - mean_bp)
most_extreme_gas = boiling_points.index[deviations.idxmax()]

print(f"Mean boiling point: {mean_bp:.2f}")
print(f"Median boiling point: {median_bp:.2f}")
print(f"Standard deviation: {std_bp:.2f}")
print(f"Gas with most extreme boiling point: {most_extreme_gas}")

# Final Answer: mean, median, std, and the gas with most extreme boiling point
print(f"Final Answer: {mean_bp:.2f}, {median_bp:.2f}, {std_bp:.2f}, {most_extreme_gas}")
```
