Step 1: The user wants the average profit of the top 5 companies based on their rank.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'profit (us billion)' column and convert it to numeric, handling negative values.
Step 4: Extract the first 5 rows (top 5 companies) and calculate the mean of their profits.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'profit (us billion)' to numeric, handling negative values
df['profit (us billion)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['profit (us billion)'], errors='coerce')
# Get the top 5 companies by rank and calculate average profit
top_5_profit = df['profit (us billion)'].head(5).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {top_5_profit:.1f}")
```
