Step 1: The user wants to forecast viewership for 2013 based on the trend from 2005 to 2011.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe and extract the relevant data (broadcast date and viewers).  
Step 3: Convert broadcast date to a numerical year (e.g., 2005 → 2005) and ensure viewers are numeric.  
Step 4: Fit a linear trend (simple linear regression) to predict viewership in 2013.  
Step 5: Use the model to predict viewership for 2013 and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert broadcast date to integer year
df['broadcast date'] = pd.to_numeric(df['broadcast date'])

# Extract year and viewers
years = df['broadcast date'].values
viewers = df['viewers (millions)'].astype(float).values

# Fit a linear model: viewers ~ year
# Using numpy's polyfit to get slope and intercept
slope, intercept = np.polyfit(years, viewers, 1)

# Predict viewership for 2013
predicted_2013 = slope * 2013 + intercept

print(f"Final Answer: {predicted_2013:.2f}")
```
