Step 1: The user is asking for the year when the athlete won a gold medal in the 100 m event.  
Step 2: In the table, "position" indicates performance: "1st" means first place, which implies a gold medal.  
Step 3: Filter rows where the "event" is "100 m" and "position" is "1st".  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding "year" from those rows.  

Note: The data shows "1st" in the 100 m event in 2007 and 2008 (Olympic Games). However, the 2007 entry has "1st" in the 100 m event, so that year qualifies.  

Let's check:  
- 2007: "1st" in 100 m → gold medal  
- 2008: "2nd" in 100 m → not gold  
So only 2007 has a gold medal in 100 m.  

Final Answer: 2007

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where event is '100 m' and position is '1st'
gold_medal_years = df[(df['event'] == '100 m') & (df['position'] == '1st')]['year']
# Since there's only one such year, we return it
print(f"Final Answer: {gold_medal_years.iloc[0]}")
```
