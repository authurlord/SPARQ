Step 1: The user wants the average annual increase in issue price from 2007 to 2011.  
Step 2: Extract the "issue price" values for each year from 2007 to 2011.  
Step 3: Calculate the yearly differences in issue price and then compute the average of those differences.  
Step 4: Since the data is per year, we can directly extract the issue prices by year and compute the average change per year.

Note: The issue price increases from 94.95 (2007) to 114.95 (2011). We will calculate the total increase over 4 years and divide by 4.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract issue price by year
issue_prices = df.groupby('year')['issue price'].first()
# Convert to numeric and calculate average annual increase
prices_list = issue_prices.values
average_increase = (prices_list[-1] - prices_list[0]) / (2011 - 2007)
print(f"Final Answer: {average_increase:.2f}")
```
