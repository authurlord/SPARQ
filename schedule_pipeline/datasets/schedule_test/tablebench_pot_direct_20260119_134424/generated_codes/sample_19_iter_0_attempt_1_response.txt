Step 1: The user wants the total revenue of companies in the 'oil' industry.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'industry' column is 'oil'.
Step 4: Sum the 'revenue (millions)' column for these rows.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for oil industry
oil_companies = df[df['industry'] == 'oil']
# Sum the revenue
total_revenue_oil = oil_companies['revenue (millions)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_revenue_oil}")
```
