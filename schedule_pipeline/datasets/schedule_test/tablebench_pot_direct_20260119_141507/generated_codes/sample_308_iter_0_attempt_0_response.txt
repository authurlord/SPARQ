Step 1: Identify the rows where the Artist is "Glen Campbell".  
Step 2: Check the Year column to see if the year falls in the 1980s (i.e., between 1980 and 1989 inclusive).  
Step 3: Count how many such rows exist.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter songs by Glen Campbell and years in the 1980s
glen_campbell_1980s = df[(df['Artist'] == 'Glen Campbell') & (df['Year'].between(1980, 1989))]
count = len(glen_campbell_1980s)
print(f"Final Answer: {count}")
```
