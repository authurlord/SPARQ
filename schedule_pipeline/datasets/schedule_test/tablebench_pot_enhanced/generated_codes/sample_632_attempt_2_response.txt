Step 1: The user wants the new average height of the top 5 buildings after increasing each of their heights by 5 meters.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the 'height' column and convert it to numeric (removing ' m' and converting to float).
Step 4: Select the top 5 buildings (first 5 rows based on rank).
Step 5: Add 5 meters to each of their heights.
Step 6: Calculate the new average height and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert height to numeric by removing ' m' and converting to float
df['height'] = df['height'].str.replace(' m', '').astype(float)
# Select top 5 buildings (by rank)
top_5_heights = df['height'].head(5)
# Increase each height by 5 meters
new_heights = top_5_heights + 5
# Calculate new average
new_average = new_heights.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {new_average:.1f}")
```
