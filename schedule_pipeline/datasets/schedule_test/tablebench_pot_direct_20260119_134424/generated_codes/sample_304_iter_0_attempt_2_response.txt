Step 1: Filter the data to include only rows where the venue is "waverley park" and the crowd is greater than 40,000.
Step 2: Extract the 'margin' column and find the maximum margin.
Step 3: Identify the team (winner) associated with that maximum margin.
Step 4: Print the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where venue is 'waverley park' and crowd > 40000
filtered_df = df[(df['venue'] == 'waverley park') & (df['crowd'].astype(int) > 40000)]
# Find the row with the highest margin
max_margin_row = filtered_df.loc[filtered_df['margin'].idxmax()]
# Get the winner team
winner_team = max_margin_row['winners']
print(f"Final Answer: {winner_team}")
```
