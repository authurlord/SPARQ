Step 1: The user wants the total 2010 population (in thousands) of the top 5 districts with the highest GDP per capita.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Identify the column for GDP per capita and sort districts by it in descending order.  
Step 4: Select the top 5 districts and sum their "2010 population (000)" values.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Sort by '2008 gdp per capita (usd)' in descending order and take top 5
top_5_gdp_per_capita = df.sort_values(by='2008 gdp per capita (usd) a', ascending=False).head(5)
# Sum the '2010 population (000)' of these districts
total_population = top_5_gdp_per_capita['2010 population (000)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_population}")
```
