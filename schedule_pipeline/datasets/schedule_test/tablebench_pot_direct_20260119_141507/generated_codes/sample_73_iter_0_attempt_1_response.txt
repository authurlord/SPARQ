Step 1: The user wants to identify the player who won the championship and the number of points they won.
Step 2: In the table, the "status" column contains information about the outcome. The player with the status "champion , defeated rafael nadal" is the one who won the championship.
Step 3: From that row, extract the player name and the "points won" value.
Step 4: Print the player and their points won.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the row where 'status' is 'champion , defeated rafael nadal'
champion_row = df[df['status'] == 'champion , defeated rafael nadal']
player_champion = champion_row['player'].iloc[0]
points_won = champion_row['points won'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {player_champion}, {points_won}")
```
