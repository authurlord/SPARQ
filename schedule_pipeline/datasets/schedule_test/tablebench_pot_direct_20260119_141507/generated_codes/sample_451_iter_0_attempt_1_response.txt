Step 1: The user wants to identify surnames that deviate significantly from the norm. Since the data shows a trend in the number of bearers over time, "deviation" can be interpreted as a significant drop or rise from the overall trend.  
Step 2: We will analyze the "number of bearers 1971" and "number of bearers 2009" columns.  
Step 3: Compute the percentage change from 1971 to 2009 for each surname.  
Step 4: Identify outliers — surnames with extreme changes (e.g., very large decreases or increases).  
Step 5: Note that "møller" is the only surname with an occupational type, which might indicate a different pattern.  
Step 6: Look for surnames with unusually high or low percentage changes.

We will calculate the percentage change and flag those with absolute changes > 20% (a reasonable threshold for "significant deviation").

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Calculate percentage change from 1971 to 2009
df['change_percent'] = ((df['number of bearers 2009'] - df['number of bearers 1971']) / df['number of bearers 1971']) * 100

# Identify significant deviations (absolute change > 20%)
deviations = df[df['change_percent'].abs() > 20]

# Extract the surnames that deviate significantly
deviant_surnames = deviations['surname'].tolist()

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(deviant_surnames)}")
```
