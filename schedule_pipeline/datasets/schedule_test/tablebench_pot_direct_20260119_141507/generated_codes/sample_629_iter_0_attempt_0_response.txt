Step 1: The user wants the total sales of the top 5 albums (i.e., the first 5 rows in the table based on position).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'sales' column and take the first 5 rows.
Step 4: Sum the sales values and print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Get the sales of the top 5 albums (first 5 rows)
top_5_sales = df['sales'].head(5).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {top_5_sales}")
```
