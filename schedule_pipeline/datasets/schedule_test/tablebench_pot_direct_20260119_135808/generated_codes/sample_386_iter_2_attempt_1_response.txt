Step 1: The user wants to count how many parties won 10 or fewer seats.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'seats won' is less than or equal to 10.
Step 4: Count the number of such rows and print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Count parties that won 10 or fewer seats
count_parties = df[df['seats won'] <= 10].shape[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {count_parties}")
```
