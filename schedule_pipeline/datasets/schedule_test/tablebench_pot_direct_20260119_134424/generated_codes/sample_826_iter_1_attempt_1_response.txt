Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of the 'total' column across all countries.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Ensure the 'total' column is numeric; convert if necessary.
Step 4: Calculate the standard deviation of the 'total' column.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total' column to numeric to ensure it's treated as numbers
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'], errors='coerce')
# Calculate standard deviation of the 'total' column
std_total = df['total'].std()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_total:.2f}")
```
