Step 1: The user wants the total sales of the top 5 albums, which are the first 5 rows in the table.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'sales' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Sum the sales of the first 5 rows.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'sales' column to integer and sum the top 5
total_sales_top5 = df['sales'].astype(int).head(5).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_sales_top5}")
```
