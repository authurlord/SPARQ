Step 1: The user is asking for the likely average finish position and total winnings for the next racing season, based on historical data from 1990 to 2002. Since the last active year in the data is 2002, we will use the data from 1990 to 2002 to calculate the average finish position and total winnings.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Extract the 'avg finish' and 'winnings' columns.

Step 4: Calculate the mean of 'avg finish' and 'winnings' across all years.

Step 5: Output the results as the likely average finish position and total winnings.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate average finish position and total winnings over the period
avg_finish = df['avg finish'].mean()
total_winnings = df['winnings'].mean()

print(f"Final Answer: {avg_finish:.1f}, {total_winnings:.0f}")
```
