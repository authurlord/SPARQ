Step 1: The question asks whether the wife's age at marriage has a significant impact on the number of children she has. This requires analyzing the relationship between "her age" and "of children".

Step 2: Load the data into a DataFrame.

Step 3: Perform a simple correlation analysis between "her age" and "of children" to assess the strength and direction of the relationship.

Step 4: Since this is a small dataset, we can compute the correlation coefficient (Pearson) and interpret it. A high absolute value (close to 1 or -1) indicates a strong linear relationship.

Step 5: Print the correlation value to answer the question in a quantitative way.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between wife's age and number of children
correlation = df['her age'].corr(df['of children'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
