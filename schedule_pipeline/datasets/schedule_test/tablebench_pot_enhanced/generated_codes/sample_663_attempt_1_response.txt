Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'commissioned capacity (mw)' and 'year of commission'.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Convert 'commissioned capacity (mw)' and 'year of commission' to numeric types.  
Step 4: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.  
Step 5: Print the result.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['commissioned capacity (mw)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['commissioned capacity (mw)'])
df['year of commission'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year of commission'])

# Calculate correlation
correlation = df['commissioned capacity (mw)'].corr(df['year of commission'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
