Step 1: The user wants to find the season when the giant slalom score was 7.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter the row where 'giant slalom' is equal to 7.  
Step 4: Extract the corresponding 'season' value.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the season where 'giant slalom' is 7
season_7 = df[df['giant slalom'] == '7']['season'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {season_7}")
```
