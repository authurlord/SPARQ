Step 1: Filter the data for earthquakes with a magnitude of 7.7 or higher.
Step 2: Identify which locations are in Asia. From the data, "Iran", "Pakistan", "Philippines", and "Japan" are Asian countries.
Step 3: Sum the death tolls for these specific earthquakes.
Step 4: Print the total death toll.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for magnitude >= 7.7
high_magnitude = df[df['Magnitude'] == '7.7']
# Define Asian countries
asian_countries = ['Iran Iran', 'Pakistan Pakistan', 'Philippines Philippines', 'Japan Japan']
# Filter for Asian locations
asian_earthquakes = high_magnitude[high_magnitude['Location'].isin(asian_countries)]
# Sum the death toll
total_death_toll = asian_earthquakes['Death toll'].astype(int).sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_death_toll}")
```
