Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between 'total passengers' and 'capacity in use' across airports.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Clean the 'capacity in use' column by removing spaces and converting to float.
Step 4: Convert 'total passengers' to float.
Step 5: Calculate the correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Clean 'capacity in use' column: remove spaces and convert to float
df['capacity in use'] = df['capacity in use'].str.replace(' ', '').astype(float)

# Convert 'total passengers' to float
df['total passengers'] = df['total passengers'].astype(float)

# Calculate correlation coefficient
correlation = df['total passengers'].corr(df['capacity in use'])

print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
