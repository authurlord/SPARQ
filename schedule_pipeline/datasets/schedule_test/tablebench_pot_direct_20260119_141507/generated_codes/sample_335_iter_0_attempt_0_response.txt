Step 1: Identify the relevant rows where the event is "Heptathlon" and the competition is "South American Championships" held in "Lima, Peru".  
Step 2: Check the "Year" and "Notes" columns for such entries to find the year and corresponding points.  
Step 3: From the data, locate the row where "Venue" is "Lima, Peru", "Event" is "Heptathlon", and "Position" is "1st" (indicating a gold medal).  
Step 4: Extract the year and points from that row.

Looking at the table:
- The row with "South American Championships" in "Lima, Peru" appears in 2009 and again in 2017.
- In 2009: Venue = Lima, Peru, Event = Heptathlon, Position = 1st, Notes = 5578 pts.
- In 2017: Venue = Taipei, Taiwan (not Lima), so not relevant.

Thus, the only match is in 2009 with 5578 points.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Event is 'Heptathlon' and Venue is 'Lima, Peru'
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == 'Heptathlon') & (df['Venue'] == 'Lima, Peru')]
# Extract the year and points (from Notes column)
result_year = filtered_df.iloc[0]['Year']
result_points = filtered_df.iloc[0]['Notes']
print(f"Final Answer: {result_year}, {result_points}")
```
