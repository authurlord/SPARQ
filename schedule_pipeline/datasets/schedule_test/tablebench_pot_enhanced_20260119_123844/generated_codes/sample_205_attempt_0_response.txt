Step 1: The question asks to determine which factor—temperature T (°C) or density of air ρ (kg/m³)—has a greater causal influence on the speed of sound c (m/s).  
Step 2: To analyze this, we can compute the correlation between temperature and speed of sound, and between density and speed of sound. A higher absolute correlation indicates a stronger influence.  
Step 3: Load the data and convert relevant columns to numeric for analysis.  
Step 4: Calculate Pearson correlation coefficients for both relationships.  
Step 5: Compare the magnitudes of the correlations to determine which has a greater influence.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['Temperature T (°C)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Temperature T (°C)'])
df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'])

# Calculate correlation coefficients
corr_temp_c = df['Temperature T (°C)'].corr(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])
corr_density_c = df['Density of air ρ (kg/m3)'].corr(df['Speed of sound c (m/s)'])

# Compare absolute values of correlations
if abs(corr_temp_c) > abs(corr_density_c):
    influence = "Temperature T (°C)"
else:
    influence = "Density of air ρ (kg/m3)"

print(f"Final Answer: {influence}")
```
