Step 1: The user wants to identify years with unusually high or low success rates compared to the team's overall performance.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'success rate' column to a numeric value (removing the % sign and converting to float).
Step 4: Calculate the overall success rate (from the 'total' row).
Step 5: Identify years where the success rate deviates significantly from the overall rate (e.g., more than 10% above or below).
Step 6: Return those years.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert success rate to float by removing % and converting to float
df['success rate'] = df['success rate'].str.replace('%', '').astype(float)
# Get overall success rate from total row
overall_success_rate = df.loc[df['year'] == 'total', 'success rate'].iloc[0]
# Define threshold for unusual performance (e.g., more than 10% deviation)
threshold = 10.0
# Find years with unusually high or low success rate
unusual_years = df[df['year'] != 'total']
unusual_years = unusual_years[
    (unusual_years['success rate'] > overall_success_rate + threshold) |
    (unusual_years['success rate'] < overall_success_rate - threshold)
]['year']
print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(unusual_years)}")
```
