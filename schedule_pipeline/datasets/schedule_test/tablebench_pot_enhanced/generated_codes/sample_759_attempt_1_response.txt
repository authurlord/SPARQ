Step 1: The user wants a projection of Tabriz's population in 2026 based on historical growth from 1956 to 2006.  
Step 2: Extract the population data for Tabriz (row 4) across the years 1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, and 2006.  
Step 3: Calculate the average annual growth rate between 1956 and 2006.  
Step 4: Use this rate to project the population for 2026 by applying compound growth over 20 years (from 2006 to 2026).  
Step 5: Round the result to the nearest whole number and output it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract Tabriz's population data
tabriz_data = df[df['city'] == 'tabriz']
years = [1956, 1966, 1976, 1986, 1996, 2006]
populations = [int(tabriz_data[f'{year}'].values[0]) for year in years]

# Calculate average annual growth rate (CAGR)
start_pop = populations[0]
end_pop = populations[-1]
n = len(years) - 1  # Number of years between 1956 and 2006
cagr = (end_pop / start_pop) ** (1 / n) - 1

# Project population for 2026 (20 years after 2006)
projected_pop_2026 = end_pop * (1 + cagr) ** 20

# Round to nearest whole number
print(f"Final Answer: {round(projected_pop_2026)}")
```
