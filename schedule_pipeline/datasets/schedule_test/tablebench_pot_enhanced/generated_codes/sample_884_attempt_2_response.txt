Step 1: The user wants to identify the year with a higher percentage increase in domestic steel tonnage compared to foreign total steel tonnage. This requires calculating the year-over-year percentage increase for both 'domestic s ton' and 'foreign total s ton', then comparing them.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and convert relevant columns to numeric.

Step 3: Calculate the percentage increase for both domestic and foreign total steel tonnage between consecutive years.

Step 4: Compare the percentage increases and find the year where domestic's increase is greater than foreign's.

Step 5: Output the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert necessary columns to numeric
df['domestic s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['domestic s ton'])
df['foreign total s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['foreign total s ton'])

# Calculate year-over-year percentage increase
df['domestic_increase_pct'] = df['domestic s ton'].pct_change() * 100
df['foreign_increase_pct'] = df['foreign total s ton'].pct_change() * 100

# Find the year where domestic increase > foreign increase (skip first year since no prior data)
result_year = df.loc[df['domestic_increase_pct'] > df['foreign_increase_pct'], 'year'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {result_year}")
```
