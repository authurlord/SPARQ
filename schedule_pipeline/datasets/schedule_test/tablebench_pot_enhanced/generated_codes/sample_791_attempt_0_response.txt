Step 1: The user wants the median number of aircraft movements for the top 10 busiest airports.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'aircraft movements' column and convert it to integers.
Step 4: Extract the top 10 airports based on rank (first 10 rows).
Step 5: Calculate the median of the 'aircraft movements' for these top 10 airports.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'aircraft movements' to integer type
df['aircraft movements'] = pd.to_numeric(df['aircraft movements'])
# Get the top 10 airports (first 10 rows based on rank)
top_10 = df.head(10)
# Calculate median of aircraft movements for top 10
median_movements = top_10['aircraft movements'].median()
print(f"Final Answer: {median_movements}")
```
