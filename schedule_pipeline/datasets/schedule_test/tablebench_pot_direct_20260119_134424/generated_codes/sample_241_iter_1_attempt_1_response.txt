Step 1: The user wants the average number of viewers for seasons with more than 20 episodes.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'episodes' > 20 (convert 'episodes' to integer).
Step 4: Calculate the mean of 'viewers (in millions)' for filtered rows.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'episodes' column to integer for comparison
df['episodes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['episodes'], errors='coerce')
# Filter seasons with more than 20 episodes
filtered_df = df[df['episodes'] > 20]
# Calculate average viewers for those seasons
average_viewers = filtered_df['viewers (in millions)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {average_viewers:.2f}")
```
