Step 1: The user wants to find the nation that has a total of 6 medals.  
Step 2: Look at the 'total' column to identify which nation has a total of 6.  
Step 3: The row with 'total' = 6 is in the data. From the table, we can see that "Russia" has a total of 6 medals.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the nation where total medals is 6
nation_with_6_medals = df[df['total'] == 6]['nation'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {nation_with_6_medals}")
```
