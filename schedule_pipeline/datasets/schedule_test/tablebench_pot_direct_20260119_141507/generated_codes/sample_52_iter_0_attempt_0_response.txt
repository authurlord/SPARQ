Step 1: The user is asking for a nation that has exactly 5 gold medals and 2 silver medals.  
Step 2: Look through the table for a row where 'Gold' = 5 and 'Silver' = 2.  
Step 3: From the data, check each row.  
Observing the table:  
- Bulgaria has 5 gold and 2 silver.  
- No other nation has both 5 gold and 2 silver.  

Thus, Bulgaria is the only nation matching the criteria.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Gold is 5 and Silver is 2
result = df[(df['Gold'] == 5) & (df['Silver'] == 2)]
if not result.empty:
    nation = result.iloc[0]['Nation']
    print(f"Final Answer: {nation}")
else:
    print("Final Answer: None")
```
