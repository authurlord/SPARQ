Step 1: Identify the work that was nominated for "Song of the Year" at the Grammy Awards.
Step 2: Among those nominations, find the year when the work won an award for a song.
Step 3: The relevant row is where 'Category' is "Song of the Year" and 'Result' is "Won".
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from that row.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Category is 'Song of the Year' and Result is 'Won'
winner_row = df[(df['Category'] == 'Song of the Year') & (df['Result'] == 'Won')]
# Extract the year
year = winner_row['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
