We are asked to find the difference in population between the 6th ranked city and the city 'acqui terme'.

Step-by-step:
1. The table has a 'rank' column and a 'city' column.
2. We need to locate:
   - The city at rank "6th" (which is the 6th row, index 5 if 0-based).
   - The city named 'acqui terme'.
3. Extract their populations.
4. Compute the absolute difference between the two populations.
5. Ensure that the population values are numeric (they are given as strings, so we must convert them to integers).

Note: The error in the previous attempt was due to trying to subtract two string values. We must convert the population columns to numeric.

Correct approach:
- Load the DataFrame.
- Convert 'population' to numeric (using pd.to_numeric with errors='coerce').
- Find the row where 'rank' is '6th' and get its population.
- Find the row where 'city' is 'acqui terme' and get its population.
- Compute the difference.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert population to numeric (in case of non-numeric values)
df['population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['population'], errors='coerce')

# Get population of 6th ranked city (rank == '6th')
sixth_ranked_population = df[df['rank'] == '6th']['population'].values[0]

# Get population of 'acqui terme'
acqui_terme_population = df[df['city'] == 'acqui terme']['population'].values[0]

# Calculate the difference
difference = abs(sixth_ranked_population - acqui_terme_population)
print(f"Final Answer: {difference}")
```
