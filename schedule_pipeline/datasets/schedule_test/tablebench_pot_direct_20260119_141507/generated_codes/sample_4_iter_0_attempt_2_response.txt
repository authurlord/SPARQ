Step 1: The user wants the total number of goals scored by Liverpool in the Premier League.  
Step 2: Identify relevant rows where the Club is "Liverpool" and the League is "Premier League".  
Step 3: Extract the "Goals" column from those rows (specifically from the "League_1" and "League Cup" or "Europe_1" columns, but note that the "Goals" values are in the "League_2" and "League Cup_1" columns for different competitions — however, the "Premier League" data is under "League_1" and "Goals" is in "League_1" column).  
Looking at the table structure, the "League_1" column corresponds to the Premier League, and the "Goals" in that context is the "Goals" under "League_1".  
Step 4: Filter rows where Club is "Liverpool" and Season is in the range 1990–91 to 1998–99 (excluding the "Liverpool Total" row), and sum the "Goals" from the "League_1" column (which is the "Goals" column in the 5th position, i.e., index 5).  

Note: In the table, the "Goals" for Premier League is in the "League_1" column, which is at index 5. We need to sum only the rows for Liverpool in Premier League seasons.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where Club is 'Liverpool' and the League is 'Premier League'
# The "League" column is at index 3, and "Goals" in Premier League is at index 5
filtered_df = df[(df['Club'] == 'Liverpool') & (df['League'] == 'Premier League')]
# Sum the goals from the "Goals" column in "League_1" (index 5)
total_goals = filtered_df['League_1'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_goals}")
```
