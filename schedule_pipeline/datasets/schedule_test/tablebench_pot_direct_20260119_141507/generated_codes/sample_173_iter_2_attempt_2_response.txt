Step-by-step approach:
1. Load the data into a DataFrame.
2. Extract the 'eccentricity', 'periselene (km)', and 'aposelene (km)' columns.
3. Compute the correlation between eccentricity and periselene, and between eccentricity and aposelene.
4. Interpret the results (positive/negative correlation) to describe the impact.

Note: Since the data is limited (only 7 rows), correlation may not be strong, but it provides insight into trends.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract relevant columns
eccentricity = df['eccentricity'].astype(float)
periselene = df['periselene (km)'].astype(float)
aposelene = df['aposelene (km)'].astype(float)

# Compute correlation

corr_aposelene = df['eccentricity'].corr(df['aposelene (km)'])

print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and periselene: {corr_periselene:.3f}")
print(f"Correlation between eccentricity and aposelene: {corr_aposelene:.3f}")
```
