Step 1: Understand the question. We are to find the year where the natural growth rate is significantly different from the average natural growth rate between 1990 and 2000. "Significantly different" implies a large deviation (e.g., more than 1 standard deviation or a clear outlier).

Step 2: Filter data for years from 1990 to 2000 (inclusive). Note that the last row "1990 - 2009" is an aggregate and should be excluded.

Step 3: Compute the average natural growth rate for the years 1990–2000.

Step 4: For each year in 1990–2000, compute the absolute difference between its natural growth and the average. Identify the year(s) with the largest deviation.

Step 5: Since the question asks for "a year", we return the year with the maximum deviation.

Step 6: Handle missing values — in this dataset, "na" appears in later years, but within 1990–2000, all values are numeric.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter rows for years 1990 to 2000 (inclusive)
df_filtered = df[df['year'].str.contains(r'199[0-9]|200[0-9]')]

# Convert 'natural growth' to float and drop any na
df_filtered['natural growth'] = pd.to_numeric(df_filtered['natural growth'], errors='coerce')
df_filtered = df_filtered.dropna(subset=['natural growth'])

# Calculate average natural growth rate from 1990 to 2000
avg_growth = df_filtered['natural growth'].mean()

# Compute absolute deviations
df_filtered['deviation'] = abs(df_filtered['natural growth'] - avg_growth)

# Find the year with the maximum deviation
max_deviation_year = df_filtered.loc[df_filtered['deviation'].idxmax(), 'year']

print(f"Final Answer: {max_deviation_year}")
```
