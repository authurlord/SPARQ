Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the 'area (km square) territorial waters' and 'percentage of total area (foreez)' across ecozones.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the two columns of interest: 'area (km square) territorial waters' and 'percentage of total area (foreez)'.
Step 4: Convert these columns to numeric types to ensure proper computation.
Step 5: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient using pandas corr() method.
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert the required columns to numeric
df['area (km square) territorial waters'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square) territorial waters'])
df['percentage of total area (foreez)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'])

# Compute correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km square) territorial waters'].corr(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
