Step 1: Identify the year with the highest number of major hurricanes between 1870 and 1878.  
Step 2: From that year, extract the total number of tropical storms.  

The data shows the number of major hurricanes per year. We need to find the year with the maximum value in the "number of major hurricanes" column, then look up the corresponding "number of tropical storms" for that year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Find the year with the highest number of major hurricanes
max_major_hurricanes_year = df.loc[df['number of major hurricanes'].idxmax(), 'year']
# Get the number of tropical storms for that year
tropical_storms_in_max_year = df.loc[df['number of major hurricanes'].idxmax(), 'number of tropical storms']
print(f"Final Answer: {tropical_storms_in_max_year}")
```
