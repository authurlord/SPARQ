Step 1: The user wants the percentage difference between the average annual growth rate of the values in 2011 and 2010 for the top 5 ranked countries.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter the top 5 ranked countries (rank 1 to 5).

Step 3: Convert the columns '2010' and '2011' to numeric.

Step 4: Calculate the annual growth rate for each country as (2011 - 2010) / 2010 * 100.

Step 5: Compute the average of these growth rates.

Step 6: Since the question asks for the percentage difference between the average growth rates of 2011 and 2010, we interpret this as the average growth rate from 2010 to 2011 for the top 5 countries.

Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 ranked countries
top_5 = df.iloc[0:5]
# Convert '2010' and '2011' columns to numeric
top_5['2010'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2010'])
top_5['2011'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['2011'])
# Calculate growth rate from 2010 to 2011
growth_rate = (top_5['2011'] - top_5['2010']) / top_5['2010'] * 100
# Average growth rate
avg_growth_rate = growth_rate.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_growth_rate:.2f}")
```
