Step 1: The user wants to identify the year with a higher percentage increase in domestic steel tonnage compared to foreign total steel tonnage. This requires calculating the percentage increase from one year to the next for both domestic and foreign total steel tonnage, then comparing them.

Step 2: Load the dataframe and convert the relevant columns to numeric (they are stored as strings).

Step 3: Calculate the year-over-year percentage increase for both 'domestic s ton' and 'foreign total s ton'.

Step 4: Compare the percentage increases and find the year where domestic's increase is greater than foreign's.

Step 5: Return the year.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert columns to numeric
df['domestic s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['domestic s ton'])
df['foreign total s ton'] = pd.to_numeric(df['foreign total s ton'])

# Calculate year-over-year percentage increase
df['domestic_increase_pct'] = df['domestic s ton'].pct_change() * 100
df['foreign_increase_pct'] = df['foreign total s ton'].pct_change() * 100

# Find the year where domestic increase > foreign increase (skip first year since no prior year)
result_year = df.loc[df['domestic_increase_pct'] > df['foreign_increase_pct'], 'year'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {result_year}")
```
