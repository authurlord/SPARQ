Step 1: The user wants two values:  
- The median total number of medals for nations in the top 5 rankings (ranks 1 to 5).  
- The median total number of medals for all countries listed.  

Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Extract the 'total' column for all nations and compute the median.  
Step 4: Filter the data for the top 5 ranks (rank 1 to 5), extract the 'total' column, and compute its median.  
Step 5: Print both medians in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total' column to integer for proper calculation
df['total'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total'])

# Median total medals for top 5 nations
top_5_medals = df[df['rank'].astype(int) <= 5]['total']
median_top_5 = top_5_medals.median()

# Median total medals for all nations
median_all = df['total'].median()

print(f"Final Answer: {median_top_5}, {median_all}")
```
