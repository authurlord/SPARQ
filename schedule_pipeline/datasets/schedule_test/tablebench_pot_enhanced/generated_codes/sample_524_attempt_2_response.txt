Step 1: The question asks for the change in total seating capacity from 2004 to 2008. We need to calculate the total seating capacity of aircraft introduced or still in service by 2008, and compare it with the capacity in 2004.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Identify aircraft that were introduced in or before 2008 and were not retired by 2008 (i.e., retired is '-' or after 2008).

Step 4: Sum the seating capacities of these aircraft.

Step 5: For 2004, only consider aircraft introduced in 2004 (airbus a319-100) since that's the first year.

Step 6: Calculate the difference in total seating capacity between 2008 and 2004.

Step 7: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter aircraft introduced in or before 2008 and not retired by 2008
# Retired field is '-' means still in service
fleet_2008 = df[(df['introduced'] <= '2008') & ((df['retired'] == '-') | (df['retired'] > '2008'))]

# Total seating capacity in 2008
total_capacity_2008 = fleet_2008['seating'].astype(int).sum()

# Aircraft introduced in 2004 only
fleet_2004 = df[df['introduced'] == '2004']
total_capacity_2004 = fleet_2004['seating'].astype(int).sum()

# Change in capacity
capacity_change = total_capacity_2008 - total_capacity_2004

print(f"Final Answer: {capacity_change}")
```
