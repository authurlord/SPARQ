Step 1: The user is asking for the correlation between 'commissioned capacity (mw)' and 'year of commission'.  
Step 2: Correlation coefficient measures the strength and direction of the linear relationship between two variables.  
Step 3: Load the dataframe.  
Step 4: Extract the two columns: 'commissioned capacity (mw)' and 'year of commission'.  
Step 5: Compute the Pearson correlation coefficient using `corr()` method.  
Step 6: Print the correlation coefficient.

Note: Since "year of commission" increases over time and "commissioned capacity" may not show a clear trend, we expect a weak or slightly negative correlation if capacity increased over time, but it's not guaranteed. We will compute it directly.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the correlation between 'commissioned capacity (mw)' and 'year of commission'
correlation = df['commissioned capacity (mw)'].corr(df['year of commission'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.2f}")
```
