Step 1: The user wants the total diameter of craters with a diameter greater than 40 km.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'diameter (km)' > 40.
Step 4: Sum the 'diameter (km)' column for these filtered rows and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter craters with diameter > 40 km
filtered_df = df[df['diameter (km)'] > 40]
# Calculate total diameter
total_diameter = filtered_df['diameter (km)'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_diameter}")
```
