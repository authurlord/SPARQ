Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete won a gold medal (1st position) in the 100 m event at the African Junior Championships.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where 'Event' is '100 m', 'Competition' is 'African Junior Championships', and 'Position' is '1st'.
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the filtered row and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for gold medal in 100m at African Junior Championships
gold_medal_100m = df[(df['Event'] == '100 m') & 
                     (df['Competition'] == 'African Junior Championships') & 
                     (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Extract the year
year = gold_medal_100m['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year}")
```
