Step 1: Identify the payout for a "natural royal flush" with a 3-credit bet. From the table, the value under "3 credits" for "natural royal flush" is 900.
Step 2: Identify the payout for a "four of a kind" with a 3-credit bet. From the table, the value under "3 credits" for "four of a kind" is 15.
Step 3: Add the two payouts to get the total winnings.
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract payout for natural royal flush with 3 credits
natural_royal_flush_payout = int(df[df['hand'] == 'natural royal flush']['3 credits'].values[0])
# Extract payout for four of a kind with 3 credits
four_of_a_kind_payout = int(df[df['hand'] == 'four of a kind']['3 credits'].values[0])
# Calculate total winnings
total_winnings = natural_royal_flush_payout + four_of_a_kind_payout
print(f"Final Answer: {total_winnings}")
```
