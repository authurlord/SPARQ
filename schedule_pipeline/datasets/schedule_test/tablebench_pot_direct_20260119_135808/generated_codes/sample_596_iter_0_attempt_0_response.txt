Step 1: The user wants the average profit of the top 5 companies (rank 1 to 5).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the first 5 rows (top 5 companies).
Step 4: Convert 'profit (us billion)' column to numeric, handling negative values.
Step 5: Calculate the mean of the profit column and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter top 5 companies
top_5 = df.head(5)
# Convert profit column to numeric, handling negative signs correctly
top_5['profit (us billion)'] = pd.to_numeric(top_5['profit (us billion)'], errors='coerce')
# Calculate average profit
avg_profit = top_5['profit (us billion)'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_profit:.1f}")
```
