Step 1: The user wants to find the first year when the urbanization rate (percentage of population living in urban areas) surpassed 50%.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the 'urban , %' column to numeric, as it might be read as string.
Step 4: Find the first year where 'urban , %' > 50.
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'urban , %' column to numeric
df['urban , %'] = pd.to_numeric(df['urban , %'])
# Find the first year where urban percentage exceeds 50%
first_year_above_50 = df[df['urban , %'] > 50]['year (january)'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {first_year_above_50}")
```
