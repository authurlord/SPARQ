Step 1: The user wants the difference in boiling points between the highest and lowest 'bp comp 1 (˚c)' values.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'bp comp 1 (˚c)' to numeric type for proper calculation.
Step 4: Find the maximum and minimum values of 'bp comp 1 (˚c)'.
Step 5: Calculate the difference and print it.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'bp comp 1 (˚c)' to numeric, handling any non-numeric entries
df['bp comp 1 (˚c)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['bp comp 1 (˚c)'], errors='coerce')
# Find max and min values
max_bp = df['bp comp 1 (˚c)'].max()
min_bp = df['bp comp 1 (˚c)'].min()
# Calculate difference
difference = max_bp - min_bp
print(f"Final Answer: {difference:.1f}")
```
