Step 1: The question asks whether any of the given factors (area, administrative capital, postal code) significantly influence the census 2006 population.  
Step 2: Since this is a qualitative question about correlation or influence, we need to analyze the relationship between 'census 2006 population' and the other columns.  
Step 3: We will check for correlations between 'census 2006 population' and 'area (km 2 )', as area is a likely candidate for a direct relationship. Administrative capital and postal code are categorical and unlikely to have a measurable quantitative influence.  
Step 4: We compute the correlation between 'census 2006 population' and 'area (km 2 )'. If the correlation is strong (e.g., above 0.3 or below -0.3), then we say it has a significant influence. Otherwise, we conclude no clear impact.

Note: Since the data is not large and the relationship may be weak or non-linear, we rely on Pearson correlation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert area (km 2 ) and population to numeric
df['area (km 2 )'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km 2 )'], errors='coerce')
df['census 2006 population'] = pd.to_numeric(df['census 2006 population'], errors='coerce')

# Drop rows with missing values
df = df.dropna()

# Compute correlation between area and population
correlation = df['census 2006 population'].corr(df['area (km 2 )'])

# Check if correlation is significant (absolute value > 0.3)
if abs(correlation) > 0.3:
    print("Final Answer: area (km 2 )")
else:
    print("Final Answer: no clear impact")
```
