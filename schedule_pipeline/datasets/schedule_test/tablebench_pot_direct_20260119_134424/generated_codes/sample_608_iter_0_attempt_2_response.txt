Step 1: The user wants the difference in performance (coefficient of performance, COP) between two heat pump types at 35 °C.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Extract the values for "High-efficiency air source heat pump (ASHP), air at 0 °C" and "Ground source heat pump (GSHP), water at 0 °C" at the 35 °C column.
Step 4: Calculate the difference and print.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract COP values for ASHP and GSHP at 35°C
ashp_35c = float(df[df['Pump type and source'] == 'High efficiency ASHP, air at 0 °C']['35 °C (e.g. heated screed floor)'].iloc[0])
gshp_35c = float(df[df['Pump type and source'] == 'Ground source heat pump (GSHP), water at 0 °C']['35 °C (e.g. heated screed floor)'].iloc[0])
# Calculate difference
difference = ashp_35c - gshp_35c
print(f"Final Answer: {difference:.1f}")
```
