Step 1: The question asks to identify countries with abnormal data points compared to overall trends. Since the dataset includes multiple variables (population, area, GDP, GDP per capita), we need to assess potential outliers in a meaningful way.  
Step 2: We will focus on GDP per capita as a key indicator of economic health and look for extreme values that deviate significantly from the rest.  
Step 3: Compute the mean and standard deviation of GDP per capita and identify values that are more than 2 standard deviations away from the mean (common outlier detection method).  
Step 4: Check which countries fall into this range.  

Note: "Abnormal" here is interpreted as statistically significant outliers in GDP per capita, as it's a strong indicator of economic disparity.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract GDP per capita column and convert to numeric
gdp_per_capita = pd.to_numeric(df['gdp per capita (us)'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate mean and std for outlier detection
mean_gdp_per_capita = gdp_per_capita.mean()
std_gdp_per_capita = gdp_per_capita.std()

# Define outlier threshold (2 standard deviations)
threshold = 2 * std_gdp_per_capita
outliers = gdp_per_capita[(gdp_per_capita > mean_gdp_per_capita + threshold) | 
                          (gdp_per_capita < mean_gdp_per_capita - threshold)]

# Get the corresponding country names
outlier_countries = df[df['gdp per capita (us)'].isin(outliers)].iloc[:, 0].tolist()

print(f"Final Answer: {', '.join(outlier_countries)}")
```
