Step 1: The user wants to find the year with the highest Hurricane Severity Index (HSI), which is calculated as (number of hurricanes + number of major hurricanes) / number of tropical storms.

Step 2: Load the dataframe from the provided table.

Step 3: Convert relevant columns ('number of hurricanes', 'number of major hurricanes', 'number of tropical storms') to numeric type for calculation.

Step 4: Compute the HSI for each year.

Step 5: Identify the year with the maximum HSI.

Step 6: Output the result in the required format.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert columns to numeric
df['number of hurricanes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of hurricanes'])
df['number of major hurricanes'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of major hurricanes'])
df['number of tropical storms'] = pd.to_numeric(df['number of tropical storms'])

# Calculate HSI
df['HSI'] = (df['number of hurricanes'] + df['number of major hurricanes']) / df['number of tropical storms']

# Find the year with the highest HSI
max_hsi_year = df.loc[df['HSI'].idxmax(), 'year']

print(f"Final Answer: {max_hsi_year}")
```
