Step 1: The user wants the total number of sterilizations for individuals aged 20-29 and 30-39 during two specific time periods: 'jul 1940 - jun 1950' and 'jul 1950 - jun 1960'.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Filter the rows corresponding to the two time periods.

Step 4: Sum the values in the 'age 20 - 29' and 'age 30 - 39' columns for these filtered rows.

Step 5: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Filter rows for the specified time periods
filtered_df = df[df['date of sterilization'].isin(['jul 1940 - jun 1950', 'jul 1950 - jun 1960'])]

# Sum the values for age groups 20-29 and 30-39
total_sterilizations = filtered_df['age 20 - 29'].astype(int).sum() + filtered_df['age 30 - 39'].astype(int).sum()

print(f"Final Answer: {total_sterilizations}")
```
