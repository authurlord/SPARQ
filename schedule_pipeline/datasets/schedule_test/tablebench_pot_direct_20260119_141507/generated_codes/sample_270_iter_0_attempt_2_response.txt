Step 1: Identify the artists who made it to the 'final' by checking the 'result' column.  
Step 2: Filter the rows where 'result' is 'final'.  
Step 3: Sum the 'jury votes' from these filtered rows.  
Step 4: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where result is 'final' and sum jury votes
total_jury_votes_final = df[df['result'] == 'final']['jury votes'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_jury_votes_final}")
```
