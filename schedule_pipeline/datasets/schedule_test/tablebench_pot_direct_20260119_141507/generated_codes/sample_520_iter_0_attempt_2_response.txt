Step 1: The user wants to find the airport with the largest growth rate in passenger traffic from 2008 to 2009.  
Step 2: The growth rate is calculated as:  
   (2009 - 2008) / 2008 * 100  
Step 3: Load the dataframe and extract the '2008' and '2009' columns.  
Step 4: Compute the growth rate for each airport.  
Step 5: Identify the airport with the maximum growth rate.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate growth rate from 2008 to 2009
df['growth_rate'] = ((df['2009'] - df['2008']) / df['2008']) * 100

# Find the airport with the largest growth rate
max_growth_row = df.loc[df['growth_rate'].idxmax()]
largest_growth_airport = max_growth_row['airport']

print(f"Final Answer: {largest_growth_airport}")
```
