Step 1: The user wants the average number of episodes for seasons that meet two conditions:
- Have at least 40 episodes (numerical comparison)
- Premiered between October and December (date-based filtering)

Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'Season Premiere' to datetime to extract the month.
Step 4: Filter rows where 'Episodes' >= 40 and the month of 'Season Premiere' is between October (10) and December (12).
Step 5: Calculate the mean of 'Episodes' for the filtered rows.
Step 6: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Season Premiere' to datetime
df['Season Premiere'] = pd.to_datetime(df['Season Premiere'])
# Extract month from 'Season Premiere'
df['month'] = df['Season Premiere'].dt.month
# Filter seasons with at least 40 episodes and premiered between October and December
filtered_df = df[(df['Episodes'] >= 40) & (df['month'].between(10, 12))]
# Calculate average episodes
avg_episodes = filtered_df['Episodes'].mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {avg_episodes:.1f}")
```
