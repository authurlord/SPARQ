Step 1: The user wants the total number of students who took SAT subject tests in mathematics, specifically both Level 1 and Level 2.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'subject' is 'mathematics'.
Step 4: Extract the 'number of students' values for these rows and sum them.
Step 5: Print the total.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where subject is 'mathematics'
math_students = df[df['subject'] == 'mathematics']
# Sum the 'number of students' column
total_math_students = math_students['number of students'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_math_students}")
```
