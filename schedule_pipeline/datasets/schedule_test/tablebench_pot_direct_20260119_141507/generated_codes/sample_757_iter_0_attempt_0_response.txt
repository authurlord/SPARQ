Step 1: The user wants to forecast the viewership for season 7 based on trends from seasons 1 to 6.  
Step 2: Extract the "us viewers (millions)" column for seasons 1 to 6.  
Step 3: Analyze the trend (e.g., average, pattern, or simple linear regression) to estimate season 7 viewership.  
Step 4: Since the data is small and the trend is not clearly linear, we can calculate the average of seasons 1–6 as a simple forecast.  

Note: There is no clear indication of a trend (e.g., increasing or decreasing), and the values vary. Using the mean of seasons 1–6 provides a reasonable baseline forecast.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Extract viewership for seasons 1 to 6
seasons_1_to_6 = df[df['season'].between(1, 6)]
viewership_1_to_6 = seasons_1_to_6['us viewers (millions)']
# Forecast as the average of seasons 1 to 6
forecast_viewership = viewership_1_to_6.mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {forecast_viewership:.2f}")
```
