Step 1: The user wants to calculate the total mintage from years 2002 to 2005, then divide it equally among 5 people.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe and filter rows for years 2002 to 2005.  
Step 3: Sum the 'mintage' values for those years.  
Step 4: Divide the total by 5 to get the number of coins per person.  
Step 5: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for years 2002 to 2005
filtered_df = df[df['year'].between(2002, 2005)]
# Sum the mintage values
total_mintage = filtered_df['mintage'].sum()
# Divide by 5
coins_per_person = total_mintage / 5
print(f"Final Answer: {coins_per_person}")
```
