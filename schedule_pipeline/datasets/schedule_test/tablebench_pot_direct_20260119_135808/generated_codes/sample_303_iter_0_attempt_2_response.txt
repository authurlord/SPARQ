Step 1: The user wants to identify the competition where the athlete won 1st place in the 3000 m steeplechase event, and the venue is a city that is also the capital of the Bahamas.

Step 2: From the data, filter rows where:
- Event is '3000 m steeplechase'
- Position is '1st'

Step 3: Check the venue for this row. The venue should be a city that is the capital of the Bahamas. The capital of the Bahamas is Nassau.

Step 4: Confirm that the venue is 'Nassau, Bahamas' and return the corresponding competition.

Step 5: Output the competition name.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 1st place in 3000 m steeplechase
filtered_df = df[(df['Event'] == '3000 m steeplechase') & (df['Position'] == '1st')]
# Check if the venue is Nassau, Bahamas (capital of Bahamas)
competition = filtered_df[filtered_df['Venue'].str.contains('Nassau, Bahamas', na=False)]['Competition'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {competition}")
```
