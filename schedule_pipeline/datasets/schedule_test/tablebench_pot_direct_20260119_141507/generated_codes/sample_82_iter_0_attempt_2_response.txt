Step 1: The user is asking for the team that has exactly 13 points and a goal difference of 17.  
Step 2: Load the dataframe.  
Step 3: Filter the rows where 'points' is 13 and 'difference' is 17.  
Step 4: Extract the 'team' name from the matching row.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter team with 13 points and goal difference of 17
target_team = df[(df['points'] == 13) & (df['difference'] == 17)]['team'].values[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {target_team}")
```
