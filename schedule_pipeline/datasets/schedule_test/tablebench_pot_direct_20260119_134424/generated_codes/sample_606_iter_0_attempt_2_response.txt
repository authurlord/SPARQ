Step 1: Identify the student populations for Karolinska Institutet and Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences from the 'student population (fte, 2009)' column.
Step 2: Sum these two values to get the combined student population.
Step 3: Increase the combined population by 18% (multiply by 1.18).
Step 4: Print the result.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows for Karolinska Institutet and Swedish University of Agricultural Sciences
karolinska_population = df[df['university'] == 'karolinska institutet']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]
swedish_agricultural_population = df[df['university'] == 'swedish university of agricultural sciences']['student population ( fte , 2009)'].values[0]

# Convert to integers and calculate combined population with 18% increase
combined_population = int(karolinska_population) + int(swedish_agricultural_population)
new_combined_population = combined_population * 1.18

print(f"Final Answer: {int(new_combined_population)}")
```
