Step 1: The user wants to identify the year in which the athlete won a gold medal in a 20 km event.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter rows where the 'Event' is '20 km' and 'Notes' contains 'GR' (indicating a gold medal, as per common notation).
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from the filtered row and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for 20 km events with gold medal (GR in Notes)
gold_medal_row = df[(df['Event'] == '20 km') & (df['Notes'].str.contains('GR', na=False))]
# Extract the year
year_gold_medal = gold_medal_row['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year_gold_medal}")
```
