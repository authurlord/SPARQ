Step 1: The user wants the standard deviation of "Political Rights" scores from 1975 to 1990.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Filter the data for years between 1975 and 1990 (inclusive).
Step 4: Extract the "Political Rights" column and calculate its standard deviation.
Step 5: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter data from 1975 to 1990
filtered_df = df[(df['Year'] >= '1975') & (df['Year'] <= '1990')]
# Calculate standard deviation of 'Political Rights'
std_political_rights = filtered_df['Political Rights'].astype(int).std()
print(f"Final Answer: {std_political_rights:.2f}")
```
