The question asks whether a higher total capacity (MW) causally influences the completion schedule for power plants. However, causal relationships cannot be determined from observational data alone without controlled experiments or advanced statistical modeling. The table only provides descriptive data (capacity and completion year), so we can analyze correlation but not causation.

Step 1: Load the dataframe.
Step 2: Convert 'total capacity (mw)' to numeric for analysis.
Step 3: Convert 'completion schedule' to integer (year).
Step 4: Compute the correlation coefficient between capacity and completion year.
Step 5: Since the question is about causality, we must clarify that correlation does not imply causation.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'total capacity (mw)' to numeric
df['total capacity (mw)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['total capacity (mw)'])
# Convert 'completion schedule' to integer
df['completion schedule'] = pd.to_numeric(df['completion schedule'])

# Calculate correlation between capacity and completion year
correlation = df['total capacity (mw)'].corr(df['completion schedule'])

# Print result with explanation
print(f"Correlation between capacity and completion year: {correlation:.3f}")
print("Note: Correlation does not imply causation. No causal relationship can be concluded from this data alone.")
```

Final Answer: Cannot determine causality, correlation value is shown
