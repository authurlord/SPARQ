Step 1: Identify the team with the most titles in the period from 1968 to 1983.  
Step 2: Find the year when that team won their first title.  
Step 3: Retrieve the winning coach for that specific year.  

From the data, we can see the "winner (number of titles)" column. We will:
- Extract the winner and the year they won their first title (i.e., the first occurrence of each team).
- Count the number of titles per team.
- Identify the team with the maximum titles.
- Then find the coach in the year when that team won their first title.

Note: The "winner" column has entries like "atlanta chiefs (1)", so we extract the team name by splitting on space and taking the first part.

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract team name from 'winner (number of titles)' column
df['winner_team'] = df['winner (number of titles)'].str.extract(r'([^(\s]+)')[0]

# Group by team and count titles
title_counts = df.groupby('winner_team')['winner (number of titles)'].count()
max_titles_team = title_counts.idxmax()

# Find the first year when this team won (first occurrence)
first_win_year = df[df['winner_team'] == max_titles_team]['year'].iloc[0]

# Get the winning coach in that year
coach_in_first_win = df[df['year'] == first_win_year]['winning coach'].iloc[0]

print(f"Final Answer: {max_titles_team}, {coach_in_first_win}")
```
