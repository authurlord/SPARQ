Step 1: The user wants to find the year when the athlete achieved their personal best (PB) in the 200m event at the Asian Games.
Step 2: Filter the data for rows where the 'Event' is '200 m' and 'Competition' is 'Asian Games'.
Step 3: Among those rows, identify the one with 'Position' containing 'PB' (personal best).
Step 4: Extract the 'Year' from that row and print it.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter for Asian Games and 200m event with PB
pb_row = df[(df['Event'] == '200 m') & (df['Competition'] == 'Asian Games') & (df['Position'].str.contains('PB', na=False))]
# Extract the year
year_pb = pb_row['Year'].iloc[0]
print(f"Final Answer: {year_pb}")
```
