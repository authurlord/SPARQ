Step 1: Identify the relevant columns: 'venue', 'crowd', and 'margin'. We are looking for matches where the venue is "waverley park" and crowd size > 40,000.  
Step 2: Filter the rows based on these conditions.  
Step 3: Among the filtered rows, find the team with the highest margin (since margin is directly given in the table).  
Step 4: The "winners" column contains the team name, so we extract that from the filtered data.  

Note: Since the question asks for the team with the highest score margin under these conditions, we directly look at the 'margin' column after filtering.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows where venue is Waverley Park and crowd > 40,000
filtered_df = df[(df['venue'] == 'waverley park') & (df['crowd'].astype(int) > 40000)]
# Find the team (winners) with the highest margin
highest_margin_team = filtered_df.loc[filtered_df['margin'].idxmax(), 'winners']
print(f"Final Answer: {highest_margin_team}")
```
