Step 1: The user wants to forecast the issue price of a coin in 2013 based on the historical trend from 2002 to 2012.  
Step 2: Extract the "issue price" column and analyze its trend over years.  
Step 3: Since the data shows a steady increase in issue prices (from $39.95 to $49.95), we can assume a linear trend and extrapolate to 2013.  
Step 4: Calculate the average annual increase and apply it to 2013.  

Note: The issue prices are not perfectly linear, but they show a consistent upward pattern. We will fit a simple linear regression model or use direct arithmetic progression for simplicity due to limited data points.

Python code to compute the trend and forecast:

```python
import pandas as pd

# Load the data
df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Convert year to integer and issue price to float
df['year'] = pd.to_numeric(df['year'], errors='coerce')
df['issue price'] = pd.to_numeric(df['issue price'], errors='coerce')

# Extract the years and issue prices
years = df['year'].dropna()
prices = df['issue price'].dropna()

# Calculate the average annual increase
slope = (prices.iloc[-1] - prices.iloc[0]) / (years.iloc[-1] - years.iloc[0])
forecast_2013 = prices.iloc[-1] + slope * (2013 - years.iloc[-1])

print(f"Final Answer: {forecast_2013:.2f}")
```
