Step 1: The user wants the total number of medals (M36 + M36B1 + M36B2) for the period from May 1944 to August 1944.  
Step 2: Identify the relevant rows in the table: May 1944, June 1944, July 1944, August 1944.  
Step 3: Extract the values for M36, M36B1, and M36B2 for these months.  
Step 4: Sum the values across all three columns for these rows.  

Note: The "Post-war" and "Total" rows are outside the specified period and should be excluded.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Filter rows from May 1944 to August 1944
filtered_rows = df[(df['Month'] >= 'May 1944') & (df['Month'] <= 'August 1944')]

# Calculate the total medals (sum of M36, M36B1, M36B2)
total_medals = filtered_rows['M36'].sum() + filtered_rows['M36B1'].sum() + filtered_rows['M36B2'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {total_medals}")
```
