Step 1: The user wants the correlation coefficient between the 'area (km square) territorial waters' and 'percentage of total area (foreez)' across ecozones.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert the relevant columns to numeric type.
Step 4: Calculate the Pearson correlation coefficient using pandas.
Step 5: Print the correlation coefficient as the final answer.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert the columns to numeric
df['area (km square) territorial waters'] = pd.to_numeric(df['area (km square) territorial waters'])
df['percentage of total area (foreez)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'])

# Calculate the correlation coefficient
correlation = df['area (km square) territorial waters'].corr(df['percentage of total area (foreez)'])
print(f"Final Answer: {correlation:.4f}")
```
