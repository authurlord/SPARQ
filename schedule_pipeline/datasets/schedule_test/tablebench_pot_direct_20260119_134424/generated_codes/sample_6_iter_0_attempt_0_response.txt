Step 1: The user wants the total number of years served by all mayors.
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Convert 'Taking Office' and 'Leaving' columns to integers (handling 'Current' as a special case).
Step 4: Calculate the difference between 'Leaving' and 'Taking Office' for each mayor, summing up the years.
Step 5: For the current mayor (Michel Angers), use the year 2024 as the leaving year since the data is current.
Step 6: Print the total years served.

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'Taking Office' and 'Leaving' to integers, handling 'Current'
df['Taking Office'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Taking Office'], errors='coerce')
df['Leaving'] = df['Leaving'].replace('Current', 2024)
df['Leaving'] = pd.to_numeric(df['Leaving'], errors='coerce')

# Calculate years served
df['Years Served'] = df['Leaving'] - df['Taking Office']

# Sum total years served
total_years = df['Years Served'].sum()
print(f"Final Answer: {int(total_years)}")
```
