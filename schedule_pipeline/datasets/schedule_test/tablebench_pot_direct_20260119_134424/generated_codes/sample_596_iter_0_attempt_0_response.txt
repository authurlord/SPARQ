Step 1: The user wants the average profit of the top 5 companies (rank 1 to 5).
Step 2: Load the dataframe.
Step 3: Select the 'profit (us billion)' column and convert it to numeric, handling negative values properly.
Step 4: Extract the first 5 rows (top 5 companies).
Step 5: Calculate the mean of their profits.
Step 6: Print the result.
```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Convert 'profit (us billion)' to numeric, handling negative signs correctly
df['profit (us billion)'] = pd.to_numeric(df['profit (us billion)'], errors='coerce')
# Get the top 5 companies (first 5 rows)
top_5_profit = df['profit (us billion)'].head(5).mean()
print(f"Final Answer: {top_5_profit:.1f}")
```
