Step 1: The user wants to find the autonomous community with the highest percentage of solid waste power in its total electricity demand.  
Step 2: The relevant column is '% renewable of total electricity demand', but this is a composite value, not specific to solid waste power.  
Step 3: However, the table does not have a direct column for "percentage of solid waste power". Instead, it has a column "solid waste power" (in MW), and the "% renewable of total electricity demand" is a combined percentage.  
Step 4: Since there's no direct percentage of solid waste power, we must infer that the question may be asking for the community with the highest absolute value of solid waste power (as a proxy).  
Step 5: Alternatively, if the question truly means "percentage contribution of solid waste power", that would require computing: (solid waste power / total electricity demand) * 100 for each region and finding the maximum.  
Step 6: We will compute this percentage for each autonomous community and identify the one with the highest value.  

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')
# Calculate the percentage of solid waste power in total electricity demand
df['solid_waste_percent'] = (df['solid waste power'] / df['total electricity demand']) * 100
# Find the community with the highest value
max_percent_community = df.loc[df['solid_waste_percent'].idxmax(), 'autonomous community']
print(f"Final Answer: {max_percent_community}")
```
