Step 1: The question asks about the impact of urbanization on population growth rate between 1939 and 2008. To analyze this, we need to:
- Calculate the overall population growth rate from 1939 to 2008.
- Observe the trend in urban population percentage over the same period.
- Correlate the increase in urban population with population growth.

Step 2: Load the dataframe.

Step 3: Extract the population (in thousands) for 1939 and 2008.

Step 4: Compute the population growth rate using the formula:
    Growth Rate = (Final Population - Initial Population) / Initial Population * 100

Step 5: Observe the change in urban population percentage from 1939 to 2008.

Step 6: Interpret the relationship: if urban population increased significantly while population grew, it suggests urbanization played a role in growth.

Step 7: Present the final answer as the growth rate (since the question asks for "impact" in terms of population growth rate).

```python
import pandas as pd

df = pd.read_csv('table.csv')

# Extract population values for 1939 and 2008
initial_pop = df[df['year (january)'] == '1939']['population (000)'].values[0]
final_pop = df[df['year (january)'] == '2008']['population (000)'].values[0]

# Convert to numeric
initial_pop = int(initial_pop)
final_pop = int(final_pop)

# Calculate growth rate
growth_rate = ((final_pop - initial_pop) / initial_pop) * 100

print(f"Final Answer: {growth_rate:.1f}")
```
